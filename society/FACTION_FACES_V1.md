# Faction Faces v1

Status: PROVISIONAL CHARACTER/POLITICS DESIGN — NAMES NOT FROZEN
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose: give each major political philosophy recurring people who can act, fail, compromise, and live outside debate scenes.

These are **not eight party leaders delivering eight speeches**.
Most are practitioners/organizers/citizens who become recognizable faces of an issue.

---

# FF1 — Civic Continuity Face

Faction: F1 Civic Continuity
Working demographics: woman, early 40s
Habitat: H1 with recurring cross-habitat work
Profession: **inter-habitat identity/benefits standards architect** in a civil-service technical office

## Independent goal
Replace incompatible habitat benefit/identity systems so families do not lose rights when they move.

## Strength
Understands how small legal-schema differences produce real exclusions.

## Flaw
Believes standardization is more neutral than it really is.

## Personal stake
A restored relative or nonstandard household once lost benefits because two habitats recognized continuity differently.

## Pre-reveal action
Pushes the standardization rule that accidentally exposes one of the obsolete provenance dependencies.

## Post-reveal position
Strongly opposes biological civic rank but initially wants to centralize provenance handling too quickly.

## Required loss
Must see one supposedly universal standard recreate a local harm.

# FF2 — Mortality Covenant Face

Faction: F2 Mortality Covenant, secular dignity wing
Working demographics: man, late 50s
Habitat: H2 / H7 network
Profession: **hospice and end-of-life care coordinator**

## Independent goal
Protect patients from default state capture becoming socially compulsory.

## Strength
Knows what families actually do around death, not just doctrine.

## Flaw
Can treat fear of restoration as moral clarity and underestimate people who sincerely want continuity capture.

## Personal stake
A spouse/close friend chose restoration; their relationship with the restored claimant is complicated rather than simply rejected.

## Post-reveal position
BH-1's harder-to-restore biology is a vulnerability, not a throne.

## Required compromise
Supports public funding for some restoration access despite philosophical discomfort because inequality itself is coercive.

# FF3 — Restoration Rights Face

Faction: F3 Restoration Rights League
Working demographics: woman, early-to-mid 30s
Habitat: H3
Profession: **industrial electrical/controls technician**
Status: restored continuity claimant after a prior industrial accident

## Independent goal
Keep professional certification and family obligations recognized after restoration.

## Strength
Practical, technically competent, unwilling to live as a philosophical example.

## Flaw
Can overstate continuity because admitting discontinuity feels politically dangerous.

## Personal stake
One family member recognizes her as fully continuous; another keeps emotional distance.

## Pre-reveal action
Becomes involved in a labor/certification dispute, not initially as activist leader.

## Post-reveal position
Uses majority artificial-origin truth to attack biological privilege, but later resists rhetoric that treats every restored claimant as identical to developmental artificial-origin people.

# FF4 — Voyage Mandate Functionalist Face

Faction: F4 Voyage Mandate, functionalist wing
Working demographics: man, late 40s
Habitat/work: Forward Navigation Complex
Profession: **destination observation / arrival-operations planner**

## Independent goal
Prevent current political conflict from destroying the decades-long sequence needed for safe arrival.

## Strength
Understands that settlement cannot be improvised in the last year.

## Flaw
Can turn mission sequencing into moral authority.

## Personal stake
His adult child is aligned with Shipborn Future and may prefer orbital life.

## Post-reveal position
Rejects the idea that BH-1 owns the mission, but insists the living cannot simply ignore promises encoded in survival infrastructure.

## Required change
Accepts that the mission can be rewritten without being abandoned.

---

# FF4B — Voyage Mandate Literalist Pressure Face

Faction: F4 literalist wing
Working demographics: woman, 60s
Profession: **charter historian / retired mission magistrate**

Use sparingly.

## Function
Provides the strongest good-faith case that old biological wording has legal force.

## Safeguard
Do not make her a fanatic or secret villain.

She may later accept amendment but insist it occur through legitimate process rather than semantic reinterpretation.

# FF5 — Shipborn Future Face

Faction: F5 Shipborn Future, reformist/permanence coalition
Working demographics: nonbinary or woman/man depending later cast balance, early 30s
Habitat: H3/H5
Profession: **fabrication labor organizer / apprenticeship coordinator**

## Independent goal
Stop current maintenance workers from sacrificing entire careers/lives to reserves they will never personally use.

## Strength
Can translate abstract mission budgets into who actually works overtime or loses housing/health.

## Flaw
Underestimates long-tail maintenance risk of keeping all habitats indefinitely.

## Personal stake
Their parent/grandparent spent decades in high-risk maintenance tied to mission reserve preservation.

## Post-reveal position
Sees artificial-origin history as proof shipborn civilization has independent legitimacy.

## Required change
Accepts some irreversible settlement conversion after seeing preservation costs transferred to younger workers elsewhere.

# FF6 — Stewardship Account Face

Faction: F6 Stewardship Account, egalitarian steward wing
Working demographics: woman, mid-40s
Habitat: H2/H3 interface
Profession: **material/nutrient systems auditor**

## Independent goal
Make hidden maintenance/resource debt public before political promises exceed inventory.

## Strength
Sees closed-loop material flows across food, fabrication, water, shielding, and waste.

## Flaw
Treats difficult-to-quantify social continuity as suspicious because it cannot be entered cleanly into the account.

## Personal stake
Her own home district once lost a repair allocation because politically powerful H1 won a discretionary project.

## Post-reveal position
Opposes biological civic rank but insists BH-1's different medical costs be honestly budgeted.

## Required mistake
Publishes a technically correct optimization that would fragment a caregiving/community network because her model omitted informal labor.

# FF7 — Kinship Commons Face

Faction: F7 Kinship Commons
Working demographics: man, late 30s/early 40s
Habitat: H4
Profession: **school/community care-network coordinator**

Household:
nontraditional multi-adult caregiving household or co-parenting arrangement.

## Independent goal
Get care relationships recognized when housing/medical systems only see legal household units.

## Strength
Knows how invisible care labor prevents institutional failure.

## Flaw
Can privilege existing relationships even when an individual wants to leave them.

## Personal stake
A young adult in his household wants less communal obligation than he thinks is healthy.

## Post-reveal position
Fiercely opposes retroactive invalidation of seeded parenthood.

## Required change
Learns relational obligation can become coercion when “family continuity” overrides individual autonomy.

# FF8 — Provenance Circle Face

Faction: F8 Provenance Circle, public historian/journalist wing
Working demographics: woman, mid-30s
Habitat: H6
Profession: **forensic archivist / investigative public-record journalist**

## Independent goal
Build a distributed authenticated archive that cannot be quietly simplified by institutions again.

## Strength
Excellent at proving that apparently disconnected records share provenance.

## Flaw
Initially overvalues disclosure as inherently corrective.

## Personal stake
A family history gap caused a previous benefits/identity dispute; she believes lost evidence is itself violence.

## Major autonomous action
Releases genuine Continuity Settlement records in Act III.

Positive result:
Breaks false history.

Negative result:
Partial data is used to target/essentialize living families.

## Required responsibility
She does not get to say “misuse is not my fault” and move on.
She changes archive-access philosophy without abandoning truth preservation.

Profession: independent genealogy/data analyst / media personality

## Function

# 2. Cross-Faction Relationships

These faces must know/work with each other before the main reveal where plausible.

Suggested links:

- FF1 and FF7 have argued for years over standardized household schemas.
- FF2 and FF3 know each other through restoration policy and disagree respectfully.
- FF4 and FF6 cooperate on arrival budgets but clash over reserve assumptions.
- FF5 and FF6 share worker/resource data but disagree about long-term mission duty.
- FF8 has used FF1's standardization datasets and FF6's public material accounts in prior reporting.

The political world therefore predates protagonist involvement.

# 3. Core-Cast Faction Sympathies

Do not make every core character formal faction members.

Working sympathies:

- protagonist: Civic Continuity + Kinship instincts, but professionally wary of faction labels
- mother: Kinship Commons locally; some Shipborn Future sympathy
- sister: Shipborn Future reformist + destination/science interest; not permanence wing
- colleague: Stewardship Account, strongly but not formally
- former partner: Mortality Covenant consent wing + Restoration Rights access wing simultaneously
- mentor: Civic Continuity + Voyage Mandate functionalist
- rival: Shipborn Future / local-autonomy wing + privacy sympathies
- BH-1: politically mixed; locally protectionist, privacy-first, skeptical of being claimed by any faction

This generates cross-cutting loyalties.
