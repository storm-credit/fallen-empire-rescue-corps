# 2. Required Revision A — ARC 09 / ARC 10

Current:
- ARC 09 primary E4 personal/family
- ARC 10 primary E4 intimate relationship

Problem:
Two consecutive emotionally inward relationship-primary Arcs occur after an 11-episode restoration block.

Reader risk around EP044–055:
The story may feel like it stops external motion to process protagonist identity/romance.

## Revision

Make ARC 09 primary **E9 Information Cascade / E1 professional consequence**, with protagonist birth-history inquiry as secondary.

Preferred premise:
A public rumor/misinterpretation of the baseline category causes a concrete transition/workplace exclusion or false “human candidate” targeting case.

While solving it, the protagonist checks their own lawful developmental history and confirms standard seeded architecture.

Then ARC 10 can remain E4 intimate relationship.

Result:
external/public → internal/intimate.

**REQUIRED.**
