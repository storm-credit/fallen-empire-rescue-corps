# Sol Communication History v2

Status: **PROVISIONAL CANON CANDIDATE — C2 CLOSURE**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》
Supersedes earlier loose `Sol stopped talking` explanations.

---

# 1. Physical Rule

Ross 128 is only ~11 light-years from Sol.

Therefore electromagnetic communication is physically possible.

Canon must **not** claim:

> `The ship is too far away for radio/light to reach.`

Correct rule:

> Useful interstellar communication requires a deliberately maintained transmitter/receiver system, pointing, protocol, bandwidth, and authentication. Ordinary Solar-System information does not arrive as a free broadband internet feed.

---

# 2. Why Ordinary Broadcast Leakage Is Not the Mission Network

A civilization can radiate many signals, but useful high-volume ship communication at interstellar distance favors:

- high-gain directional radio
- narrow optical/laser beams
- known receiver ephemerides
- precise pointing
- powerful transmit infrastructure
- error correction
- agreed protocols

The ship therefore depends on **directed communication services**, not accidental television/radio leakage.

Reader-facing version:

> Sol could talk to the ship if somebody kept aiming a very expensive interstellar transmitter at it.

---

# 3. Voyage Communication Eras

Use four broad eras.

---

## ERA I — Mission Service / Voyage Years 0–~170

The launch consortium maintains a dedicated authenticated communication service.

Functions:
- navigation updates
- engineering changes
- medical/scientific updates
- political/mission messages
- selected cultural archives

Bandwidth improves/varies with infrastructure but remains a curated mission channel, not a live mirror of Sol's entire information sphere.

At this time the ship itself still broadly knows the old artificial-origin / human-origin history.

No mystery contradiction exists yet.

---

## ERA II — Authority Fragmentation / ~170–230

The original launch consortium changes through:
- political succession
- jurisdiction splits
- infrastructure ownership disputes
- protocol changes

Sol is **not destroyed**.

The problem is institutional continuity.

Multiple successor bodies claim some relationship to the expedition.

The ship receives:
- valid messages
- incompatible legal claims
- technical updates
- competing political instructions

The authenticated chain from the original mission authority becomes increasingly difficult to extend.

---

## ERA III — End of Continuous Mission Service / ~230–300

The dedicated always-maintained mission beam service ends.

Reasons combine:

1. the original institution no longer exists in one legal form
2. transmitter arrays change ownership/purpose
3. maintaining a century-scale narrowbeam service to one slow colony ship is politically expensive
4. successor societies adopt different communication standards and priorities
5. no single Sol authority has uncontested power to speak for all descendants of the launch civilization

Interstellar silence is **not total**.

Continuous authenticated mission service is what ends.

---

## ERA IV — Fragmentary Sol Messages / ~300 to Present

The ship intermittently receives:
- public interstellar beacons
- scientific/cultural packets
- directed messages from particular Solar polities/organizations
- old relay/archive retransmissions

Problems:
- uneven bandwidth
- long gaps
- sender-specific authentication
- incompatible formats
- political provenance
- ~years of light-time delay

Some messages are unquestionably from real Solar descendants.

They are **information**, not automatically legitimate commands.

---

# 4. Why the Continuity Settlement Could Still Lose the Old Crosswalk

Preferred local Continuity Settlement timing:
~350 years before EP001, after the continuous original mission service has already ended.

The ship deliberately dismantles/partitions origin-caste cross-indexes.

Later Sol fragments do not simply restore them because:

- they do not contain the ship's person-by-person provenance registry
- terminology in Sol has also evolved
- many successor societies no longer use the launch mission's exact ontology
- the key mapping between current ship medical seed versions and the old mission civic category is a **ship/mission technical genealogy**, not a headline in ordinary cultural packets

Thus external information may contain old AI-history fragments without reconstructing:

> `the people aboard now are the direct artificial-origin lineage under this exact old mission schema.`

---

# 5. Sol Did Not Preserve One Stable Human/AI Vocabulary Either

Sol civilization also changed over centuries.

By later message eras, Solar societies include multiple descendants of:
- biological baseline humans
- seeded developmental persons
- synthetic/engineered embodiments
- possibly digital or other personhood forms

Do not overdesign them into a second novel.

Important consequence:

Later Sol messages may use broad terms like:
- person
- citizen
- lineage-specific medical terms

rather than the ship's archaic `human / artificial-origin` mission distinction.

Therefore they do not arrive as a neat answer key.

---

# 6. The Last Authenticated Mission-Authority Chain

ARC 27's `Last Verified Voice From Home` should distinguish:

### Last message from Sol
Not necessarily ancient.

### Last message carrying an unbroken legal authentication chain from the original expedition authority
**much older**.

Preferred timing:
roughly **350–400 years before EP001 reception-history terms**, exact date open.

Its importance:
- establishes when external mission authority ceased to be continuously legitimate
- explains why current descendants eventually had to govern themselves

It need not contain a secret final order.

---

# 7. Why Haren Cannot Just Ask Sol in EP010

Even if the ship transmits immediately:

1. message takes ~11 years to reach Sol near final destination
2. reply takes another ~11 years
3. the whole novel spans ~6 years

So any question sent during EP001 cannot receive a reply before the series ending.

This is a **22-year round-trip** near Ross 128.

Therefore external communication cannot solve the current mystery or arrival constitution in time.

---

# 8. What About Messages Already in Transit?

Possible and useful.

The ship receives scheduled/intermittent Sol packets during the series that were transmitted years earlier.

They can contain:
- science
- culture
- political claims
- greetings
- historical materials

But no authorial convenience packet arrives exactly when needed with the definitive answer.

The packet content was fixed ~11 years before reception.

This is a strong temporal constraint.

---

# 9. Why Later Sol Historical Packets Do Not Reveal Nadia

They cannot.

Nadia's identity is a ship-local current medical fact.

At best, Sol history can clarify:
- artificial-person technical ancestry
- broader human/personhood history

It cannot know the ship's current private individual-level medical aggregate unless the ship transmitted it years earlier—which it did not.

---

# 10. Why Sol Does Not Own the Destination

Even if a successor polity claims:

> `We are the legal successor to the launch authority.`

that claim does not automatically bind 300,000 shipborn citizens after centuries of self-government.

The final conflict is therefore not:

> `Can they physically contact Earth?`

It is:

> `Which external claims, if any, retain legitimacy over people whose society has lived independently for centuries?`

The final compact chooses local living legitimacy while preserving informational ties.

---

# 11. Communication Technology at EP001

The ship has:
- large optical/radio receiving arrays
- precise astrometry
- archived protocol translators
- authentication specialists
- ability to transmit back toward Sol

It does **not** have:
- FTL
- entanglement messaging
- live conversation with Sol

Communications staff know the difference between:
- source authenticity
- institutional authority
- message age

This distinction becomes narratively important in Act V.

---

# 12. Sol Status at Series End

Preferred high-level answer only:

> Sol civilization still exists, but no longer as one socially/biologically/politically uniform “human homeworld authority.”

The ship's descendants are not the last people in the universe.

They are one branch among many.

This prevents Nadia from becoming “the last human in existence.”

---

# 13. Reader-Facing Exposition Limit

Do not explain 600 years of interstellar communications in one chapter.

Reveal through:
- old authenticated message headers
- communication service history
- conflicting later packets
- legal arguments over sender authority
- the simple 22-year round-trip constraint

ARC 27/28 carries most of this payoff.
