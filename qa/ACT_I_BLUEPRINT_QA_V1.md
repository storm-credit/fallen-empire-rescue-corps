# Executive Verdict

**PASS WITH TWO MANDATORY REVISIONS + THREE SMALL STRENGTHENING EDITS.**

The Act succeeds at the most important commercial and structural goals:

- EP001 has competence pleasure and lived-world texture.
- `count = 1` is paid by EP006.
- Nadia appears ordinarily before significance.
- mystery intensity falls after the title payoff.
- arrival pressure becomes material by EP016.
- H8 contains internal disagreement.
- no combat engine is needed.
- Haren makes consequential choices rather than only investigating.

However:

1. EP003–006 risk four consecutive episodes with too-similar `cross-system evidence gathering` texture.
2. EP011–015 use a child-development case that should be reserved for Act II's stronger `A Child Is Not Installed` engine and also repeats the hidden-care-network pleasure of EP001 too soon.

Both can be fixed without changing Act structure, title timing, or episode count.

# 2. EP001–006 Title-Promise Audit — PASS / TEXTURE REVISION REQUIRED

- EP001: CL-010A legacy dependency
- EP003: CL-010B one live service user
- EP004: CL-010C old class

Timing: **STRONG PASS**.

### Problem
EP003, EP004, EP005, EP006 are all currently dominated by:

`trace system → interpret category → cross-check → next piece`.

Even though each has operational stakes, the reader experience may still feel like four clue episodes.

### Mandatory correction A
Make **EP004 primarily a physical compatibility/transition test**, not archive interpretation.

Recommended structure:

- team tries a modern replacement interface/component to eliminate the legacy dependency,
- it fails a simulated/live-safe compatibility test because the rare route lacks the standard cognition-seed interface assumptions,
- this proves the distinction is physically/medically current,
- only then does old terminology become relevant.

EP004 primary engine should become:
**E5 System Constraint**, secondary E6.

Reward becomes:
`RWD-COMP + RWD-FACT + RWD-WONDER/physical system`.

This breaks the clue texture:

EP003 fact → EP004 physical test failure → EP005 historical mapping → EP006 confirmation/action.

# 4. EP011–015 Event-Diversity Audit — REVISION REQUIRED

## Current problem
The child-development relocation case is good by itself but strategically mistimed.

It creates three risks:

### Risk 1 — Pre-spends Act II
Act II has a dedicated foundational payoff:

> pregnancy, child development, cognition seed, and `a child is not installed`.

Using a full child-development/care-network case in Act I reduces novelty and emotional force of Act II.

### Risk 2 — Repeats EP001 pleasure
EP001 already teaches:

> official household/address data misses care/work/service networks.

EP011–014 currently teach a very similar planning lesson with a child/care network.

### Risk 3 — Theme clustering
TH-001/TH-005/TH-002 begin clustering too early, while `record/status/civic classification` is more directly connected to the title reveal.

## Mandatory correction B
Replace EP011–015 with an **adult certification/status case** caused by legacy origin taxonomy.

Recommended event family:

A skilled seeded-origin fabrication/maintenance worker is assigned to a transition/arrival-adjacent role, but a legacy safety/certification layer marks the worker as incompatible/not recognized because the old system expects the baseline-human class.

Important:
The worker is not medically abnormal and is not the sole human.

### EP011
Old certification rejects a demonstrably competent modern worker.

### EP012
Modern law/standards say provenance should not determine civil/professional standing, but the legacy safety system cannot simply be ignored.

Possible introduction:
**Selma Noa** / Civic Continuity standards role, without faction lecture.

### EP013
A physical competence/safety validation demonstrates the worker can perform the role under modern standards.

### EP014
Haren/Tessa/Selma choose a temporary competence-based recertification route, but it costs testing time/resources and creates a precedent question.

### EP015
Case closes:
- modern society legally treats seeded citizens as ordinary people,
- old infrastructure contains a different taxonomy,
- the answer is **not** `seeded people are fake`,
- next question becomes why the distinction disappeared and what cognition seed historically meant.

This preserves Act II for the full birth/child-development reveal.

Primary themes after revision:
- TH-003 Record vs Person
- TH-013 Difference vs Hierarchy
- TH-015 Expertise vs Self-Determination / professional standing

Mystery:
M-020/M-030, not M-040-heavy.
