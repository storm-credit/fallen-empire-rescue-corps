# M1 — Seed H5/H2 Shared Fabrication/Thermal Window Before Refusal

## Binding placement: EP160 and EP168

The working insertion schedule must explicitly show **before EP179** that two preferred early projects share one constrained fabrication/thermal window:

Early fabrication of a dedicated ecological-transfer shell/support frame for H2-derived settlement ecology.

Why coupled:
- both require the same class of large fabrication cradle time,
- both require overlapping radiator/thermal commissioning capacity,
- both draw from a limited early arrival transfer-frame production sequence.

## EP160 required note

> H5 and H2 can both receive their preferred early packages only if H5 begins within the currently reserved conversion window.

## EP168 required note
When 3/5 + surface becomes the working architecture, the convention is shown the explicit fallback:

If H5 slips beyond the bounded window:
- H2 loses its preferred early dedicated ecological shell,

This is known before H5's consent case.

## Narrative consequence
EP182 is therefore not punishment invented after H5 says no.

# M2 — Physical Standardized-Stack Test

## Binding placement: EP162

EP162 must include a **real pilot-scale H2-derived closed-ecology settlement module** operating under the common standardized settlement-stack assumptions.

This is not merely simulation/model review.

### Pilot purpose
Test whether the standard module can replace the deeper H2-derived ecology package while preserving acceptable resilience for Ross 128 b's dry settlement conditions.

### Physical result
Short-run baseline metrics can appear acceptable, but controlled disturbance/recovery testing reveals:
- reduced microbial/functional redundancy,
- slower recovery from a simulated water/nutrient interruption,
- greater sensitivity to one component failure,
- lower resilience than the heavier H2-derived architecture.

No dangerous habitat-scale failure occurs.

### Decision
Yuna/H2 ecology team rejects the standard package for this function based on measured pilot behavior.

### Cost
The specialized variant requires more mass, fabrication, and commissioning time.

### Distinction from Yuna Act-II failure
Act II:
- Yuna herself scales a real ecology experiment too early and pays the consequence.

Act V EP162:
- her matured team deliberately runs a bounded comparative pilot before committing,
- the pilot does exactly what the new cautious methodology is supposed to do: expose a hidden weakness safely.

This is a **payoff of growth**, not a repeat mistake.
