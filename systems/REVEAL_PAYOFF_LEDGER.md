## 1. Reveal Rule

Every major factual reveal needs:

- at least 3 independent clue families
- at least 1 plausible false interpretation
- an informational payoff
- an emotional/social consequence payoff

Clue families should not all be documents.

Preferred diversity:

- medical/body
- legal/archival
- infrastructure/system
- family/social practice
- behavioral/relationship

# RV-010 — There is exactly one active unseeded developmental profile

Linked mystery: `M-010`

### CL-010A — Compatibility Allocation Anomaly

A transition/resource system contains a compatibility class with population count `1`.

Surface interpretation:
Rare disability / obsolete equipment user / protected medical exception.

### CL-010B — Medical Supply Exception

A tiny, oddly persistent allocation exists for a treatment incompatible with standard seeded neurobiology.

Surface interpretation:
Legacy disease support.

### CL-010C — Old Infrastructure Warning

An obsolete system distinguishes standard seeded neural-interface users from an autonomous-development class.

Surface interpretation:
Old hardware generation.

### False interpretation

The count refers to one surviving user of obsolete implants, not one developmental lineage.

### RV-010 payoff

Cross-domain evidence establishes that the count is a **living-person developmental classification**, not equipment count.

### Consequence

The protagonist now has a concrete question with privacy/legal stakes and cannot simply query the identity.

# RV-020 — Modern citizens’ cognition is artificial-origin

Linked mysteries: `M-020`, `M-030`, `M-040`

### CL-020A — Public Prenatal Seed

Everyone knows standard cognition-seed intervention exists.

Early meaning:
Normal human developmental medicine.

Later meaning:
The most ordinary fact was the biggest clue.

### CL-020B — Transition-Era Technical Language

An old technical source describes a generative cognition architecture using terminology historically associated with artificial minds.

Surface interpretation:
Metaphor / obsolete taxonomy.

### CL-020C — Legal Threshold Case

A transition-era case debates whether a seeded child’s cognition is “derived” from parental human continuity or constitutes a new constructed lineage.

Surface interpretation:
Old prejudice using offensive technical language.

### CL-020D — Medical Development Comparison

Baseline/autonomous developmental neural organization differs systematically from seeded architecture.

Surface interpretation:
Rare pathology vs standard health.

### False interpretation

Old society merely *called* heavily modified humans AI as a political insult.

### RV-020 payoff

Evidence establishes that the cognition seed historically crossed from assistance to the generative foundation of person-level cognition.

### Emotional payoff

The protagonist and loved ones must confront that nothing about their remembered lives changed at the moment the label changed.

### Social payoff

Public definitions of human, restoration, family, and mission begin destabilizing.

# 3. Clue QA

Before blueprint freeze, every RV item must be checked for:

- clue appears before reveal
- clue has legitimate scene purpose besides being clue
- alternative interpretation is fair
- no clue depends on narrator deception
- clue is memorable enough to recontextualize but not highlighted with neon
- reveal resolves more contradictions than it creates
- emotional/social payoff follows within bounded time

No series-wide object is currently required.

- a transition permit
- archive provenance key

- charter copy

but the story must not reduce to chasing one master file containing the truth.
