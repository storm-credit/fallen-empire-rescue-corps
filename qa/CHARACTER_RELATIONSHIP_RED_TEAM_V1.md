# Executive Verdict

Core cast is strong enough to support 250 episodes.

However, four characters still risk functioning too neatly as thematic/professional counterweights:

- Yuna — needs an ecology mistake independent of Haren's old falsification
- Tessa — needs a positive independent ambition and relationship outside Haren
- Elin — needs a concrete present-tense professional arc plus a chosen relationship endpoint
- Kian — needs a cost caused by his own successful autonomy politics

Toma is close to passing but requires one autonomous information-control choice with a real cost.

Bram is supporting-tier and already sufficient if his present institution remains active.

Nadia is strong.

# 3. Yuna Savin

## Current strengths
- adult autonomy conflict with Haren
- settlement ecology career
- owns correction/disclosure of falsified record
- final future choice independent of Haren

## Gap
If all her major conflict exists because Haren acted on her, she remains reactive.

### Required independent mistake
Yuna needs a professional/ecological decision that is **hers**.

Preferred:
During an H2 / settlement-climate pilot, Yuna advocates scaling a closed ecological/water-release experiment after short-term data looks stable.

She is not reckless.
The model genuinely supports the trial.

But she underweights a slow microbial/atmospheric feedback and pushes the test beyond a conservative monitoring window.

Result:
- contained testbed instability
- crop/microbial stock loss or months of reset work
- no deaths
- no planet-scale catastrophe

She must:
- report it herself
- accept project consequences
- revise her own view of autonomy/irreversibility

The lesson is not “Haren should have protected her.”

It is:
> choosing for yourself also means owning downstream effects.

### Verdict
**PASS AFTER REQUIRED ADDITION.**

This must be partly **Tessa's model**, not only Haren's plan.

She publicly owns the failure.

### Final agency
One Act VII conversion segment succeeds under Tessa/local-team leadership while Haren is elsewhere.

**PASS AFTER BINDING THESE ELEMENTS.**

# 5. Elin Vess

## Current strengths
- former intimate relationship
- neural rehab/continuity profession
- consent-oriented voice
- independent medicine domain

## Risk
Could become:
- ethical doctor who explains consent
- patient ex who waits for Haren's growth

### Required independent professional arc
Preferred:
Elin leads/helps lead the arrival-era **continuity-medicine transition protocol** for people moving among:
- rotating habitats
- altered gravity
- surface 1.1g
- incomplete restoration/capture access

Her practical problem:
How much continuity preparation should medicine recommend without making state capture socially compulsory?

### Required mistake/blind spot
Elin overcorrects against coercion.

In one case, she frames refusal so strongly around individual rights that the care team/family is not forced to articulate concrete burdens until late.

No violation of patient's refusal right occurs.

Her growth:
> Respecting a choice does not mean refusing to discuss who carries its cost.

### Relationship endpoint requirement
Do not leave Haren/Elin in indefinite “maybe someday” stasis.

Recommended working endpoint:

**They deliberately begin a new relationship during/after the final transition, but do not recreate the old one or sacrifice careers to co-locate perfectly.**

Conditions:
- no crisis confession solves everything
- Elin explicitly states what she wants
- Haren asks instead of planning for her
- geography remains real

This provides emotional payoff without making romance the theorem proof.

### Verdict
**PASS AFTER ENDPOINT / PROFESSIONAL ARC PROMOTION.**

# 6. Toma Venn

## Current strengths
- institution/common-floor logic
- mentor without secret villain role
- can fairly articulate opposition

## Risk
Could become benevolent wise administrator whose only flaw is “too cautious.”

### Required autonomous mistake
During ACT III, Toma delays release of one authenticated historical crosswalk / mission key because:
- a live medical/service transition is underway
- he fears immediate misuse/re-identification

His reason is good.

But the delay:
- allows rumors/false data to dominate for several days/weeks
- damages trust when the delay becomes known
- strengthens critics who accuse institutions of continuing paternalistic information control

He is not secretly protecting the truth forever.

### Required consequence
He loses some unilateral information-release authority / accepts external oversight.

### Final agency
He supports a common rights/safety floor even when it reduces H1 institutional control.

### Verdict
**PASS AFTER CONSEQUENCE LOCK.**

# 7. Kian Rel

## Current strengths
- legitimate rival
- consent/local autonomy
- defeats Haren's process at least once

## Risk
If his wins always prove Haren wrong without cost, he becomes author's perfect corrective.

### Required self-caused cost
ARC 29 local-refusal victory delays a conversion sequence.

The delay is legitimate and prevents an unjust process.

But it also consumes:
- fabrication window
- thermal/transport margin
- another habitat's preferred option

Kian must publicly acknowledge that local autonomy cannot mean invisible externalization.

### Final position
Still defends strong local authority.

He does not become Haren's lieutenant.

### Verdict
**PASS AFTER COST LOCK.**
