# 0. Mandatory Opening Rule

The title premise is not a mystery to be hoarded.

By **EP006**, the reader and Haren should have reliable confirmation that:

> current shipwide baseline-development human population = **1**.

The person's identity remains private/unknown.

From EP007 onward, the main question becomes:

> Then what are the other 299,999 people—including Haren?

# 3. EP003 — A Service for One Person

A second independent operational/medical capacity system confirms:

- the rare route is current,
- it requires real present-day support capacity,
- the anonymous shipwide service count is **1**.

At this point the reader knows:

> there is one living person who needs this route.

But neither reader nor Haren yet has enough evidence to equate the route with the title's historical category.

This is a real factual payoff, not another locked door.

# 5. EP005 — Human-Origin Is Not a Metaphor

The team obtains an independent cross-system mapping sufficient to establish that the old route refers to development **without the cognition-seed architecture that became standard later**.

Haren does not yet receive an ancient philosophy lecture.

- this is not merely a rare disease,
- current medicine still preserves the route for compatibility/reversibility reasons,
- old systems classified it under a human-origin/baseline category.

Hook:
If the live anonymous count is 1, the old category's live count may also be 1.

# 6. EP006 — The Number Is One

Three independent evidence families now agree.

### Hard factual payoff

> Current baseline-development human population aboard the ship: **1**.

### What Haren does NOT learn
- identity,
- habitat,
- age,
- gender,
- reason the person exists,
- full artificial-origin history of everyone else.

### Immediate consequence
The personal detail layer remains protected because modern systems retain medical privacy while aggregate capacity data is visible for safety/resource planning.

### Required emotional beat
Haren's first useful reaction is not a philosophical monologue.

He has to finish or redesign the live transition under the knowledge that the classification system supporting the project contains a category whose meaning nobody around him fully understands.

### New hook

> If one person belongs to the old baseline-human route, what developmental lineage describes Haren and everyone he knows?

This is the actual long-form question.
