# EP033 — A Person Returns to an Address That Moved

**Immediate Goal**
Haren must close an H3 housing/work transition whose registry still treats Daria Tov as deceased even though a restored continuity claimant has just completed primary rehabilitation.

**Primary Engine**
E2 Continuity Claim.

**Secondary**
E1 Habitat Transition / E4 Relationship.

**Dominant Experience**
social SF + personal dislocation.

**External Event**
During Daria's months-long restoration process:
- her old household entered inheritance/occupancy transition,
- her work team reassigned her operational role,
- some belongings were distributed,
- the district itself began a scheduled move.

Now Daria is alive and legally recognized as a provisional RC, but there is no simple `undo death` button for the material/social changes already made.

**Decision / Action**
Haren freezes only the claims directly affected by her current rights; he does not reverse all completed transactions.

**Result**
Daria receives immediate personal housing/access protection while broader continuity claims are reviewed.

**Cost**

# EP035 — The Seed Could Not Bring Her Back

**Immediate Goal**
Haren/Tessa must understand which restoration systems have to remain active through the H3 transition.

**Primary Engine**
E5 Material/System Constraint.

**Secondary**
E2.

**External Event**
A physical review of the continuity clinic distinguishes three systems:

1. developmental cognition seed — architecture for a new child's development,
2. adult continuity capture — recorded mature cognitive state/model,

Daria's adult self came from authenticated capture data integrated into a newly conditioned embodied substrate; the developmental seed alone contained none of her memories/personality.

# II-B Rollup

## Technical payoff
- developmental seed != adult backup
- adult continuity capture is separate
- capture has real time gap and incomplete lived continuity
- restoration is embodied, slow, resource-intensive, rehabilitative
- multiple reconstruction possible in principle, creating separate persons after divergence
- no objective metaphysical same-person answer

## Emotional payoff
- family recognition and legal recognition do not move in lockstep
- death created real absence even when a claimant returns
- Daria has present agency and future choices

## Material payoff
- restoration/rehab competes for heat, staff, conditioning throughput, and transition timing
- no magic unlimited resurrection infrastructure

## Character payoff
- Elin enters through real work and makes a consequential consent decision
- Haren/Elin professional trust begins without romance solving the arc
- Tessa gets material constraint authority
- Daria becomes recurring Restoration Rights face through lived competence

## Mystery
M-050 core paid.
No major progress on historical AI lineage; that remains later.

## Handoff
EP044–055 moves from `what is death/return?` to:
- rumor/candidate-hunting,
- Haren's own standard seed history,
- the limits of using loved ones as proof,
- present restoration/arrival resource tension,
- the question of why historical origin categories disappeared.
