# 4. World / Canon Deep Design

## Human / AI ontology

Preferred current model:

- all ordinary citizens live in real human-derived organic bodies
- pregnancy, infancy, childhood, illness, aging, work, love, and death are real
- most citizens are **seeded-development persons**
- artificial origin is the causal origin of cognitive development, not an android body or server avatar
- cognition seed does not contain an adult personality
- personality emerges through embodied development and lived experience
- no hidden network control / kill switch / personality reset

Primary sources:
- `canon/COGNITION_SEED_SYSTEM_V1.md`
- `canon/ONTOLOGY_TERMINOLOGY_V1.md`
- `canon/AUTOMATION_PERSONHOOD_BOUNDARY_V1.md`

## Reproduction / family

Completed structural model:

- real conception/gestation/birth
- reproductive medicine and cognition-seed developmental support
- kinship law beyond genes alone
- demographic-window governance
- no trait-ranked eugenic birth rights
- rare autonomous/non-seeded developmental route
- present count of baseline-development persons statistically near one

Primary source:
`canon/REPRODUCTIVE_KINSHIP_SYSTEM_V1.md`

## Death / restoration

Completed structural model:

- developmental seed ≠ adult backup
- continuity capture is optional, costly, partial, consent-dependent
- restoration creates a conscious continuity claimant, not automatic metaphysical certainty
- murder/death remain consequential
- concurrent copies possible in principle but legally/ethically constrained
- right to refuse capture/restoration remains central

Primary source:
`canon/DEATH_RESTORATION_SYSTEM_V1.md`

## Historical truth

Preferred current answer:

- launch population was baseline human
- AI did not massacre humanity
- developmental artificial cognition began as medical technology / descendant of artificial-person architecture
- human parents adopted it to save/improve children's viability
- baseline and seeded people coexisted inside families for generations
- provenance records became politically dangerous
- Continuity Settlement removed provenance from ordinary civil rank and compartmentalized medical/history data
- modern society remembers equality reform but lost the technical historical crosswalk connecting seeded cognition to older artificial-person lineage
- original biological humans mostly died ordinary generational deaths while becoming ancestors of the present civilization

Primary sources:
- `canon/TRANSITION_HISTORY_V1.md`
- `canon/ARTIFICIAL_ORIGIN_HISTORY_CROSSWALK_V1.md`

## Why the system still knows “1”

Resolved:

- there is no secret `one human database`
- medical systems maintain anonymous current service-capacity counts for rare developmental routes
- a legacy safety adapter maps the modern non-seeded developmental route to an old human-origin category
- aggregate count can therefore be verified while identity remains private

Primary source:
`canon/LEGACY_HUMAN_COUNT_SYSTEM_V1.md`

Important discipline:
Current astronomical facts are separated from future in-universe survey data.

Working mission scale:
