# Character State Ledger

Status: **ACTIVE PRE-BLUEPRINT LEDGER**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Track the internal and external state of major recurring characters across the seven Acts.

State fields:
- `Goal`
- `Belief`
- `Authority / Agency`
- `Known Truth`
- `Cost / Loss`
- `Next Pressure`

This ledger tracks character movement independently of relationship movement.

---

# C1 — Haren Savin

## Act I
Goal: complete difficult transitions without breaking communities.
Belief: accurate records + humane planning can reconcile competing lives.
Authority: respected mid-level officer with limited binding powers.
Known Truth: notices obsolete developmental category; learns baseline count = 1.
Cost: early competence solutions consume real resources and create losers.
Next Pressure: asks what category describes the other 299,999.

## Act II
Goal: understand seeded development while keeping projects functioning.
Belief: categories may be incomplete, but good systems can still contain complexity.
Authority: intact.
Known Truth: his own birth followed standard seeded-development architecture.
Cost: damages family trust by treating memory as evidence.
Next Pressure: why did origin distinctions disappear?

## Act III
Goal: prevent public-truth cascade from destroying services.
Belief: truth and order are not automatically enemies, but sequencing can itself become power.
Authority: increasingly public-facing.
Known Truth: population-wide artificial cognitive provenance becomes historically clear.
Cost: institutional trust deteriorates around him.
Next Pressure: who is the sole baseline person and who owns that information?

## Act IV
Goal: protect ordinary civic rights during BH-1 identification pressure.
Belief: begins recognizing protection without consent as another form of control.
Authority: reduced after old Yuna-record falsification is exposed.
Known Truth: Nadia is BH-1; Haren is definitively seeded-origin.
Cost: career authority, reputation, sibling trust.
Next Pressure: act effectively without unilateral authority.

## Act V
Goal: help design legitimate arrival transition under shared authority.
Belief: expertise is necessary but cannot substitute for representation.
Authority: delegated/coordinated rather than unilateral.
Known Truth: founder mission, reversibility safeguard, Sol silence context.
Cost: some preferred plans blocked by consent and resource limits.
Next Pressure: irreversible conversion begins.

## Act VI
Goal: execute transitions that acknowledge loss rather than hiding it.
Belief: continuity can survive change, but not every form can be preserved.
Authority: shared with local/system actors.
Known Truth: no hidden solution will save every place/resource.
Cost: H8's original form ends; model failures become public.
Next Pressure: scale mature method to civilization without becoming ruler.

## Act VII
Goal: enable several worlds to emerge without ranking persons by provenance.
Belief: truthful difference + equal civic dignity + distributed choice.
Authority: one coordinator among many.
Known Truth: central mysteries closed; future remains plural.
Cost: accepts decisions and risks he would not personally choose.
Final State: expert without ownership of others' futures.

# Ledger Rules

1. Episode Blueprint references `C#` when a character state changes.
2. Revelation alone does not count unless it alters goal/belief/agency/cost.
3. Core characters may disappear for stretches if their independent track is documented.
4. No core character should enter the final Act with all internal conflicts already solved.
5. No character may receive a new secret wound late merely to manufacture climax.
