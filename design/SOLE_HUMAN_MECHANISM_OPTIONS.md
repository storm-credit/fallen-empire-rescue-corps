# Sole Biological Human — Mechanism Options

Status: COMPARATIVE DESIGN — NOT FROZEN
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

## Design Requirement

The existence of exactly one biological/baseline human must:

- be causally believable
- not turn the person into a chosen savior
- not grant combat/supernatural authority
- not copy `Star Child`’s machine-created last-human-child structure
- support mystery before and after the identity reveal
- matter socially without making every other life objectively lesser
- not require thousands of doctors to knowingly maintain a lie

---

# Option A — Anonymous Baseline Continuity Protocol

## Mechanism

A deep legacy protocol maintains one unmodified human developmental instance at a time, anonymously embedded in ordinary society.

The person is generated from preserved baseline germline material and develops without the artificial cognition-seeding process used for standard citizens.

## Original purpose candidates

- validate that preserved baseline germline remains viable
- preserve an unmodified developmental pathway as civilizational redundancy
- provide long-term compatibility reference for destination ecology/medicine
- satisfy an old ethical requirement that biological humanity not be made irreversibly extinct

## Strengths

- clean explanation for exactly one
- identity can remain hidden even from the person
- supports old charter/mission mystery
- no need for bloodline inheritance
- can make “true human” status a mundane technical category

## Weaknesses

- risks “secret experiment” feeling
- can still feel like chosen-one machinery even without powers
- one-person scientific sample is weak unless purpose is carefully framed
- requires convincing reason for anonymity

## Chosen-one risk

MEDIUM.

## Mystery value

VERY HIGH.

## Thematic value

HIGH if the protocol treats the human as **a reference specimen, not a ruler**.

---

# Option B — Rare Unseeded Birth

## Mechanism

Modern children normally receive the developmental cognition seed during gestation. In an extremely rare failure, sabotage, or unrecognized exception, one child’s nervous system develops without it.

That person becomes the only current baseline-development human.

## Strengths

- strongest anti-chosen-one answer
- “the only human” may literally be the result of a medical/process anomaly
- undermines biological supremacy
- no secret lineage or royal protocol required
- powerful irony: society may obsess over someone who was never meant to be special

## Weaknesses

- high-tech reproductive medicine should normally detect the failure
- “exactly one” can feel coincidental
- requires deciding whether engineered bodies without seeding still qualify as baseline biological human
- harder to connect to destination/history unless later institutions exploit the accident

## Chosen-one risk

VERY LOW.

## Mystery value

HIGH.

## Thematic value

VERY HIGH.

## Special design opportunity

The eventual reveal could be:

> There was never a sacred “last human program.” The category reappeared by accident.

This strongly attacks the belief that biological uniqueness implies destiny.

# Option C — Last Survivor of a Hidden Baseline Minority

## Mechanism

After the artificial-successor transition, a small number of people retained unmodified biological reproduction for ideological, religious, scientific, or consent reasons.

Their population dwindled over generations through choice, infertility, migration/assimilation into standard reproduction, and mortality until only one remains.

## Strengths

- rich historical conflict
- makes the sole human a consequence of social history, not a machine protocol
- supports factions/religion/old rights
- strong family/genealogy mystery

## Weaknesses

- bloodline/chosen-family risk
- harder to explain total public ignorance
- hidden lineage can resemble secret-royalty fiction
- more records/medical traces should exist
- may imply biological “purity” ideology, which must be handled carefully

## Chosen-one risk

HIGH.

## Mystery value

VERY HIGH.

## Thematic value

HIGH but dangerous.

---

# Option D — Original Human Recovered from Legacy Preservation

## Mechanism

One biological person/embryo from the launch-era population is recovered or awakened from long-term preservation.

## Strengths

- easy explanation of biological status
- direct bridge to original voyage
- immediate historical contrast

## Weaknesses

- extremely high chosen-one/outsider risk
- easily becomes “person from the past explains everything”
- cryogenic amnesia would feel contrived
- strong overlap with many classic colony-ship premises
- weakens the protagonist’s uncertainty if protagonist is the recovered person

## Chosen-one risk

VERY HIGH.

## Mystery value

MEDIUM after reveal.

## Thematic value

MEDIUM.

## Current verdict

REJECT as primary mechanism unless radically transformed.

---

# Option E — Deliberate Illegal Birth

## Mechanism

Someone knowingly bypasses the standard developmental seeding process and creates a baseline human for ideological, personal, religious, or scientific reasons.

The child/person is not told the truth.

## Strengths

- strong culprit/motive mystery
- human status is a consequence of another person’s choice, not destiny
- can create intimate betrayal
- naturally connects to family and factions

## Weaknesses

- tends to turn the story into “who created the special child?”
- creator may dominate the central mystery
- risks secret cult / mad scientist cliché
- difficult to explain system-level count without detection

## Chosen-one risk

MEDIUM–HIGH.

## Mystery value

HIGH.

## Thematic value

HIGH if the creator’s motive is morally mixed.

# Current Recommendation: A/B Hybrid, with a crucial inversion

Do **not** make the protagonist the heir of a sacred preservation program.

Preferred design to stress-test:

1. An ancient continuity protocol once required that the ship retain the *capacity* for baseline human development.
2. The active “one baseline person” may not have been continuously maintained as a revered specimen.
3. A rare modern reproductive routing event—possibly caused by the old protocol automatically exercising that capacity—produced the current baseline individual.
4. No current institution necessarily intended to create a “true human.”
5. The person has no authority and may have worse compatibility with modern medicine.
6. The ship’s oldest systems classify the person, but current society lacks the conceptual context to understand the classification.

This hybrid preserves:

- Option A’s causal connection to voyage history
- Option B’s anti-chosen-one thematic force
- exact-one plausibility through a legacy constraint
- uncertainty over whether anyone currently controls the process

## Why this is better than pure A

The human is not a secretly groomed sample or monarch.

## Why this is better than pure B

The existence of exactly one is not pure coincidence; it is a side effect of a forgotten continuity safeguard.

# Critical Question Still Open

The identity of the sole baseline human is **not selected**.

Do not choose based on twist shock alone.

Candidates later must be tested against:

- protagonist arc
- family arc
- romance/intimacy arc
- political consequences
- ending philosophy
- reread/recontextualization value
- chosen-one risk

A particularly important possibility remains:

> The protagonist is **not** the sole biological human.

If this creates the strongest emotional/thematic resolution, it should be preferred over making the protagonist special.
