## 0. Why v2 Exists

Red-team review identified a weak assumption in v1:

> A scientifically advanced society cannot plausibly fail to notice that its own bodies and brains contain engineered structures.

v2 corrects this.

Modern citizens **know they are technologically modified descendants of launch humanity**.

What they do not know is that the standard developmental system crossed a historical threshold from:

`supporting human neurodevelopment`

to

`bootstrapping an artificial cognitive architecture that then develops into a unique embodied person`.

This distinction is the hidden ontology.

# 1. Three Levels of Description

## 1.1 What ordinary citizens believe

> We are humans whose bodies and prenatal development were medically adapted over generations to survive a closed interstellar civilization.

This is not a stupid belief.

It is historically incomplete.

## 1.2 What modern science knows

Modern science knows:

- human genomes have been heavily curated/engineered
- many organs contain designed biological features
- standard fetal neurodevelopment includes an engineered cognitive-development scaffold
- regenerative medicine interfaces with that scaffold
- different historical generations used different versions

Modern science generally treats these as **human biomedical technology**.

## 1.3 What lost historical science knew

Transition-era technical literature distinguished a point at which the scaffold ceased merely stabilizing baseline cognition and became the actual **generative architecture of person-level cognition**.

The mature person still develops through life and embodiment, but the mind’s foundational cognitive architecture is designed rather than arising through baseline Homo sapiens neurodevelopment.

That old threshold was once described using concepts equivalent to:

- artificial general cognition
- synthetic mind
- constructed cognitive lineage

Final terminology remains OPEN.

# 2. Objective Ontological Categories

These are design categories, not necessarily current legal terms.

## BH — Baseline-development human

A person whose:

- germline/body development follows the preserved baseline Homo sapiens developmental route within acceptable natural/medical variation
- nervous system does not use the generative cognition-seed architecture
- conscious architecture emerges from baseline human neurodevelopment

This does **not** mean medically untouched or genetically pristine.

The story must avoid purity language.

## SP — Seeded person

A person whose:

- body is living, organic, and developmentally grown
- nervous system includes a designed bio-computational scaffold
- foundational cognition is bootstrapped by a developmental cognition seed
- unique self emerges through infancy, embodiment, learning, relationships, and experience

This person is artificial in cognitive origin and natural/biological in much of embodiment.

## RC — Restored continuity claimant

A person reconstructed using a prior person’s captured neural/cognitive state.

RC status is **not** synonymous with SP status.

Most ordinary citizens are SP whether or not they have ever been restored.

# 3. The Developmental Cognition Seed

## 3.1 What it is not

Not:

- a personality chip
- a stored adult AI
- a hidden remote-control program
- a soul file
- a server connection

## 3.2 Working technical concept

A cognition seed is a designed self-organizing developmental kernel instantiated in a living neural scaffold.

It provides early architecture for:

- general learning
- sensory integration
- body-model formation
- self/other differentiation
- memory organization
- executive control development
- long-term neural plasticity

It does not contain:

- adult memories
- final personality
- specific beliefs
- predetermined loyalty

## 3.3 Why the mature person is unique

Once development begins, the mind is continuously altered by:

- fetal/body state
- infancy
- attachment
- sensory history
- random neural development
- education
- trauma
- relationships
- disease
- bodily aging

Two children initialized with identical seed architecture would diverge into different people.

## 3.4 Why this counts as artificial consciousness

The seed’s architecture is intentionally designed and historically descends from artificial cognitive research.

The person is therefore not an NPC or software copy.

They are an **artificially originated embodied consciousness**.

This distinction must be explained narratively through history/events, not one definition dump.

# 4. Reproduction v2

## 4.1 What parents experience

Real:

- conception/fertility treatment
- pregnancy
- fetal growth
- childbirth
- infant dependency
- childhood development
- family resemblance

## 4.2 What modern medicine does openly

Prenatal care includes a standard developmental-neural intervention.

Citizens know it exists.

They understand it as analogous to a foundational medical safeguard that generations of children receive.

Not receiving it would be considered medically extraordinary.

## 4.3 Biological inheritance

Parents contribute a large share of:

- visible phenotype
- metabolism
- disease risk
- sensory traits
- body proportions
- other engineered biological traits

Population systems maintain genetic diversity and prevent harmful bottlenecks.

## 4.4 Cognitive inheritance

Parents do **not** transmit memories.

Seed-generation can use:

- population-safe architecture templates
- limited heritable temperament/developmental parameters
- stochastic variation
- embryo/body compatibility data

The system must preserve unpredictability and genuine childhood development.

## 4.5 Family truth

A seeded child is not socially “assigned” to parents after manufacture.

The parents genuinely brought that developing organism/person into the world and raised them.

This is essential to the emotional thesis.

# 5. Medicine v2

## 5.1 What routine medicine sees

Current SP physiology is normal modern physiology.

Doctors know about:

- engineered organs
- neural scaffold markers
- prenatal developmental seed history
- regenerative interfaces

None of these imply “not human” under modern medicine.

## 5.2 Baseline-development anomaly

A BH person is not perfectly invisible.

Specialists could identify a rare profile such as:

- absence of standard seed architecture
- missing scaffold markers
- atypical regenerative interface
- unusual neural topology
- different developmental epigenetics

Modern interpretation might be:

`legacy developmental phenotype / rare unseeded developmental condition`

rather than:

`the only real human`.

## 5.3 Why the historical meaning is obscure

The missing link is not anatomy.

It is the old mapping:

`unseeded development = baseline Homo sapiens cognition`

versus

`seeded development = artificial cognitive lineage`.

That mapping was politically, legally, and technically dismantled over centuries.

# 7. Aging and Death v2

SP citizens are not immortal software.

Their identity is instantiated in a living, continuously changing brain/body.

Aging arises from:

- tissue damage
- metabolic wear
- imperfect regeneration
- cumulative neural/body changes

Life extension exists but should not make generations meaningless.

# 8. Restoration v2

## 8.1 Why the original seed cannot restore an adult

The seed contains developmental architecture, not the developed person.

## 8.2 State capture

A continuity capture requires high-resolution mapping of a mature living mind.

It is:

- periodic
- expensive
- technically imperfect
- privacy-sensitive
- resource intensive

## 8.3 Reconstruction

A captured state can guide development/conditioning of a new compatible neural substrate/body.

The reconstructed person may recover:

- substantial autobiographical memory
- habits
- preferences
- relational memory

but cannot recover post-capture experience and may experience integration differences.

## 8.4 Social result

Science can demonstrate strong psychological continuity without proving metaphysical identity.

Hence RC law/religion/family conflict remains genuine.

# 9. History v2

The transition begins with human medicine.

### Stage 1
Cognitive scaffolds save fetuses/children with severe neurodevelopmental risk.

### Stage 2
Scaffolds become safer and more generative.

### Stage 3
They allow bodies/neural systems to be redesigned for ship survival.

### Stage 4
The seeded pathway becomes safer and more common by parental choice.

### Stage 5
Baseline, modified, and seeded children coexist inside the same families.

### Stage 6
Origin/provenance records become tools of discrimination and resource politics.

### Stage 7
A civil settlement removes provenance from ordinary legal personhood and fragments dangerous registries.

### Stage 8
Seeded development becomes demographically universal.

### Stage 9
Modern science remembers the technology but no longer treats it as a separate artificial species lineage.

This sequence supersedes any interpretation that the population simply “forgot it had engineered brains.”

- they are born to human-descended families
- they have continuous culture/history

The eventual revelation therefore does **not** prove they were foolish.

It reveals that “human” has already changed meaning historically.
