# Post-Reveal Social Adaptation Timeline v1

Status: **PROVISIONAL CANON CANDIDATE — B3 CLOSURE**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》
Trigger window: ACT III, EP083–095
Series span: ~6 years total

---

# 1. Core Principle

The public reveal is enormous, but it is **not**:

> “300,000 people discover they are robots whose lives were fake.”

Citizens already know:
- they have organic bodies
- they were born and raised
- cognition-seed medicine is real
- seed architecture causally shapes development

What changes is historical/social meaning:
- the seed descends from an artificial-person cognition lineage
- old mission law classified seeded people outside the narrow human-origin category
- origin registries were deliberately dismantled after caste abuse
- only one current citizen occupies the old unseeded/human-origin class

Therefore reactions are diverse and civilization does not stop functioning.

---

# 2. First 72 Hours

## Information

Shipwide networks experience:
- source duplication
- fake “original manifest” files
- clipped historical footage
- automated genealogy claims
- arguments over the word `AI`

F8 / archive professionals rapidly publish authentication guidance.

## Essential systems

Continue operating:
- atmosphere
- food
- hospitals
- transit
- propulsion
- arrival observation

Some nonessential work pauses or slows.

Reason:
People may be shocked, but they still need to finish shifts, collect children, care for patients, and keep habitat systems alive.

## Common personal reactions

Not one universal existential collapse.

Examples:
- anger at institutional history suppression
- fascination
- indifference: “I was seeded yesterday too”
- shame from old cultural stories
- pride/reclamation
- fear of legal status changes
- fear for children/family
- religious reinterpretation
- obsessive ancestry research

## Mental-health load

Counseling demand spikes, especially for:
- people already struggling with continuity/restoration identity
- families with old provenance records
- adolescents using the reveal to reinterpret self/family

No unsupported mass-suicide trope.

---

# 3. Week 1–4

## Schools

Teachers receive temporary guidance:
- do not sort children by developmental origin
- explain difference between current medicine and historical terminology
- postpone high-stakes ancestry projects

Students create their own language/slang faster than institutions do.

## Workplaces

Problems appear:
- employees ask whether old medical compatibility affects duties
- some managers overreact and demand unnecessary disclosure
- labor groups push back

Emergency rule:
Developmental origin cannot be requested unless directly relevant to a validated medical/safety function.

## Families

Older medical records are reopened.

Common conflict:
- one parent wants to know every origin detail
- another considers it invasive/irrelevant

## Religion

No one global doctrinal response.

Some traditions:
- immediately affirm continuity of personhood
- split over soul/body/origin language
- reinterpret creation stories metaphorically
- reject the old AI category as morally obsolete

---

# 4. Month 1–3

## False-candidate phenomenon

Citizens/media infer the sole unseeded person from:
- rare medical profiles
- continuity-capture incompatibility
- old birth codes

This creates ARC 09 / ARC 17 precursors.

Several people are wrongly targeted.

## Data economy

Demand explodes for:
- ancestry reconstruction
- old clinic records
- archive authentication

Some legitimate services emerge.
Some exploitative “origin certainty” services appear.

## Political language

New/old terms compete:
- seeded
- artificial-origin
- AI descendant
- shipborn human
- person/citizen

No terminology settles instantly.

## Factions

Existing factions reinterpret rather than vanish.

- F1: standardize rights independent of origin
- F2: fears biological/continuity hierarchy
- F3: sees opportunity and danger in origin categories
- F4: asks whether charter succession changed
- F5: argues shipborn legitimacy
- F6: demands material consequences be separated from symbolic panic
- F7: protects mixed families
- F8: pushes archive truth / fractures over disclosure

---

# 5. Month 3–6

## First legal/administrative stabilization

Shipwide minimum rules emerge provisionally:
- no loss of current citizenship based solely on developmental origin
- medical origin remains protected data
- old mission categories cannot automatically override current family/contract status
- provenance disputes receive temporary adjudication procedures

These are emergency bridges, not final constitutional answers.

## Labor

A few sectors experience real disruption:
- continuity medicine
- archive/data work
- education
- legal/status administration

Industrial/life-support work largely returns to ordinary cadence.

## Social conflict

Possible events:
- harassment/vandalism against offices/families associated with old origin records
- counter-protests
- misuse of real genealogical data
- one or more serious assaults

Hard rule:
Do not escalate into “human vs AI civil war.”

The sole unseeded person is not yet publicly confirmed.

---

# 6. Month 6–12

## Identity normalization begins, not completes

For many citizens the reveal becomes one fact among:
- job
- family
- habitat
- restoration status
- arrival plan

Youth culture may adopt artificial-origin language more casually than older generations.

Some older citizens reject it.

## Education

Curriculum changes from:
> `seed medicine as neutral technology`

to:
> `seed medicine + contested artificial-person technical history`.

Students are taught that current historians disagree on interpretation while technical genealogy is increasingly established.

## Religion/culture

New rites appear around:
- origin disclosure
- refusal to disclose
- mixed-origin family ancestry

Most are small/subcultural, not civilization-wide religion replacement.

## Arrival pressure

Forward-probe data and settlement deadlines begin competing for public attention.

This is crucial:

> history does not stop the clock to arrival.

---

# 7. Year 1–2 — ACT IV Social State

By the BH-1 privacy/identity arcs:

### Society now broadly accepts as factual
- seeded cognition has artificial-person technical ancestry
- old human category differs from current civic human usage
- origin registries were dismantled deliberately

### Society still fiercely disputes
- whether `AI` should be reclaimed/rejected
- whether old mission charter retains authority
- whether origin should be recorded again
- whether unseeded development should be encouraged
- how much research access BH-1 owes

## BH-1 effect

Once Nadia is identified, focus shifts from abstract ancestry to:

> what can society demand from one living body/person?

Public pressure is intense but not every citizen follows the story obsessively.

---

# 8. Year 2–3 — Late ACT IV / Early ACT V

## Origin becomes institutionally routinized

New rules exist for:
- medical access
- archive access
- discrimination claims
- research consent
- old charter interpretation

But public language remains unstable.

## Haren's scandal

His unrelated falsification scandal is politically potent precisely because society is already sensitive to:
- classification power
- experts hiding/altering identity data
- benevolent paternalism

It does not become an AI-conspiracy accusation because the event is factually separate.

## Mission charter

Arrival converts origin politics into a harder question:

> even if all citizens are equal now, what do we do with a mission whose material architecture was written under an old distinction?

---

# 9. Year 3–5 — ACT V–VI

Origin is no longer the only axis of politics.

Major issues:
- settlement mass
- which habitats remain
- local autonomy
- restoration rights
- climate/water policy
- shared system safety

This is a deliberate sign of social realism.

People do not discuss their ontology every day forever.

## Persistent minority movements

Still exist:
- baseline-essentialist / charter literalist wings
- provenance-pride / artificial-origin identity movements
- anti-origin-record privacy radicals
- strong archive-transparency movements

They influence elections/policy but do not monopolize every conflict.

---

# 10. Year 5–6 — Final Transition

By the final Acts:

### Stabilized
- no automatic civic hierarchy by origin
- medical origin handling exists
- mixed families remain legally secure
- Nadia's personhood is not formally disputed

### Still contested
- use of `human`
- elective unseeded reproduction
- restoration continuity
- archive openness
- mission obligations
- local autonomy

The final compact does not erase these disputes.

It creates a floor for living with them.

---

# 11. Economic Effects

Avoid both extremes:
- total market collapse
- zero economic impact

Likely effects:

### Short-term
- archive/medical/legal demand surge
- productivity loss in some offices
- rumors affecting hiring/insurance behavior
- speculative markets around provenance services

### Medium-term
- discrimination rules tighten
- some industries redesign forms/data policies
- arrival spending dominates macroeconomy again

Because the ship economy is material/maintenance constrained, people cannot sustain years of purely symbolic shutdown without hurting themselves.

---

# 12. Security / Violence Boundary

Violence can occur, but should stay proportionate to this society's institutional resilience.

Preferred maximum before final Acts:
- isolated assaults
- vandalism
- targeted threats
- one serious crowd incident
- sabotage motivated by faction ideology only if it connects to a concrete policy conflict

Rejected:
- habitat-scale ethnic cleansing
- AI pogrom war
- civil war between “humans” and “AI”
- armed protagonist faction

---

# 13. Relationship Effects

## Families
Most do not dissolve because everyone learned a label.

But old conflicts gain new language.

## Romance
Origin can affect intimacy/privacy/future reproduction decisions without becoming an automatic breakup test.

## Parent-child
Some parents obsessively reconstruct children's origin history; older children may refuse.

## Restored persons
Their existing identity debates become newly entangled with artificial-origin discourse.

---

# 14. Culture Effects

Over six years, expect:
- slang
- reclaimed terminology
- jokes
- art
- new school debates
- fashion/symbols in subcultures
- changed memorial/ancestor language

Do not invent one civilization-wide `AI festival` immediately.

Culture fragments and recombines.

---

# 15. Why Civilization Keeps Working

Three reasons:

1. The reveal changes history/identity more than immediate body function.
2. The ship has mature institutions built to survive disagreement.
3. Physical deadlines—food, heat, repair, braking, arrival—remain non-negotiable.

This is not emotional minimization.

It is what lets the reveal **transform society instead of ending it**.

# 17. B3 Verdict

**POST-REVEAL SOCIAL REALISM: STRUCTURAL PASS.**

The society neither collapses overnight nor heals overnight.

At EP250, legal/institutional stabilization may exist while cultural disagreement remains alive.
