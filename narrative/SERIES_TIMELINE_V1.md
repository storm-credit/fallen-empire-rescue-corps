# Series Timeline v1

Status: **PROVISIONAL PREFERRED TIMELINE**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose: align the 250-episode narrative with the physical voyage so the finale can reach actual system arrival without implausible sudden time jumps.

---

# 1. Correction

Previous loose working note placed series start ~10–20 years before arrival.

That is too early if the 250-episode ending is meant to show:
- completed ship conversion
- orbital/planetary settlement becoming real
- plural future physically enacted

Preferred correction:

## **Series begins ~6 years before final target-system arrival / orbital insertion.**

The ship is already ~90+ years into a century-class braking phase.

---

# 2. Physical Order of Magnitude

Illustrative deceleration profile only:

- peak cruise: ~0.02c (~6,000 km/s)
- prolonged braking: ~100 years class
- simple average deceleration order: ~0.002 m/s²

If roughly six years remain under a broadly similar final-braking profile:

- remaining speed can be a few hundred km/s class
- remaining distance can be a few hundred AU class

This puts the generation ship in the **outer reaches of the target system**, not halfway through interstellar space.

Exact braking curve will not be constant and remains engineering-open.

---

# 3. Why Six Years Works Narratively

## Immediate arrival culture
Every citizen has grown up knowing arrival is near enough to affect their lifetime.

## Irreversible deadline
Some settlement-fabrication decisions must be locked within ~1–2 years of EP001.

## Scouts
Small forward survey craft can be months/years ahead of the massive ship and provide increasingly detailed destination data.

## Character age
A ~6-year series span permits meaningful life change without turning the cast into a different generation.

Working age movement:
- protagonist: ~36 → ~42
- sister: ~25 → ~31
- BH-1: ~52 → ~58

## Macro payoff
ACT VII can physically show orbital insertion / first sustained settlements instead of ending with a promise ten years away.

# 6. Arrival Calendar as Everyday Culture

At EP001, arrival is already:
- part of school curriculum
- a career-planning axis
- a reserve-budget argument
- a source of family plans
- an ordinary countdown in public infrastructure

This prevents ACT V from feeling like a new novel.

Citizens can disagree about what “arrival” means even before ontology truth:
- surface settlement
- orbital settlement
- ship continuation
- professional opportunity
- unwanted disruption

---

# 7. Forward Scout Architecture

The huge generation ship does not need to be first to sample every local environment.

During late braking it deploys much smaller forward craft with far higher acceleration.

Scouts can:
- map outer-system hazards
- refine planet atmosphere/weather
- survey orbital resources
- place navigation beacons
- test communications

They do **not**:
- build a secret colony
- contain human-level hidden AI society
- solve all settlement engineering

Their improved data is one trigger for ACT V.

---

# 8. Character Timeline Consequences

A six-year span allows:

### Sister
complete training / choose settlement career independently.

### Protagonist
move from mid-level trusted specialist → disciplinary damage → delegated civilization transition integrator without overnight promotion.

### BH-1
move from cooperative succession planning → public ontology crisis → chosen new future.

### Mother
experience multi-year real relocation preparation rather than one-day emotional demolition.

### Intimate relationship
resolve through actual repeated choices over years rather than one crisis confession.

---

# 9. Mystery Timing Benefit

Fast early reveals still occur over months, not one frenetic week.

The public artificial-origin revelation can happen ~1 year into the series.

That leaves ~5 years of post-truth society before final arrival.

This supports the project's central goal:

> truth changes civilization rather than ending the story.
