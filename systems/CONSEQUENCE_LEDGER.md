# Consequence Ledger

Status: **ACTIVE PRE-BLUEPRINT LEDGER**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Prevent episodic reset. Major events must leave durable personal, social, political, material, or informational effects.

Every major Arc later records at least one persistent consequence.

Consequence families:
- `PERS` personal/psychological
- `REL` relationship
- `INST` institutional/legal
- `SOC` social/cultural
- `POL` faction/political
- `MAT` material/resource/infrastructure
- `INFO` information ecology
- `ARR` arrival/mission

---

# CQ-01 — ARC 01 H8 transition redesign

Event:
Haren changes a nominally equivalent relocation plan after finding hidden care/work/school dependencies.

Immediate gain:
Several households retain practical continuity.

Persistent consequences:
- MAT: extra transit/material capacity is consumed.
- POL: another stakeholder becomes a visible loser.
- INST: dependency-network mapping gains legitimacy as a planning tool.
- REL: Mara sees both the usefulness and danger of Haren's profession.
- CHAR: Nadia exists in the affected cooperative network before her later reveal.

Must reappear:
Act IV Nadia transition politics; Act VI H8 preservation debate.

# Consequence Propagation Rules

1. Every Arc Blueprint lists at least one `CQ` created, advanced, or paid.
2. If an event affects law, later cases must reflect the changed law.
3. If material is consumed/destroyed, it cannot silently return.
4. If trust is broken, later warmth must be earned through state change.
5. Public truth cannot be "forgotten" because later Arc needs old normality.
6. At least one consequence from Acts I–II must still materially matter in Acts VI–VII.
