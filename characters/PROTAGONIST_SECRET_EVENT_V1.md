# Protagonist Secret Event v1

Status: **PROVISIONAL PREFERRED CHARACTER CANON**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose: turn the protagonist's private hypocrisy into a concrete past action with real beneficiaries, real cost, and a direct thematic echo in the finale.

---

# 1. Timing

Working chronology:

- protagonist current age: ~36
- younger sister current age: ~25
- incident: ~11 years before series start
- protagonist: ~25, junior transition staff / early-career field officer
- sister: ~14

The event predates the central ontology mystery by many years.

It is not connected to hidden baseline-human history.

---

# 2. Background Crisis

A major H8 Legacy Habitat subdistrict undergoes an **emergency long-duration relocation** after a coupled infrastructure problem.

Preferred cause:

- coolant / environmental-service contamination
- followed by structural access closure for overhaul

Not:
- terrorist attack
- AI malfunction conspiracy
- giant war event

The point is ordinary aging-ship reality.

Residents must be redistributed for many months under emergency transition rules.

---

# 3. Sister's Real Medical Situation

At the time, the sister is finishing treatment for a non-mystical neurodevelopmental/vestibular disorder.

Working form:

- an adolescent developmental coordination / gravity-adaptation condition
- managed through a specific H4 clinic + school support network
- condition has improved enough that formal high-priority dependency status has recently expired

She is not dying.

She does **not** require a unique secret technology.

However, moving her far from:
- the clinic team
- school support
- trusted adults
- peers

would create a meaningful risk of regression and educational/social disruption.

---

# 4. The Emergency Algorithm

Emergency relocation uses a protected `continuity dependency` classification to prioritize scarce placements near:

- specialist clinics
- accessible transit
- schools
- caregivers

The sister's certification expired shortly before the crisis.

Under the valid record, her household would be assigned to a more distant overflow district.

The system is not evil.

There are simply fewer high-continuity placements than households who would benefit from them.

---

# 5. What the Protagonist Does

The protagonist has legitimate work access to transition records.

He changes one field so his sister's recently expired dependency remains listed as **active through the relocation period**.

Working form:

- backdates/extends the certification window
- attributes the extension to continuity-of-care review
- does not forge a physician's entire medical record

This distinction is important.

He manipulates **administrative eligibility**, not medical reality.

---

# 6. Immediate Result

His family receives a scarce temporary placement connected to the sister's existing H4 care/school network.

The sister does well.

Her recovery, education, and social stability genuinely benefit.

From the protagonist's perspective, the choice appears vindicated.

This is why he can live with the secret for years.

---

# 7. The Hidden Cost

The high-continuity placement was scarce.

His alteration displaces another eligible household down the queue.

Do not make the result melodramatically lethal.

Better consequence:

- another family receives a farther placement
- their caregiving burden grows
- one adult reduces work
- a child/elder changes program or clinic
- the family absorbs real but survivable cost

The protagonist did **not** consciously choose a named victim.

The system hid the displaced cost from him in the moment.

This is precisely the flaw:

> He optimized the person he could see by moving cost onto people he could not.

---

# 8. Why Sister Did Not Meaningfully Consent

At 14, the sister knew only:

- her brother was helping the family stay near her clinic/school

She did not know:

- he altered eligibility
- the altered status would remain in derived administrative history
- another household could lose priority
- the record might later affect her adult certifications

Even if she would have said “yes” at 14, that does not equal informed adult consent to a durable falsified identity record.

---

# 9. Why the False Record Persists

The original source field later expires, but derived systems preserve historical classifications for:

- continuity audits
- housing history
- care-plan provenance
- emergency planning
- professional risk/placement assessment

No one is continually treating the sister as ill.

But the history exists as a trusted signed event.

This is much more plausible than one false checkbox magically controlling her whole adult life.

---

# 10. Present-Day Trigger

The sister is applying for / entering a **settlement ecology / environmental systems field track** with stricter medical/field-history review because it involves:

- remote work
- gravity transitions
- destination training
- limited specialist support

Her old record conflicts with:

- contemporaneous clinic notes
- later recovery documentation
- transition-system timestamps

At first it looks like ordinary legacy-data inconsistency.

Later provenance-era audits and increased scrutiny make it impossible to ignore.

---

# 11. Sister's Reaction

Do not write a simple:

> “You lied, I hate you.”

Her reaction should contain incompatible truths:

- the placement probably helped her
- she remembers wanting to stay
- her brother acted out of care
- he still decided what risk she should accept
- he never told her what he did
- another household paid part of the cost
- the record now belongs to her life, not his conscience

Her strongest grievance:

> He has spent years telling other communities that procedure protects everyone while privately deciding his family deserved an exception.

---

# 12. Professional / Legal Consequences

The act is serious but does not automatically turn the protagonist into a felon fugitive.

Working consequences:

### Professional
- immediate suspension of certain signing/record-amendment authority
- ethics/disciplinary review
- loss of eligibility for some central promotion pathways
- transition projects reassigned or placed under co-signature

### Administrative/legal
- formal correction of sister's historical record under her control
- review of the displaced household consequence
- possible restitution/priority remedy where still meaningful
- finding of unauthorized administrative alteration / conflict of interest

### Political
- rivals/factions can legitimately question whether protagonist's “care-centered planning” is selective paternalism
- supporters cannot simply dismiss it as a technicality

### Narrative safeguard
The protagonist can continue participating later because:
- competence remains needed
- penalty is not erased
- later authority is delegated/earned under transparent constraints
- final role does not require restoring the old pristine career path

---

# 13. Why He Did Not Confess Earlier

Weak reason:
> “I forgot.”

Rejected.

Preferred layered reasons:

1. He believes the outcome proves the rule was wrong.
2. The sister recovered, so reopening it seems like needless harm.
3. Confession would affect his career and family.
4. He tells himself no one was seriously hurt.
5. Most importantly, his profession lets him transform guilt into a principle:

> I learned to look beyond the form.

But he has not fully learned:

> I also took someone else's right to choose.

---

# 14. Thematic Function

This event is the miniature of the whole series.

## Early protagonist logic

`The category is wrong → I know the humane outcome → I will override the category.`

## What he eventually learns

A category can be wrong **and** a benevolent expert can still become coercive.

Final transition ethics therefore require all three:

- truthful information
- material constraints
- meaningful agency/consent

None is sufficient alone.

---

# 15. Finale Payoff

The protagonist must face a civilization-scale temptation structurally similar to the old incident:

> He can produce a plan that he sincerely believes preserves more people, but only by treating community consent as an obstacle to optimize around.

He must not repeat the old act.

This does not mean unlimited local veto.

The mature answer is a process where:
- constraints are honestly disclosed
- minimum obligations are shared
- communities have real choices among viable paths
- costs displaced onto others are visible

---

# 16. Mystery Separation Rule

The falsification must remain **independent of the main AI/human conspiracy/history**.

Do not later reveal:
- sister was secretly BH-1
- the medical condition was hidden ontology
- protagonist was manipulated into altering the record
- mentor ordered the falsification

It matters because the protagonist chose it.

---

# 17. Current Verdict

This version is stronger than a generic “he lied to save someone” secret because:

- the benefit was real
- the rule had a real reason
- another household paid a hidden cost
- sister's consent was incomplete
- the false record has plausible long-term administrative residue
- the act directly predicts the protagonist's final blind spot

Recommendation: **PROMOTE after sister/other-household character consequences are designed.**
