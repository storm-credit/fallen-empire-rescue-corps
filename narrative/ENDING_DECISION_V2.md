# Ending Decision v2

Status: **PROVISIONAL WORKING ENDING — NOT FROZEN UNTIL MACRO/RED-TEAM PASS**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

## 1. Working Ending Selection

Adopt a synthesis centered on:

### Philosophical answer
**Plural personhood / truthful provenance without hierarchy**

### Destination answer
**Rewritten settlement rather than total obedience or total refusal**

This combines the strongest parts of previous END-B, END-C, and END-D without flattening them into a compromise-only ending.

---

# 2. Final Philosophical Answer

The civilization eventually separates three things that history had repeatedly confused:

## A. Personhood

A universal civic status grounded in conscious agency, rights, obligations, and lived continuity.

This is the basis of legal dignity.

## B. Provenance

Technical/historical descriptors such as:

- baseline-development
- seeded-development
- restored continuity
- embodiment/medical classes

These may matter medically, historically, or personally.

They do **not** create ranked citizenship.

## C. Human identity

`Human` is no longer forced into one scientific/legal definition.

Individuals and communities may use it as:

- historical identity
- cultural inheritance
- biological descriptor in narrow contexts
- personal self-identification

but the state does not use “how human are you?” as a rights hierarchy.

---

# 3. Why This Pays Off the Historical Arc

The earlier Continuity Settlement solved caste by suppressing provenance distinctions.

That saved families and rights.

But destroying distinctions also destroyed knowledge.

The final society learns a different lesson:

> **Truth does not have to become hierarchy, and equality does not require pretending differences do not exist.**

This directly reinterprets the central history instead of merely condemning ancestors.

---

# 4. Sole Baseline Human Resolution

The sole BH adult receives explicit rights to:

- medical privacy
- bodily autonomy
- refuse research
- refuse reproduction
- refuse symbolic/public role
- choose whether to disclose their status personally

The existence of baseline-development capacity becomes a **civilizational capability**, not an individual obligation.

Future baseline births may occur only through ordinary voluntary reproductive/medical choice.

No one is created simply to maintain a category.

---

# 5. Protagonist Resolution

The protagonist is seeded-origin.

Their final legitimacy does not come from becoming biologically human or discovering hidden founder authority.

Their arc completes when they can design a transition that:

- acknowledges real differences
- refuses to rank people by those differences
- preserves relationships/communities that matter
- allows some inherited structures to end
- does not pretend one plan can preserve everything

This is the mature form of the Habitat Transition Officer’s competence.

---

# 6. Destination Resolution

The civilization rejects the false binary:

- completely dismantle ship and obey founder colony plan
- abandon settlement entirely and stay unchanged forever

Instead it rewrites the mission based on current people and actual target-system conditions.

Working final structure:

- some habitat/industrial mass is used for planetary settlement
- some major habitats remain intact as orbital/shipborne communities
- settlement groups choose different forms under one broader federation/compact
- communities have negotiated transition paths rather than being treated as raw mission inventory

The ship therefore changes function:

> from a vehicle carrying one civilization toward a destination

into

> one founding world among several in the new system.

---

# 7. Material Cost

The ending must not preserve everything.

At least some beloved physical structures must be dismantled or permanently transformed.

Otherwise the protagonist learns nothing about transition.

The emotional answer is not:

> nothing has to be lost.

It is:

> loss does not automatically erase continuity, and preservation does not require freezing the world.

---

# 8. Parent/Home Payoff

The protagonist’s early conflict with a parent/community over relocation becomes a miniature version of the finale.

Early belief:

> If I reproduce the function elsewhere, I preserved the life.

Later correction:

> Function is not enough; relationships, memory, consent, and place matter.

Final mature belief:

> Preservation means identifying what people themselves require for continuity—and accepting that some forms must still end.

The protagonist does not simply agree with the parent or defeat them.

The series integrates both sides.

---

# 9. Younger-Family Payoff

The protagonist’s old unauthorized classification falsification is exposed/resolved before the finale.

Final lesson:

Protecting someone without respecting their agency can still be a form of control.

In the finale, the protagonist must not repeat the same mistake at civilization scale by “saving” communities through decisions made for them.

---

# 10. Intimate-Relationship Payoff

The relationship must choose continuation based on current persons, not biological reassurance.

If the relationship continues, it continues after both understand the protagonist is artificial-origin.

Its value is therefore demonstrated rather than argued.

---

# 11. Mentor Payoff

The mentor’s strongest point survives:

> Civilization needs trustworthy procedure; pure local choice can let powerful groups abandon weaker people.

The protagonist does not destroy institutions.

They redesign them to include:

- provenance protections
- consent
- representation
- transparent transition costs

The ending rejects both blind bureaucracy and anti-institution romanticism.

---

# 12. Rival Payoff

The rival’s strongest point also survives:

> No model of continuity is legitimate if people cannot meaningfully participate in deciding what must continue.

The final transition system therefore gives communities genuine options and representation, while still respecting civilization-scale physical constraints.

---

# 13. Restoration/Death Payoff

Final personhood law does not force one metaphysical answer about RC continuity.

Instead it protects:

- RC as a person
- prior consent
- family/legal continuity through explicit rules
- the right to refuse capture/restoration

This mirrors the broader ending:

Difference in origin/state does not justify treating one conscious claimant as disposable.

---

# 14. Solar-System Payoff

No message from Sol arrives in time to validate the decision.

The civilization acts without external permission.

Potential epilogue/sequel seed:

Only after the new target-system society commits to its own structure does it begin building communication capacity capable of recontacting Sol.

The answer, if it ever arrives, cannot retroactively authorize their choice.

---

# 15. Final Image Requirement — Structural, Not Prose

The final visual concept should juxtapose:

- a ship habitat that remains alive
- material/structures from another habitat becoming part of a new settlement
- people of different developmental provenance participating without ranked civil status

This is an image concept only, not a written scene.

---

# 16. Why This Ending Is Preferred

It avoids:

- biological-human restoration as ultimate goal
- “AI replaces humanity, so humanity is over” nihilism
- everyone becoming identical
- one speech solving philosophy
- total founder obedience
- total mission rejection
- protagonist crowned leader
- the sole human reproducing to save the species

And it pays:

- transition profession
- ship geography
- family
- provenance history
- privacy
- factions
- destination
- one-human premise

---

# 17. Freeze Conditions

Before final ending freeze:

1. sole BH character designed
2. target system engineering fixed
3. macro Acts prove causal route to plural settlement
4. faction outcomes are nontrivial
5. ending obligations ledger fully seeded
6. at least one meaningful irreversible loss remains
7. web-novel climax has sufficient external action/decision pressure
8. no final solution depends on protagonist holding unique authority

Current recommendation strength: **HIGH**.
