**Primary Engine**
E6/E2.

**Secondary**
E5 System Resilience.

**External Event**
Continuity Settlement and later reproductive-medicine records show the route was deliberately preserved because making every future child dependent on one generative cognition architecture created systemic risk.

# EP130 — Preserve the Option, Not a Quota

E7 Policy.

**Secondary**
E2/E5.

**External Event**

- baseline route has different risks and worse compatibility with some modern therapies,
- seed route has monoculture/systemic dependency risks,
