# EP154 — Everything Has Mass

**Immediate Goal**
Build the first transparent integrated ledger of what the ship can preserve, dismantle, transfer, fabricate, and land during arrival.

**Primary Engine**
E5 Material Constraint.

**Secondary**
E8 Arrival / E7 Governance.

**External Event**
Tessa's team classifies civilization-scale assets into:
- non-negotiable survival mass,
- habitat structural/life-support mass,
- shielding/water/ecology reserves,
- fabrication feedstock,
- arrival propellant/transfer systems,
- medically/biologically constrained infrastructure,
- potentially convertible mission structures.

The total ship remains roughly 4×10^11 kg class; only a fraction can move between roles on the remaining timeline.

**Decision**
Tessa refuses political categories like `preserve habitat` or `settle planet` until each is translated into actual mass/energy/heat/logistics flows.

**Result**
Every faction receives the same physical accounting baseline.

**Cost**
Some politically popular promises immediately become mutually incompatible.

**Character**
C4 Tessa takes independent lead; Haren supplies social dependency inputs rather than owning the model.

**Theme**
TH-007 EQUALITY VS UNEQUAL NEED; TH-010 secondary.

**Continuity**
Tessa final-arc ambition becomes central.

# EP159 — Four Futures That Do Not Close

**Immediate Goal**
Stress-test popular high-level futures against the integrated ledger.

**Primary Engine**
E5/E8.

**Secondary**
E7.

**External Event**
Representative scenarios fail for different reasons:

1. **Move almost everyone to the surface quickly** — ecology/medical/landing/fabrication bottlenecks fail.
2. **Preserve all eight habitats unchanged near the planet** — orbital space/logistics/maintenance/settlement mass fails.
3. **Keep almost everyone shipborne and minimize settlement** — mission/long-term repair/resource risks rise and underuses a genuinely viable world.
4. **Dismantle aggressively for rapid settlement** — destroys living-world continuity and creates massive near-term transition risk.

**Decision**
The convention accepts that no single pure option is physically free.

**Result**
Hybrid/plural architecture becomes the only family of plans still alive.

**Cost**
Every surviving plan is more complex to govern and maintain.

**Theme**
TH-011 SHIP AS VEHICLE VS HOME; TH-010.

**Reward / Hook**
RWD-FACT + RWD-REVERSAL.
HK-REVERSAL.

**4-point** 4/4.

# V-B Rollup

## Material answer
No pure future closes.
A plural multi-world architecture is physically stronger than one standardized settlement template.

## Character
- Tessa leads independently
- Yuna's earlier failure causally changes planet ecology policy
- Elin adds medical/capture/refusal constraints
- Haren remains one systems contributor, not master planner

## Physical architecture
Working basis:
- H2/H4/H5 planet-linked
- H1/H3/H6/H7/H8 broader system roles
- staged surface minority

## Anti-duplication
Act-V `elegant plan fails` is about **architectural interchangeability**.
Act-VI ARC35 remains reserved for a later **live move model that is physically correct but socially incomplete**.

## Handoff
The civilization now knows what it can build.
It still has to answer whether it has the right to choose without Sol.
