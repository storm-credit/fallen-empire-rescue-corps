## Preferred core names

- protagonist: **하렌 사빈 / Haren Savin**
- mother: **마라 사빈 / Mara Savin**
- younger sister: **유나 사빈 / Yuna Savin**
- colleague: **테사 노르 / Tessa Nor**
- former partner: **엘린 베스 / Elin Vess**
- mentor: **토마 벤 / Toma Venn**
- rival: **키안 렐 / Kian Rel**
- older H8 anchor: **브람 오르 / Bram Or**
- sole baseline human: **나디아 벨로 / Nadia Velo**

## Why mentor changed

`오렌 다스` collided rhythmically with `하렌`.
`토마 다스` was too close to an ordinary present-day real-world name combination.

`토마 벤`:
- has distinct first sound
- is easy to hear in Korean
- does not rhyme with 하렌/키안
- keeps an Earth-derived/light-drift feel
- exact web searches did not surface a major fictional character collision

`Venn` does evoke the mathematical Venn-diagram surname in English, but this is not thematically loaded enough to be disqualifying. Monitor during English-localization work.

## Why BH-1 changed

`나디아 펠` exactly matched a discoverable real-person name combination.

`나디아 벨로`:
- preserves the deliberately ordinary `나디아`
- avoids Adam/Eve/Genesis symbolism
- is phonetically distinct from every core name
- exact search did not surface a major fictional-character collision

`Velo` has ordinary Romance-language associations and can plausibly survive as an Earth-derived household name.
