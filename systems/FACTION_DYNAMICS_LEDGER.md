# Faction Dynamics Ledger

Status: **ACTIVE PRE-BLUEPRINT LEDGER**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Ensure factions move autonomously across the seven Acts and never collapse into `human side vs AI side`.

Tracked fields:
- agenda,
- public move,
- gain,
- cost,
- internal fracture,
- coalition shift.

Recurring faces use names from `society/FACTION_FACE_NAME_DECISION_V1.md`.

# Cross-Faction Coalition Map by Phase

## Phase A — Pre-truth
Typical coalitions are issue-specific:
- F1 + F7 on portable household rights
- F2 vs F3 on restoration, but both oppose coercion/inequality in different ways
- F4 + F6 on reserves
- F5 + labor networks against over-sacrifice
- F8 intersects with everyone through transparency

## Phase B — Population-origin reveal
Temporary anti-caste coalition:
F1 + F3 + F7 + parts of F5.

Privacy coalition:
F2 + H7 + parts of F7.

Mission/material coalition:
F4 functionalists + F6.

No coalition is permanent.

## Phase C — Nadia identity
Cross-cutting pressure:
- privacy can unite F2, Kian-aligned localists, and some F1 lawyers
- research/mission arguments split F4/F6 internally
- F8 splits hardest

## Phase D — Arrival conversion
Main axis shifts away from ontology to:
- inherited mission,
- local consent,
- resource limits,
- restoration access,
- world distribution.

This shift is mandatory.

## Phase E — Final compact
No faction receives full victory.
The final constitutional structure is a negotiated coexistence of surviving strongest arguments.

# Autonomous-Move Requirement

Before Full Episode Blueprint freeze:

- each faction must make at least **2 consequential moves not initiated by Haren**,
- at least 5 factions must win something before the finale,
- at least 5 factions must pay a cost created by their own preferred policy,
- at least 3 coalitions must cross ideological boundaries,
- no final crisis may align all factions into one pro/anti-human binary.
