# Direction Decision

Status: **PROVISIONAL — NOT FROZEN**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

## Selected Working Architecture

Carry forward the following layered architecture for deep-design stress testing:

- **Macro engine:** ship-world expedition / district traversal
- **Decision engine:** civic continuity/personhood cases with binding consequences
- **Emotional core:** family/kinship/closest relationships
- **Periodic pressure engine:** infrastructure/environment/system crises

This is not permission to combine all engines in every episode.

## Why this combination

It is currently the only candidate that simultaneously answers the project’s major long-form risks:

1. **30-episode exhaustion** — the ship itself contains many societies and institutions.
2. **investigation monotony** — cases, relationships, politics, exploration, and crises rotate.
3. **philosophy lecture risk** — questions become decisions with costs.
4. **AI emotional distance** — family/relationship value is central before ontology labels dominate.
5. **SF exposition risk** — world rules are encountered through work and consequences.
6. **100+ episode ceiling** — the public meaning of the truth can keep changing after the factual mystery is solved.
7. **adaptation/IP weakness** — distinct ship regions and institutions create visual identity.

## Non-Negotiable Constraint

The protagonist must ultimately have **one coherent profession/social role**, not a collection of plot-authority jobs.

The role must plausibly provide:

- cross-district movement
- concrete competence
- decisions that affect people rather than merely observation
- access to family/institutional conflicts
- occasional interaction with infrastructure emergencies
- no magical security clearance

## Durability Test

The working direction must remain functional in three phases:

### Phase I — Before the “one biological human” fact is public
The protagonist’s work and relationships must already be compelling.

### Phase II — After the fact becomes socially known or partially known
The same institutions must produce new conflicts rather than collapse into one revolution plot.

### Phase III — After the protagonist’s own identity becomes known
The story must still have stakes, agency, institutions, relationships, and destination problems.

Failure in any phase means redesign.

## Protected Core

The project’s unique center remains:

> A 300,000-person civilization sincerely lives as human—through family, work, birth, medicine, death, law, religion, and memory—although biological humanity is almost absent and the sole exception cannot be safely identified.

The series is about what happens when that fact enters real relationships and institutions while the voyage still has a future to choose.

## Next Design Step

Build the **world/canon option space** without yet freezing the answers. In particular:

1. embodiment architecture
2. birth/childhood architecture
3. death/backup/restoration architecture
4. medical indistinguishability architecture
5. reason for the system
6. fate of original humans
7. destination function
8. protagonist-role candidates consistent with this working architecture

Each major world solution must be red-teamed against both SF logic and long-form narrative value before freeze.
