# 1. Ending Governance Principle

The series does **not** end with:

- one constitution everyone loves
- Haren becoming president
- all habitats culturally merging
- every philosophical dispute resolved

Preferred end state:

> **A limited common compact creates enforceable personhood/safety/mobility floors while allowing the new worlds to remain politically different.**

The compact is designed to govern **interdependence**, not erase plurality.

---

# 2. What Must Be Shared

Some systems cannot be local because one world's failure can physically harm others.

Shared compact jurisdiction covers:

### A. Personhood floor
- no civic rank based solely on developmental origin
- bodily autonomy
- due process
- equal basic legal personality for seeded, unseeded, and restored claimants

### B. Inter-world mobility floor
- no origin-based travel exclusion
- recognition of basic identity/person status across worlds

### C. System safety
- collision/navigation
- major radiation events
- epidemic/biosecurity
- shared orbital traffic
- emergency life-support assistance obligations

### D. Shared-resource agreements
Where systems remain physically coupled:
- water/volatile transfer
- ecological backup
- power/fuel reserve
- critical fabrication

### E. Archive authentication minimum
No world may falsify common mission/history records used for inter-world legal claims.

---

# 3. What Remains Local

Within the common rights floor, habitats/settlements control much of:

- education
- culture
- local data/privacy architecture
- housing
- local economic institutions
- family/care recognition details
- environmental policy
- settlement development pace
- local public services

This is not full sovereignty.

Physical interdependence places limits on local refusal.

---

# 4. Institutional Shape

Preferred structure uses **three different legitimacy sources**.

## 4.1 Population Assembly

Representation roughly proportional to citizens.

Function:
- common rights
- shared budgets
- inter-world legislation

Purpose:
Large worlds cannot be ignored merely because every settlement gets a flag.

## 4.2 World Council

Each recognized habitat/surface settlement receives representation with safeguards so tiny worlds cannot permanently dominate population majorities.

Function:
- treaties
- major system-sharing changes
- world-status / autonomy questions

Purpose:
Prevents H1 or another large population center from simply outvoting every smaller world on local survival issues.

## 4.3 Systems Stewardship Board

Technical/operational body responsible for:
- orbital safety
- radiation
- major ecological coupling
- shared reserve thresholds

Members include professional experts and rotating civic oversight.

It cannot legislate personhood or ordinary local policy.

Its emergency powers expire automatically and are reviewable.

This is the mature version of the institutions Haren/Tessa/Toma have spent the series arguing about.

---

# 5. Double-Legitimacy Rule

Major irreversible shared-system decisions require both:

- population legitimacy
- affected-world legitimacy

But no individual world gets unlimited veto if refusal creates imminent physical danger elsewhere.

This avoids both:
- pure central majoritarianism
- absolute local sovereignty

Exact voting thresholds remain open; do not bog prose down in percentages unless plot requires them.

---

# 6. Rights Are Not Based on “Human” Definition

Final law does not need to abolish the word `human`.

It separates:

### Person/citizen status
rights-bearing legal category.

### Developmental origin
medical/historical fact.

### Restoration continuity status
specific legal/medical continuity question.

### Human
cultural, biological, historical, philosophical self-description.

No `human purity score` exists.

Nadia receives no special constitutional seat.

---

# 7. Provenance / Origin Record Regime

The ending does **not** repeat the old solution of deleting origin data.

Nor does it create a universal public genealogy database.

Preferred three-layer model:

## Medical provenance
Accessible under medical need / patient consent rules.

## Historical aggregate / authenticated archive
Preserved for scholarship and public accountability.

## Identifiable family/person origin map
Protected; access requires subject consent, strong legal basis, or narrowly defined adjudication.

Distributed authenticated archives prevent one H6/H1 office from rewriting history.

This is the synthesis of F8 truth + H7 privacy, but both sides lose something.

---

# 8. Reproduction Under the Compact

No origin quota.

No requirement to preserve a baseline population.

Unseeded development is legal where medically viable and voluntarily chosen.

Founder germline access remains highly regulated because:
- it affects future children
- it can create political pressure around “restoring humanity”

Nadia has **no reproductive obligation**.

A future unseeded child is not required as the closing symbol.

---

# 9. Restoration Under the Compact

Restored claimants receive immediate legal personhood.

But continuity with prior legal identity is adjudicated domain by domain where necessary:
- property
- marriage/partnership
- professional license
- parental authority
- stored reproductive material

This disappoints both extremes:

- F2 cannot simply say restoration is never the same person
- F3 cannot demand every prior legal relationship reattach automatically

Public funding guarantees a **minimum access floor**, but capacity remains finite.

---

# 10. Mission Obligation

The old charter is formally amended/replaced for civic hierarchy.

However, the final compact retains a binding idea from Voyage Mandate:

> Current citizens may rewrite the mission, but they cannot spend all intergenerational survival reserves on themselves without accounting for those who inherit the result.

Thus some:
- ecological reserves
- safety margins
- long-term infrastructure

remain protected from ordinary short-term politics.

This is not founder worship.

It is recognition of intergenerational externality.

# 11. Durable Costs by Faction

Every major faction loses something.

## F1 Civic Continuity
Wins:
common rights/identity portability.

Loses:
full standardization; H7/local systems retain meaningful variation.

## F2 Mortality Covenant
Wins:
right to refuse capture/restoration.

Loses:
public resources still support restoration access for people who choose it.

## F3 Restoration Rights
Wins:
restored persons recognized immediately as persons.

Loses:
not every previous legal relationship/obligation returns automatically.

## F4 Voyage Mandate
Wins:
intergenerational reserve duties and arrival commitments survive.

Loses:
old biological charter hierarchy / founder instructions can be amended.

## F5 Shipborn Future
Wins:
shipborn worlds remain permanent legitimate homes.

Loses:
some habitats/material are still irreversibly committed to planetary settlement.

## F6 Stewardship Account
Wins:
material limits become transparent and binding.

Loses:
social/care dependencies receive formal weight even when not easily quantified.

## F7 Kinship Commons
Wins:
care networks gain legal/planning recognition.

Loses:
individuals retain the right to exit family/community obligations.

## F8 Provenance Circle
Wins:
history is preserved/authenticated.

Loses:
identifiable origin data is not automatically public.

# 12. Durable Costs by Habitat

## H1
Loses automatic central-capital status.

## H2
Must export ecological stock while preserving backup integrity.

## H3
Gives substantial industrial/material capacity to settlement build.

## H4
Cannot keep every family network physically together across migration.

## H5
Carries transit/logistics burdens and crowding during transition.

## H6
No longer monopolizes archive authority.

## H7
Must accept narrowly defined shared safety telemetry despite privacy culture.

## H8
Keeps the habitat but loses the Old Transfer District for real.

Every world pays something.

# 13. The Dissenting Bloc

At least one organized political wing does **not** ratify the full common compact immediately.

Preferred composition:
- some Voyage Mandate literalists
- some H7 maximal-autonomy communities

They disagree for opposite reasons, so they do not form a natural ideological party.

They remain connected through an interim:

## Safety & Mobility Treaty

They agree to:
- collision/navigation rules
- epidemic/radiation emergency cooperation
- mobility/personhood minimums for travelers
- no origin-based deprivation of basic personhood

They do **not** initially accept every common legislative institution.

This is a healthy ending feature.

The civilization can coexist with constitutional dissent without resolving it by force.

---

# 14. Limits on Dissent

Local worlds may:
- teach different history interpretations
- use different terms for human/AI
- reject some shared policy programs
- maintain stricter local data rules

They may **not** under the safety/personhood treaty:
- enslave seeded/unseeded/restored people
- deny basic personhood based on origin
- create unmanaged collision/radiation risk for others
- trap citizens by revoking all inter-world mobility rights without due process

Pluralism has a rights/safety floor.

---

# 15. Haren's Role

Haren does not write the constitution.

His late-series role is:

**temporary transition-integration coordinator**

He and a multi-disciplinary team translate political choices into:
- move sequences
- dependency maps
- material schedules
- fallback plans

He has:
- bounded authority
- co-signature / oversight
- transparent constraints
- term expiration after transition

The institutional framework must remain functional when he leaves the role.

---

# 16. Anti-Chosen-One Operational Test

At least one major Act VII operation must succeed without Haren directing the local execution.

Preferred:
A H2/H4 or SR-B transition team identifies a hidden dependency and changes sequence using the shared method while Haren is physically elsewhere.

The reader should see:

> what Haren learned has become a civilization practice, not his superpower.

---

# 17. Ratification Is Not One Climactic Vote

Avoid a single parliament scene deciding the series.

Preferred enactment sequence:

1. rights/safety floor negotiated during active transition
2. worlds provisionally adopt local transition charters
3. shared operational rules tested under real failure
4. population/world representatives ratify common compact in stages
5. dissenting blocs sign narrower treaty
6. institutions begin functioning before EP250

Politics is proven by operation, not speech alone.

# 18. Ending Social State

At EP250:

### Established
- multi-world geography
- common personhood floor
- basic mobility/safety rules
- provenance record regime
- plural settlement institutions

### Unfinished
- universal ratification
- human/AI terminology
- long-term terraforming
- exact restoration metaphysics
- whether unseeded reproduction grows
- political rivalry among worlds

The story has answered its **normative operating question** without pretending history has ended.
