# Death / Capture / Restoration System v1

Status: PROVISIONAL — NOT FROZEN
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

## 1. Core Rule

Artificial cognitive origin does **not** imply that a mature person exists as a clean software file.

The standard cognition seed creates developmental architecture.

It does not contain the adult individual.

A mature person exists as a unique, embodied, continuously changing state distributed through a living neural system.

Therefore:

> Death remains real even in a civilization that can sometimes reconstruct a continuity claimant.

---

# 2. Three Different Technologies

Do not collapse these into “backup.”

## 2.1 Developmental Seed

Purpose:
- bootstrap a new child’s cognitive development

Contains:
- architecture
- no adult memories
- no specific person

Cannot restore a dead adult.

---

## 2.2 Continuity Capture

Purpose:
- record enough of a mature person’s current neural/cognitive organization to attempt later reconstruction

Requires:
- specialized clinical interface
- time
- consent/legal authority
- large data capture
- compatibility with the person’s neural scaffold

A capture is a **snapshot/model**, not the person living in storage.

---

## 2.3 Restoration

Purpose:
- use a prior capture to reconstruct a new embodied person with strong psychological continuity to the deceased

Result:
- `Restored Continuity Claimant (RC)`

The law does not automatically answer whether RC is metaphysically the same person.

---

# 3. Capture Availability

## 3.1 Technically available, socially optional

Capture should be common enough to shape society but not universal enough to erase mortality.

Working distribution target, NOT canon numbers:

- many citizens have at least one historical capture
- regular current capture is more common in high-risk professions, wealthy/insured households, restoration-friendly cultures, and some public-service roles
- significant populations refuse capture for religious, privacy, philosophical, or economic reasons

## 3.2 Capture frequency

Working range:

- high-risk occupational plan: monthly/quarterly
- ordinary voluntary plan: annual or life-event based
- privacy/finality adherents: never

Exact cadence later.

### Why not continuous upload

Continuous high-resolution capture would:

- require intrusive neural telemetry
- destroy meaningful privacy
- create extreme network/security load
- make identity copying too easy
- weaken death

Therefore standard society does not possess or permit perfect continuous mind streaming.

---

# 4. What Capture Misses

Even a technically excellent capture does not perfectly preserve:

- post-capture memories
- instantaneous hormonal/body state
- every synaptic/molecular microstate
- bodily procedural adaptation
- evolving sensory associations
- some implicit/emotional integration

This does not mean RC wakes as an amnesiac stranger.

A good capture can produce remarkable continuity, which is precisely why the moral question remains difficult.

---

# 5. Restoration Body

## 5.1 No instant adult printer

A viable adult-compatible body/neural substrate requires significant biological fabrication and conditioning.

Possible future process:

- grow tissues/organs from compatible cells
- develop neural scaffold under accelerated but bounded conditions
- integrate captured cognitive state gradually
- sensorimotor rehabilitation
- immune/endocrine calibration

## 5.2 Time cost

Restoration should require **months to more than a year**, depending on damage, body availability, and infrastructure.

This prevents immediate death reset.

## 5.3 Resource cost

Scarce resources include:

- specialized bioreactors
- medical staff
- neural conditioning time
- high-quality biological feedstock
- compute/storage verification
- rehabilitation capacity

Thus restoration competes with other medical and civic needs.

---

# 6. Legal Status

## 6.1 Central default

A successful RC receives provisional continuity recognition if:

- capture provenance is authenticated
- no conflicting active instance exists
- identity-integrity tests pass
- the deceased had authorized restoration or applicable law permits it

## 6.2 What remains disputed

Even with recognition, law may treat some domains differently:

- marriage
- inheritance already distributed
- parental custody
- elected office
- security credentials
- debt
- criminal sentencing
- insurance
- bodily consent agreements

Different habitats/factions may push different rules.

---

# 7. Multiple Instances

## 7.1 Technical possibility

The technology should make multiple reconstructions **possible in principle**.

If it were physically impossible solely because the plot says so, readers will challenge it.

## 7.2 Legal norm

Deliberately creating concurrent claimants from one capture is heavily restricted because:

- both become autonomous persons immediately after divergence
- property/relationship obligations become impossible to assign cleanly
- creating a person merely to deny them rights is ethically unacceptable

## 7.3 Narrative consequence

Rare accidents/illegal forks can exist, but they must not become routine clone gimmicks.

Once two conscious instances exist, neither is legally disposable as “the copy.”

---

# 8. Murder Still Matters

Killing someone with a current capture is still murder because:

- the victim loses all post-capture experience
- restoration is uncertain
- restoration requires months/resources
- RC may not be accepted by loved ones or by the victim’s own prior beliefs
- a unique embodied continuity was forcibly ended

The law therefore treats “they had a backup” as irrelevant to whether lethal violence occurred.

It may affect damages/inheritance consequences, not homicide status.

---

# 9. Suicide / Right to Permanent Death

A real social conflict:

Can a prior capture be used after a person chooses death?

Potential legal tensions:

- earlier consent vs later refusal
- family petition
- mental-capacity determination
- religious rights
- state interest in scarce specialists

The Mortality Covenant’s right-to-erasure movement naturally grows from this.

---

# 10. Baseline Human Compatibility

The current BH person lacks standard seeded neural architecture.

Therefore standard continuity capture may be:

- lower fidelity
- more invasive
- medically risky
- partially incompatible

This makes the baseline person **more vulnerable to irreversible death**, not more powerful.

### Critical rule

Do not invent a perfect custom baseline backup later simply to protect a favorite character.

If technology improves, it must be seeded and earned earlier.

---

# 11. Restoration and Family

This system creates cases that cannot be answered by science alone.

Examples of question families:

- a spouse remarries during the 14 months before RC returns
- a child refuses the restored parent
- a parent wants a deceased child restored from a capture the adolescent later revoked
- inheritance has already kept another household alive
- a restored teacher remembers students who experienced years without them
- two legal systems disagree whether RC continues a marriage

These are design case families, not fixed episode plots.

---

# 12. Economic / Class Pressure

Restoration must not be a purely metaphysical luxury.

If access differs by:

- wealth
- employment
- habitat
- profession
- insurance/resource allocation

then continuity becomes class politics.

The Restoration Rights League has a concrete reason to exist.

The Stewardship faction has a concrete reason to oppose unlimited entitlement.

---

# 13. Religion / Culture

Possible positions:

- RC is the same person returning
- RC is a child/successor of the deceased
- capture itself violates dignity
- RC is legally continuous but spiritually new
- refusing available restoration is a legitimate death ritual

No one doctrine is canonically proven by technology.

---

# 14. Information Security

Continuity captures are among the most protected records on the ship.

Threats:

- theft
- unauthorized reconstruction
- manipulation
- blackmail
- political exposure

### Important limit

Do not turn the series into cyber-heist fiction.

Security issues should appear when they affect identity, family, law, or social trust.

---

# 15. Quantitative Working Targets

Not canon; use for later balance testing:

- 300,000 population
- annual deaths perhaps roughly human-demographic scale depending final lifespan
- actual restorations limited to a minority of deaths
- restoration capacity small enough that triage matters but large enough that ordinary citizens know real RC people
- body preparation + reintegration: roughly 6–24 months

Exact rates must be simulated later against population and resource budgets.

---

# 16. Hard No-Rules

Reject future design that introduces:

- perfect continuous cloud copy
- free instant restoration
- unlimited duplicates with no social consequences
- hidden “original soul bit” that scientifically proves identity
- baseline human uniquely impossible to die for plot reasons
- resurrection used to reverse emotional consequences cheaply

---

# 17. Current Verdict

This system supports:

- real death
- restoration politics
- family drama
- legal cases
- class conflict
- privacy
- religion
- murder stakes
- artificial-consciousness logic

without reducing artificial persons to disposable software.

**Status: PROVISIONAL PASS.**
