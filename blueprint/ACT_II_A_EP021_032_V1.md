This is not a baseline-development route and not an ontology mystery specimen.

The family must also move service jurisdiction during an H2 transition cycle.

Purpose:
- real pregnancy and fetal medicine,
- show cognition seed as developmental causal architecture,
- show seed does not contain personality,

Her program's resource/reset consequences later affect transition scheduling, but it does not directly endanger the pregnancy.
