# Executive Verdict

**PASS WITH TWO MANDATORY PLACEMENT CLARIFICATIONS + THREE EXECUTION GUARDRAILS.**

Act V successfully changes the series' dominant engine from ontology mystery to civilization transition.

The Act now answers:
- who can amend the mission,
- what futures physically close,
- whether Sol can decide for the ship,
- what meaningful consent requires,
- whether the mission rewrite is physically real.

No structural Act rewrite is required.

Two causal links should be made explicit in the authoritative blueprint before freeze:

1. The shared H5 cargo-hub / H2 ecological-transfer fabrication-and-thermal window must be **seeded before H5 chooses delay**, preferably EP160/168.
2. The standardized-plan failure in EP161–168 must include at least one **physical pilot or hardware/service demonstration**, not only model reviews.

These are placement clarifications, not story changes.
