## Current Working World Model

PROVISIONAL only:

- ~300,000-person generation ship
- connected rotating habitat archipelago rather than endless corridor ship
- most citizens are embodied developmental artificial consciousnesses
- they have real organic bodies, genuine infancy, development, illness, aging, love, and death
- artificial status concerns developmental origin of consciousness, not obvious robot hardware
- pregnancy and childhood are real
- medicine uses the current artificial-origin population as its normal baseline, so old biological/artificial categories are not routine clinical categories
- backup/restoration, if retained, is imperfect and does not trivialize death
- original 300,000 launch passengers were biological humans
- artificial successors were created by humans during a voyage continuity crisis
- public forgetting should result from legal/language/demographic/archival transformation, not a single mass memory wipe

## Protagonist

Four professions were compared.

Current #1 PROVISIONAL role:

**Habitat Transition Officer / 생활권 전환관·구획 이주조정관**

Role function:

When aging ship districts must be emptied, rebuilt, merged, or repurposed, the protagonist decides how to relocate households/services/communities without destroying the social systems that keep people alive.

Why it works:

- legitimate travel across habitats
- visible professional competence
- decisions with real consequences
- family/law/infrastructure interaction
- protagonist is not a detective
- classification anomalies matter because they break real relocation plans

Working inner contradiction:

- strength: sees dependency networks others miss
- false belief: fair procedure + accurate records can reconcile conflicting lives
- need: accept that personhood/value can exist before institutions have a valid category for it
- secret candidate: once falsified a classification to protect a vulnerable relationship
