## EP191–196

# EP191 — Keep It All

**Immediate Goal**
Evaluate a formal proposal to preserve H8 Old Transfer District intact while the broader H8 habitat remains in its long-term Ross 128 system role.

**Primary Engine**
E5 Material Constraint.

**Secondary**
E3 H8 / E4 Relationship / E8 Arrival.

**External Event**
After AC-3's irreversible dismantling, some H8 residents argue the civilization has already sacrificed enough nonresidential infrastructure and should preserve the district as one continuous lived heritage zone.

Unlike a fantasy preservation request, the proposal is physically possible.

It requires keeping alive:
- old pressure/isolation architecture,
- duplicate utility loops,
- obsolete thermal-control interfaces,
- low-volume legacy parts fabrication,
- maintenance access that constrains future reconfiguration,
- extra shield/structural repair reserve.

**Decision**
The new externality-aware process accepts `preserve all` as a real option and sends it to full material/social costing instead of dismissing it as nostalgia.

**Result**
Mara's strongest position receives a fair test.

**Cost / Hook**
Tessa/Sara identify that the burden is not a one-time price; it is a long-duration maintenance claim against future fabrication/spare capacity.

**Character / Relationship**
C2 Mara gains legitimate political standing beyond being Haren's mother.
R1 Haren↔Mara re-enters direct conflict under more mature rules.

**Theme**
TH-001 HOME VS FUNCTIONAL REPLACEMENT; TH-011 secondary.

**Continuity**
CM-01, CM-04, CM-05.

**Reward / Hook**
RWD-SOCIAL + RWD-FACT.
HK-PROMISE.
`REAL PRESERVATION OPTION→FULL COSTING`.

**4-point** 4/4.

# EP192 — The Spare Parts of Memory

**Immediate Goal**
Determine what full physical preservation actually requires over the first decades after arrival.

**Primary Engine**
E5 Engineering/Maintenance.

**Secondary**
E3.

**External Event / Physicalization**
Haren/Tessa/Mara/Jona inspect H8's old systems in operation:
- hand-adapted pressure doors,
- repair patches with nonstandard dimensions,
- heat exchangers kept alive by workshop-level fabrication,
- access tunnels that no modern standardized replacement can use without rebuilding adjoining structures.

The old district's resilience exists partly because generations of people know how to keep irregular systems alive.

**Decision**
The ledger models preservation as **ongoing skilled labor + fabrication + reserve**, not only retained hull mass.

**Result**
The real preservation cost becomes visible.

**Cost**
Keeping H8 intact would require H3/H8 fabrication capacity to support increasingly rare legacy components instead of destination-yard, medical, and other habitat spares.

**Character**
Mara's repair expertise proves the district is maintainable—but also proves why it depends on a shrinking human/practice base.

**Theme**
TH-001 + TH-007.

**Continuity**
CM-05 repair-tools/practice motif deepens.

**Reward / Hook**
RWD-WONDER/WORK + RWD-FACT + RWD-COMP.
HK-CONSEQUENCE.

**4-point** 4/4.

# EP193 — Who Pays for an Intact Past

**Immediate Goal**
Represent the communities and systems that would absorb the long-term cost of full H8 preservation.

**Primary Engine**
E7 Externality Governance.

**Secondary**
E5/E3.

**External Event**
The preservation package would not bankrupt the civilization, but it would predictably reduce:
- H3 large-fabrication/spare flexibility,
- destination-yard contingency inventory,
- some cross-habitat repair reserve,
- future replacement options for other aging districts.

Jona/fabrication workers and other habitats become legitimate cost-bearers in the process.

**Decision**
The hearing/process does not ask `is H8 worth it?`
It asks which external costs others must be allowed to contest.

**Result**
Full preservation remains possible but loses the illusion of being a purely local choice.

**Cost**
Mara must defend keeping the old district to people who would actually make/forgo the parts—not to an abstract budget.

**Theme**
TH-007 + TH-015.

**Continuity**
Act-V H5 externality rule pays forward.

**Reward / Hook**
RWD-SOCIAL + RWD-CONSEQ.
HK-EMOTIONAL/POLITICAL.

# EP195 — A Smaller H8 Is Not the Same H8

**Immediate Goal**
Test compromise preservation designs instead of forcing all-or-nothing choice.

**Primary Engine**
E1/E5 Transition Design.

**Secondary**
E3.

**External Event**
Alternatives include:
- preserve one contiguous heritage block,
- preserve Mara's workshop + route spine,
- reconstruct representative architecture elsewhere,
- preserve only selected industrial/ritual elements,
- full adaptive redevelopment with social-network relocation.

Each changes the district's actual lived geometry.

**Decision**
Haren refuses to call any smaller plan `equivalent` to full preservation.

The team evaluates what each option truly preserves and truly ends.

**Result**
A mixed carry-forward plan becomes technically/socially viable:
- preserve selected objects/structures only where they still serve living use,
- relocate institutions/practices,
- retire much of the old physical utility network.

**Cost**
The plan cannot preserve the original routes/workshop relationship as a whole.

**Theme**
TH-001 master payoff.

**Continuity**
CM-01 `equivalent space` returns at district scale.

**Reward / Hook**
RWD-COMP + RWD-FACT + RWD-CALLBACK.
HK-DECISION.

# VI-A Rollup

## Place-loss payoff
H8 Old Transfer District physically ends by EP202.
This is not:
- the destruction of all H8,
- proof that place never mattered,
- proof that preservationists were wrong.

## Material logic
Full preservation was feasible but created ongoing external claims on:
- fabrication,
- legacy spares,
- skilled maintenance,
- future repair flexibility.

## Community agency
H8 does not have one opinion.
The mixed plan is chosen through real internal/external claims.

## Relationship
Mara/Haren do not reach philosophical consensus.
Haren stops equating helping Mara with deciding what she should value.

## Motif/payoff
CM-01 apartment equivalence → neighborhood/world equivalence.
CM-04 old routes → new accessibility institution.
CM-05 tools/practice survive without identical workshop.
CQ-16 H8 loss fully paid.

## Handoff
Act VI-B moves from place continuity to personal continuity and the right to reject institutional preservation.
