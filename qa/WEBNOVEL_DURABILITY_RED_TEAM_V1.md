# Executive Verdict

**250-episode durability: PASS WITH ONE EARLY-PACING REVISION AND EXECUTION RULES.**

The project no longer has the classic failure mode:

> brilliant one-line twist, then nothing after 30 episodes.

The largest remaining commercial risk is the opposite:

> the design is so patient and well-reasoned that the title promise arrives later than a web-novel reader expects.

### Mandatory revision
The exact `current baseline-development human count = 1` should be confirmed **by EP005–007**, preferably EP006, rather than allowing the payoff to drift to EP009–010.

The story already tells the reader this in the title. Withholding it creates no useful suspense.

Everything after that becomes the stronger hook:

> Then what are the other 299,999 people—including the protagonist?

No other macro Act requires structural replacement.

# 1. First-Episode Attack

## Risk
A relocation professional solving dependency maps could sound like thoughtful bureaucratic SF rather than an urgent web novel.

## Required EP001 contract
EP001 must contain all four:

1. **Visible competence problem**
   - a live move/transition is failing now,
   - Haren sees a hidden dependency,
   - he changes the plan,
   - someone benefits and someone pays a cost.

2. **World wonder**
   - rotating-habitat geography / curved horizon / gravity-transfer consequence,
   - shown through work, not tour-guide exposition.

3. **Human relationship**
   - Mara/H8/Nadia cooperative or another recurring ordinary-life anchor,
   - so the ship is inhabited before it is mysterious.

4. **Anomaly promise**
   - transition closeout hits an obsolete developmental/safety dependency that should not still be active.

### Forbidden EP001 ending
A generic locked file / mysterious door / anonymous warning.

### Preferred hook family
A **real operational system refuses to let Haren complete a change because a live protected dependency exists that no current registry category explains.**

Verdict: PASS if these four are enforced.

# 10. Reward Cadence Audit

The series needs five reward currencies:

## RWD-A Competence
Haren/Tessa/Yuna/Elin/local teams solve real professional problems.

Target:
visible reward every ~2–4 episodes in early serial rhythm.

## RWD-B Factual answer
A real question receives an answer.

Target:
- title premise by EP5–7,
- seeded-development mechanism progressively answered Act II,
- historical transition substantially answered Act III,
- Nadia identity Act IV,
- mission/Sol questions Act V.

No 20+ episode answer desert.

## RWD-C Emotional movement
Trust/boundary/loss/choice changes.

Target:
at least one meaningful relationship-state movement every ~5–10 episodes on average, rotated across relationships.

## RWD-D World wonder
New physical/cultural use of ship/destination.

Target:
at least one memorable visual/social SF payoff per Sub-Act.

## RWD-E Consequence
Prior decision visibly changes later conditions.

Target:
frequent callbacks to earlier policy/material/relationship costs.

Verdict: **PASS if blueprint tags these rewards explicitly.**

# 13. AI Emotional Distance Audit

Potential market problem:
Readers may emotionally disengage if `everyone is AI` feels like characters were fake.

The design directly counters this by establishing **before the reveal**:
- actual birth,
- childhood,
- family care,
- bodily vulnerability,
- work,
- grief,
- ordinary meals,
- place attachment.

After reveal, do not immediately flood the story with abstract AI terminology.

Mandatory language rule:
Characters remain people by default in narration. Provenance terms appear when medically/historically/socially relevant.

Verdict: **STRONG PASS.**

# 14. SF Explanation Audit

Risk:
Cognition seed / restoration / orbital mechanics / material flows can overwhelm serial reading.

Rule:
No explanation enters because the world is interesting.

Every explanation requires:
`problem → failed assumption → necessary concept → decision`.

Long technical explanation must be broken by:
- choice,
- physical manipulation,
- relationship response,
- visible consequence.

Verdict: **PASS with Style Bible enforcement.**

# 15. 300-Episode Expansion Attack

Current 250-episode ending closes:
- central ontology,
- historical origin,
- protagonist identity,
- Nadia autonomy,
- destination choice,

- more settlement bureaucracy,
- another provenance secret,
- Sol finally replies,
- alien invasion.

Current recommendation:
**250 is the canonical target. Do not pad to 300.**
