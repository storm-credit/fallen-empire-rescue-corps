## Key Architecture Decision

Foreshadowing and MacGuffins are **not** the narrative architecture by themselves. They are individual tools within a larger system. The project will manage mystery, reveal, character, relationship, faction, information, theme, choice, cost, consequence, escalation, pacing, reward, callback, motif, style, narration, and anti-repetition as coordinated systems.
