# EP084 — Artificial-Origin

**Immediate Goal**
Establish the strongest public factual statement the evidence supports without inventing moral conclusions it does not support.

**Primary Engine**
E9 Public Information.

**Secondary**
E6/E2.

**External State / Major Reveal**
The authenticated synthesis establishes:

- modern standard cognition-seed architecture is a descendant of designed synthetic-person cognition technology,
- over generations it became a necessary developmental organizer for most emerging minds,
- current citizens' bodies/pregnancies/childhoods remain organic and real,
- their mature personalities were not stored in the seed,
- under old technical lineage terms, almost all current citizens are **artificial-origin developmental persons**.

**Decision**
Current civil institutions explicitly separate this historical/provenance fact from present personhood status while broader policy review begins.

**Result / Reveal**
`RV-020` pays publicly.

**Cost**
The word `artificial` has cultural meanings far beyond the technical claim, and institutions cannot control what citizens infer from it.

**Character**
Haren's EP050 fact is recontextualized: he already knew he was seeded; now he knows what that means historically.

**Theme**
TH-012 BIOLOGICAL HUMANITY VS HUMAN DESCENT; TH-013 secondary.

**Continuity**
CQ-08 activated.
CM-13 becomes available for post-reveal callback.

**Reward / Hook**
RWD-FACT + RWD-SOCIAL.
HK-CONSEQUENCE.
