# IV-C — EXACTLY ONE WAS NEVER THE RULE
## EP123–132

**This replaces the old Haren provenance-closure Arc.**

Working Arc title:
`One Is a Number, Not a Mandate`

Primary: E2 Reproduction/Personhood
Secondary: E6 History / E7 Faction / E4 Nadia family

### Core factual question
Why is there exactly one baseline-development person now?

### Answer
- Continuity Settlement preserved autonomous/non-seeded development as a reversibility and medical-resilience safeguard.
- rare contraindications can still lead families to choose that route.
- the historical count has varied: sometimes zero, occasionally one, very rarely more.
- current `1` is a demographic fact, not a hidden quota or sacred conservation program.

### Required reveal
`RV-080` / sole-human mechanism payoff.

### Present conflict
Once society understands the route can still exist, factions propose incompatible futures:
- actively preserve more baseline developmental capability,
- leave it as rare medical fallback,
- expand voluntary reproductive choice,
- restrict it as medically riskier/outdated,
- study Nadia to improve the route.

### Nadia agency requirements
- Refusal 1: rejects at least part of a genuinely beneficial research program.
- Refusal 3: rejects personal reproductive duty / sacred-line logic.
- her seeded child must not be treated as a failure of biological preservation.

### Critical rule
The series may support preserving the **option** of baseline development without claiming society must manufacture more baseline persons.

### Emotional/thematic payoff
Nadia is not the last queen of a species.
She is one current citizen produced by a rare legitimate route.
