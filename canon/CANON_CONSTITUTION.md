# CANON CONSTITUTION

Status: DRAFT — PRE-FREEZE
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the canonical categories that must be resolved before drafting. It does **not** yet declare the answers. It declares what must become canon and what cannot remain vague at the Full Prewriting Gate.

## 2. Canon Classes

### C-A. Physical Ship Canon
Must resolve:
- ship scale and habitable volume
- population capacity and current population logic
- gravity model
- propulsion and cruise state
- power generation/storage
- heat rejection
- air/water/nutrient cycles
- food production
- maintenance ecology and spare-parts economy
- radiation protection
- external repair capability
- network/data-center topology
- major catastrophic failure modes

### C-B. Embodiment Canon
Must resolve:
- what bodies AI citizens inhabit
- whether bodies are biological, synthetic-biological, mechanical, hybrid, or multiple classes
- why routine medicine does not trivially reveal human/AI status
- what medical tests can and cannot reveal
- how injuries, illness, aging, anesthesia, surgery, pregnancy, and autopsy function
- whether bodily replacement is possible and under what limits

### C-C. Consciousness Canon
Must resolve:
- substrate of artificial consciousness
- continuity rules
- copying rules
- simultaneous-instance rules
- memory persistence
- synchronization rules
- identity collision rules
- offline operation
- tamper resistance
- deletion
- corruption
- legal personhood implications

### C-D. Birth / Childhood Canon
Must resolve:
- how a child comes into existence
- what parents believe happens
- what actually happens
- inheritance of appearance/personality/talent
- whether consciousness is generated, derived, copied, recombined, or instantiated by another mechanism
- gestation or its social equivalent
- childhood development
- why family bonds are genuine rather than decorative simulation

### C-E. Death / Backup / Restoration Canon
Must resolve:
- what counts as death
- whether backups exist
- who receives them
- backup interval
- memory gap
- restoration eligibility
- cost
- social stigma
- inheritance consequences
- marriage/family consequences
- whether restoration is legally the same person
- whether restoration is metaphysically the same person under project rules

### C-F. Biological Human Canon
Must resolve:
- exact biological definition used by the story
- whether “one human” is a measurable physical fact, a legal category, a hidden system category, or combination
- why the biological human matters operationally, if at all
- what the human can do that others cannot, if anything
- what the human categorically **cannot** do
- how to prevent chosen-one power creep

### C-G. Historical Canon
Must resolve:
- launch-era civilization
- original passenger composition
- major voyage epochs
- origin of the current social order
- fate of the original biological humans
- when/why artificial citizens came to believe themselves human
- major suppressed events
- public history vs real history

### C-H. Institutional Canon
Must resolve:
- who governs the ship
- how legitimacy works
- law and jurisdiction
- identity records
- birth/death registration
- medicine
- education
- labor allocation
- property
- inheritance
- media/information control
- religion tolerance/control
- emergency authority

### C-I. Destination Canon
Must resolve:
- target system
- whether it is real
- original reason for choosing it
- current reason for continuing
- remaining travel time
- arrival protocol
- whether the ship can stop/turn/change destination
- what arrival changes socially, physically, and morally

### C-J. Protagonist Identity Canon
Must resolve before drafting:
- protagonist’s actual ontological status
- what the protagonist believes
- what evidence exists
- who knows more than the protagonist
- what can be inferred fairly by the reader
- why the protagonist is narratively central without being cosmically chosen

## 3. Canon Confidence States

Every canon claim must be tagged internally as one of:

- `OPEN`: unresolved
- `CANDIDATE`: plausible option under evaluation
- `PROVISIONAL`: selected but not stress-tested
- `FROZEN`: passed QA and binding for drafting
- `RETIRED`: rejected; may not silently return

## 4. Canon Change Rule

A `FROZEN` item may change only if all are recorded:

- old rule
- new rule
- reason
- affected mysteries
- affected characters
- affected factions
- affected episode blueprints
- affected foreshadow/payoff placements
- retcon risk
- migration action

## 5. Anti-Cheat Rules

The final canon may not rely on:

- “the scanner somehow cannot detect it” without a systemic reason
- arbitrary memory wipes used whenever the plot needs ignorance
- omnipotent AI selectively becoming stupid
- unlimited backup with no identity/social/economic consequences
- unlimited energy/storage/compute
- fake births with no convincing family/medical mechanism
- a last-minute external creator or alien that explains everything
- a simulation reveal that invalidates prior stakes
- a unique human gene/key that turns the protagonist into a superweapon or divine administrator

## 6. Freeze Condition

This document becomes `FROZEN` only after every Canon Class C-A through C-J has a concrete answer, cross-dependency audit, failure-mode audit, and compatibility check against the narrative/ending architecture.
