# 1. Ship Class

The ship is a **civilization-scale self-renewing industrial generation vessel**, not an enlarged modern spacecraft.

Working order of magnitude:

- population: ~300,000
- 8 major rotating habitats + axial/service/industrial structures
- habitat radius class: ~1.5 km
- habitat length class: ~1.6 km
- total primary inner habitat surface: ~120 km²
- nominal habitat rotation: ~0.77 rpm for ~1 g at outer living surface
- total ship mass: order `5×10^11–1×10^12 kg`

Exact values remain adjustable within this envelope.

---

# 2. Habitat Rotation Architecture

Habitats are arranged so counter-rotating pairs/groups reduce net angular momentum and simplify attitude control.

Residents normally live near ~1 g.

The axial spine remains low/zero gravity and creates a distinct labor/transit environment.

The very low ~0.77 rpm rotation rate means ordinary habitat movement does not require constant adaptation to high-rpm Coriolis effects.

---

# 3. Shielding Architecture

Radiation shielding is **mass inventory architecture**, not a thin hull coating.

Use multifunctional shielding:

- water/ice
- organic/polymer feedstocks
- food/process reserves
- soil/substrate
- low-Z composites
- equipment/material stock

Major inhabited zones target order `100–200 g/cm²` equivalent protection in critical directions, with heavier local storm shelters.

The ship does not promise Earth-surface radiation dose everywhere.

Occupancy, dose, maintenance, and shielding are jointly managed.

---

# 4. Forward Impact Architecture

At percent-c travel, interstellar dust is a strategic threat.

Mandatory systems:

- sacrificial forward shield complex
- separated bumper layers
- replaceable ice/feedstock/graphite/composite protection
- long-range particle/object detection
- active mitigation attempts for larger detectable threats
- continuous erosion measurement
- route planning around known dense regions

Forward shielding is one reason the habitats are physically far behind the leading structure.

No transparent panoramic bridge at the nose.

---

# 5. Life-Support Philosophy

The ship does not attempt one magical perfectly closed ecosystem.

It uses overlapping loops:

### Biological
- crops
- microbial systems
- fermentation
- ecological water/carbon cycling

### Physico-chemical
- water purification
- CO2 capture
- electrolysis
- chemical reduction
- trace-contaminant removal

### Industrial
- nutrient recovery
- waste processing
- chemical separation
- elemental stock management

System rule:

> Failed first-pass recovery is not automatic mass loss.

Water/nutrients remain inside the ship as brine, biomass, waste, chemical stock, or stored material until later recovery.

Routine external disposal of useful elemental inventory is culturally and economically abnormal.

---

# 6. Food Architecture

Use a mixed food system:

- intensive controlled crop canopy
- multilevel agriculture
- H2 open/cultural ecological production
- fungal/microbial protein
- fermentation
- cultured animal products where socially valued
- stored strategic reserves

Working high-intensity crop canopy equivalent:

**~10–15 km²**

This is not all visible farmland and may occupy multiple stacked levels.

---

# 7. Power Architecture

Normal civil electrical grid:

**~30–50 GW class**

High industrial/conversion peak:

**~60–100 GW class**

Primary source:
advanced fusion-class power systems.

Backup / restart:
- fission
- isotope
- distributed stored energy
- isolated habitat black-start capability

No “unlimited energy” assumption.

Power is often less limiting than:
- heat rejection
- maintenance
- material throughput
- component lifetime

---

# 8. Heat Rejection

Large external radiators are among the ship's most visible vulnerable systems.

Working effective radiator surface:

**~80–150 km² class**

Use multiple temperature loops:
- low-temperature habitat/environment loops
- medium-temperature industrial loops
- higher-temperature power/processing loops

This gives realistic system-pressure stories:
- coolant allocation
- radiator maintenance
- shadowing/orientation
- damaged segments
- conversion conflict

---

# 9. Self-Renewal

The ship is not 600-year-old hardware kept pristine.

It continuously reproduces:
- structural members
- pumps
- electronics
- sensors
- vehicles
- pressure seals
- machine tools
- chemical catalysts
- medical equipment

It carries industrial chains capable of remaking most ordinary ship systems from recycled feedstock.

Some highly specialized components require:
- long reserve inventories
- rare-material recovery
- dedicated fabrication lines

This creates maintenance debt and historical layers.

# 10. Voyage Profile

Preferred distance class:

**~10–15 light-years**

Preferred cruise peak:

**~0.02c class**

Preferred total voyage:

**~600 years class**

Illustrative profile:
- several decades departure/acceleration
- ~450–480 years fast coast
- ~90–110 years prolonged braking/approach

Story start:
- roughly 10–20 years before arrival
- braking already far advanced
- irreversible settlement-preparation threshold only ~2–4 years away

This is the canonical narrative shape even if exact mission numbers change.

---

# 11. Launch Propulsion

The launch civilization uses external Solar-System infrastructure for a large share of initial acceleration.

Preferred technology family:
- beamed particles / pellets
- optical/particle momentum transfer
- distributed external acceleration infrastructure

This is a **vast extrapolation** from current probe/ton-scale advanced concepts.

The ship should be remembered historically as one of the largest infrastructure undertakings ever attempted by its origin civilization.

---

# 12. Onboard Propulsion / Braking

Onboard interstellar propulsion is separate from the civil electrical grid.

Preferred broad family:
- advanced direct-exhaust fusion propulsion
- enormous stored propellant/fusion-fuel inventory
- magnetic/plasma-sail drag assisting long braking

Working mission-reserve envelope:

~15–25% of departure mass may be tied to:
- propulsion propellant
- braking systems
- forward shielding consumption
- related reserves

Exact mass ratio remains OPEN until full propulsion trade study.

Hard rule:

The ship does not brake from 0.02c in the last few months.

Braking is a century-scale phase.

---

# 13. Destination Observation

Because the ship is deep into approach by the story period:

- target-system astronomy is extremely high quality
- planets/moons/atmospheres are directly characterized far better than launch-era data
- arrival plans are updated repeatedly
- destination uncertainty comes from complex environment and choices, not “we don't know whether the planet exists”

Forward observation is operational infrastructure.

---

# 14. Sol Communication

At 10–15 ly, electromagnetic communication remains physically possible but delayed.

Canon working answer:

- early voyage had authenticated mission communications
- the original centralized mission authority later fragmented/dissolved
- long-term authenticated command-channel continuity ended
- Sol civilization diversified politically and biologically/technologically
- later received transmissions cannot act as a simple unitary legal command

The ship can transmit.

It cannot receive a timely, universally legitimate answer to its final constitutional choice.

---

# 15. Settlement Cannibalization

The original mission was designed around the ship becoming settlement infrastructure.

Major components were intentionally designed for eventual reuse:

- structural stock
- industrial plants
- water/volatile reserves
- habitat ecological systems
- reactors
- fabrication equipment
- transport systems

Therefore “dismantling the ship” is not an accidental emergency twist. It is an old mission assumption that current shipborn society has inherited.

This directly creates the late-series conflict.

---

# 16. Numerical Story Rules

Writers/designers must remember:

- air is mass
- water is strategic mass
- heat must leave through radiators
- nothing discarded casually can be replaced from Earth
- moving hundreds of thousands of people requires years, not montage magic
- settlement equipment consumes material currently doing another job
- propulsion energy is not equivalent to household electrical power
- every old habitat is maintained by real labor and industrial supply chains

---

# 17. Reader-Facing Detail Rule

Most exact numbers remain in the Bible.

- scale

- consequence

Use exact figures when a decision depends on them.

No chapter-length propulsion lecture.
