# Sole Baseline Human Mechanism v2

Status: **PROVISIONAL PREFERRED MECHANISM — NOT FROZEN**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

## 1. Design Change

v1 considered a legacy protocol that might intentionally maintain one baseline person.

Red-team risk:

- chosen-one implication
- secret experiment implication
- ethically dubious reproduction without consent
- weak scientific value of “one reference specimen”

v2 removes the need for a program whose purpose is to **keep exactly one person alive**.

The current count of one becomes an emergent demographic fact.

---

# 2. Core Mechanism

## The Reversibility / Autonomous Development Safeguard

During the Continuity Settlement, society abolishes provenance caste but refuses to make standard seeded cognition **irreversible as the only possible way a person can ever develop**.

Therefore medicine preserves:

- baseline-compatible neurodevelopmental repair techniques
- genomic/developmental reference libraries
- an approved pathway for **unseeded autonomous neural development**

The purpose is not to produce rulers, specimens, or “true humans.”

It exists as:

- medical redundancy
- scientific humility
- reproductive autonomy
- protection against catastrophic failure of the cognition-seed platform

---

# 3. Why the Protocol Still Exists

A civilization that makes every child dependent on one cognition architecture creates a monoculture risk.

If the standard seed platform develops:

- systemic defect
- pathogen/vulnerability
- incompatibility with an individual fetus
- manufacturing failure
- unknown destination-environment interaction

there must be another viable developmental route.

Thus preserving unseeded development is analogous to maintaining:

- seed banks
- backup operating architectures
- genetic diversity
- black-start systems

not preserving a royal bloodline.

---

# 4. How a Modern Baseline Person Is Born

## Standard route

Most pregnancies use the modern cognition-seed pathway.

## Rare contraindication

Extremely rarely, fetal/genetic/immune/developmental conditions make standard seed integration unusually dangerous or nonviable.

Modern medicine then offers an old but legitimate **autonomous-development rescue protocol**.

### Consent

Parents/guardians are told:

- the route is rare
- it carries different medical risks
- the child will lack standard neural-regenerative compatibility
- long-term monitoring is required

They are **not** told “this creates a true human” because modern medicine does not use that historical ontology.

They consent to a medical developmental alternative, not to a secret experiment.

---

# 5. What the Rescue Protocol Does

It does **not** insert preserved ancient personality or awaken a frozen embryo.

It supports the fetus’s own endogenous human-derived neural development without the generative cognition seed.

Depending on final genomic design, it may use:

- gene repair
- developmental signaling correction
- scaffold removal/avoidance
- supportive organ/neural therapy

but the resulting cognitive architecture emerges through unseeded biological neurodevelopment.

This is the key historical definition of BH.

---

# 6. Why Exactly One Exists Now

There is no sacred rule that there must always be exactly one.

The historical count can vary across generations:

- sometimes zero
- occasionally one
- extremely rarely more than one

**At the present moment, exactly one living citizen has completed the unseeded autonomous-development route.**

This is statistically rare but not cosmically ordained.

The title describes the current fact, not an eternal law.

This strongly reduces chosen-one gravity.

---

# 7. Why the Ship Knows the Count

Certain medical/infrastructure systems must track developmental compatibility because BH and SP citizens can respond differently to:

- neural-regenerative interfaces
- some cognitive state-capture technologies
- specific drugs/procedures
- long-term tissue maintenance

Therefore an old compatibility class still exists in technical records.

Modern labels may describe it as something like:

- autonomous developmental architecture
- legacy neural compatibility class
- nonseeded profile

Final terminology OPEN.

A legacy system can therefore truthfully report:

`CURRENT ACTIVE POPULATION — CLASS X: 1`

without containing a dramatic database field labeled `REAL HUMAN: 1`.

---

# 8. Why Doctors Have Not Announced the Truth

Because the medical fact is not hidden.

A small number of treating specialists may know:

> One citizen has an extremely rare unseeded developmental profile.

They interpret this as a medical condition/variant.

The missing knowledge is the transition-era historical mapping:

> This developmental profile is what old technical literature meant by baseline biological human cognition, while modern seeded development was once classified as artificial cognitive lineage.

Thus the mystery requires **cross-domain synthesis**:

- medicine
- old law
- historical terminology
- population systems
- transition records

No conspiracy of doctors is required.

---

# 9. Why the Baseline Person Is Not Superior

Likely disadvantages:

- poorer compatibility with modern neural-regenerative treatment
- inability or difficulty using some continuity state-capture systems
- higher risk from diseases or biological wear mitigated in SP physiology
- less medical data because the profile is extremely rare

Possible advantages should be narrow and incidental, not power fantasy:

- different response to a specific pathogen/therapy
- useful comparative medical data
- resilience to a vulnerability specific to the cognition-seed architecture

Any advantage must come with equal or greater limitations and must never become a superpower.

# 10. Identity Mystery Candidates Under v2

## Candidate A — protagonist

The protagonist has always known they possess a rare legacy medical profile but never knew its historical meaning.

Strength:
- fair clues
- personal recontextualization

Risk:
- chosen protagonist gravity remains high

## Candidate B — unrelated ordinary citizen

The protagonist discovers the category through work and eventually has to protect a stranger whose life society wants to turn into a symbol.

Strength:
- strongest anti-chosen structure
- protagonist matters through choices/competence

Risk:
- requires strong separate protagonist identity arc

## Candidate C — close relation

Makes privacy/family stakes immediate.

Risk:
- can feel engineered for melodrama

### Current preference

**Candidate B receives strongest design preference.**

Protagonist remains uncertain for a meaningful period because the evidence required to identify the current Class-X person is protected by medical privacy.

The protagonist does not have the right to simply search everyone’s medical record.

---

# 11. Why This Is Distinct from `Star Child`

The baseline person is not:

- recreated by machine guardians to restore extinct humanity
- raised knowingly as humanity’s heir
- a preserved ancient personality
- the goal of the ship’s machine civilization

They are a contemporary child/person born through a rare but legitimate medical branch inside a society of ordinary families.

The narrative focus is what the society does **after reinterpreting that medical category**, not raising the last human.

---

# 12. New Ethical Question

v2 creates a stronger question than “why did they create one human?”

> Once society learns that unseeded biological-human development is still technically possible, **should it deliberately create more?**

Possible positions:

- yes, preserve biological diversity
- yes, but only through voluntary parental choice
- no, creating children to preserve a category instrumentalizes them
- no, baseline biology is medically riskier
- preserve capability but do not create people for symbolic reasons

This becomes a late-series reproductive-rights conflict without making the current sole person reproductively obligated.

---

# 13. Red-Team Verdict

Compared with the v1 maintained-specimen model:

- chosen-one risk: sharply reduced
- conspiracy burden: reduced
- medical detection logic: improved
- parental consent: improved
- exact-one logic: plausible as current demographic state
- thematic value: improved
- Star Child overlap: reduced

Remaining technical question:

Can current engineered human-derived embryos retain or recover a fully viable unseeded developmental route without the cognition seed?

This is future biotechnology and cannot be validated with present science; final canon must keep the mechanism limited, costly, and medically exceptional.

**Current verdict: PASS PROVISIONALLY.**
