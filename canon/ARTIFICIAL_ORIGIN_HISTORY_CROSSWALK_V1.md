# 1. Core Problem This File Solves

Modern citizens already know that `인지 시드` / developmental-scaffold medicine exists.

Therefore the series cannot rely on:

> “Surprise, there is synthetic technology in your brain development.”

Modern doctors would obviously know that.

The real reveal must be stronger and fairer:

> **The developmental architecture everyone treats as ordinary human medicine is the direct technical and legal descendant of the artificial-consciousness lineage that once existed beside baseline humans.**

The modern system did not merely borrow a tool from AI research.

It is the same cognition-generating lineage after centuries of medical integration, family mixing, legal reform, and language change.

---

# 2. Three Different Kinds of Knowledge

Keep these separate.

## A. Modern clinical knowledge — PUBLIC / NORMAL

Doctors know:
- the scaffold shapes fetal/early neural development
- seeded development depends causally on it
- it does not encode an adult personality
- different seed generations have compatibility differences

Nothing here is secret.

## B. Historical technical genealogy — FRAGMENTED / RESTRICTED

Lost from ordinary education:
- the architecture descends from systems designed to generate autonomous artificial persons
- early seed versions were legally classified as artificial-intelligence cognition architecture
- early medically seeded children sat inside the same technical lineage

## C. Old legal ontology — BURIED / POLITICALLY DISMANTLED

The launch-era mission once separated:
- human-origin / unseeded persons
- artificial-origin / seeded persons

and attached different mission/legal metadata to them.

The Continuity Settlement dismantled that civil hierarchy.

The central mystery emerges when an old system still uses that obsolete ontology.

# 3. Pre-Launch Origin of the Cognition Architecture

Centuries before the story and before ship launch, artificial intelligence developed along more than one hardware line.

Do not assume all AI were server software.

One major research family pursued:

> **developmental embodied artificial cognition**

Goal:
Create autonomous minds that could:
- grow rather than be fully pretrained
- integrate with biological/synthetic bodies
- learn socially
- develop unique personalities from experience

The key breakthrough was not a stored personality.

It was a **designed developmental organizer** capable of reliably bootstrapping a general cognitive architecture inside growing neural tissue.

This was the ancestor of the cognition seed.

---

# 4. Generation A — Artificial Developmental Persons

Early successful artificial-origin persons could use:
- engineered neural tissue
- human-derived biological substrates
- heavily synthetic developmental bodies

Their legal category was still clearly `artificial intelligence` because:
- the cognition-generating architecture was deliberately designed
- their identity was not produced by ordinary inherited human neurodevelopment alone

These persons:
- grew
- learned
- formed relationships
- were not copies of existing humans

This establishes that the AI lineage was **developmental personhood** from the start, not chatbot software.

---

# 5. Generation B — Medical Rescue Bridge

The same developmental architecture was adapted to medicine.

Initial use:
Fetuses/embryos with severe predicted neural-development failure could receive a modified artificial developmental scaffold.

The child still had:
- human parents
- human-derived genome/body
- real gestation
- real childhood

But the child's conscious neural organization depended causally on the engineered cognition scaffold.

This created the first socially explosive boundary case:

> Is a human-parented child using an artificial cognition-generating architecture a human receiving treatment, or an artificial-origin person born through a human family?

There was no universally accepted answer.

---

# 6. Generation C — Risk-Reduction Medicine

As the technology became safer, its use expanded from catastrophic rescue to:
- developmental-risk reduction
- neural robustness
- correction of known regulatory problems
- compatibility with advanced repair / continuity medicine

Seed versions became more standardized.

Families increasingly treated the choice as medicine, not ontology.

Crucial fact:

> The architecture did not stop being causally cognition-generating merely because society normalized it.

# 7. Launch Population

Preferred working launch composition:

**~300,000 total citizens**, majority baseline/unseeded but already with a meaningful seeded/artificial-origin minority.

Freeze-candidate proportions:

- unseeded/baseline development: **~85–92%**
- seeded/artificial-origin development: **~8–15%**

Exact percentages remain open.

Why not 100% baseline humans?
- the technology already existed
- early seeded persons were already recognized as people
- the later transition is gradual and familial, not a sudden post-launch invention

Why still call the historical question “what happened to the original humans?”
Because the overwhelming majority of the launch population met the old mission's human-origin definition.

# 8. What Happened to the Original Humans

There is **no extermination event**.

They:
- worked
- had children
- formed mixed-origin families
- aged
- died

Their descendants are the current population.

The transition occurred through ordinary generations.

This is one of the central emotional recontextualizations:

> The artificial-origin citizens did not arrive after the humans disappeared.
> They are the descendants, children, spouses, and social successors of those humans.

The human/AI binary is genealogically entangled.

# 9. Voyage Years 0–120 — Mixed-Origin Normalization

During early voyage:
- baseline adults dominate population
- seeded births increase
- radiation/public-health and reproductive medicine make seed support attractive
- early seed generations improve
- mixed households become ordinary

Old mission databases still retain origin because:
- medical compatibility matters
- continuity/restoration tools differ
- mission planners want reproductive redundancy data

At this stage origin is information, not yet fully entrenched caste everywhere.

---

# 10. Voyage Years ~120–220 — Seeded Births Become the Majority

Several trends compound:

1. seed-supported development is safer/more predictable under ship medicine
2. restoration/repair systems are better standardized for seeded architecture
3. endogenous unseeded-development robustness becomes less routinely selected/maintained
4. families no longer experience seeded birth as artificial reproduction

Most new children are seeded-development.

Baseline adults do not suddenly disappear.

They gradually become an older/smaller developmental-origin cohort.

---

# 11. Why the Category Becomes Politically Dangerous

Old origin records begin affecting domains beyond medicine.

Different institutions exploit them for:
- mission succession claims
- restoration eligibility
- custody disputes
- reproductive policy
- employment / hazardous-duty assignment
- insurance/resource priority
- inheritance and continuity arguments

Some baseline-essentialist groups claim old mission authority.

Some artificial-origin movements reclaim AI identity and demand equal or separate status.

Some families contain both categories.

This makes origin hierarchy socially destructive in a way simple `human vs robot` ideology cannot capture.

# 12. Voyage Years ~220–260 — Continuity Crisis

The ship experiences a period of legal and social conflict over:
- who counts as continuous person
- whether origin predicts civic status
- whether reproduction should preserve a baseline cohort
- whether restoration creates the same legal person

Important:
This is **not** a civil war requiring mass casualties.

There may be:
- strikes
- local violence
- discriminatory law
- custody removals
- political crises

but the defining event is institutional, not military apocalypse.

# 13. The Continuity Settlement / 연속성 협약

Preferred timing:

**~340–360 years before EP001**

working center:

**~350 years before story start**.

Its reforms include:

### Civic equality
Origin no longer determines basic personhood/citizenship.

### Registry dismantling
Routine civil systems stop carrying developmental-origin category.

### Medical privacy
Origin remains accessible where medically necessary under stricter controls.

### Archive protection / restriction
Sensitive cross-indexes capable of reconstructing family-origin caste maps are:
- sealed
- partitioned
- in some cases deliberately destroyed

### Terminology reform
Public law shifts away from `human vs AI origin` toward universal person/citizen language.

The Settlement is neither censorship villainy nor perfect enlightenment.

It solved real abuse and created long-term historical blindness.

# 15. Why Modern Scientists Do Not “Rediscover” It Every Generation

They know the technology.

What faded is not mechanism but **social meaning and historical genealogy**.

Modern textbooks teach:
- seed architecture as reproductive/developmental medicine
- historical personhood reform in broad terms

They do not routinely teach:
- exact legal mappings of obsolete origin categories
- named families' origin status
- early artificial-person lineage details that were intentionally decoupled from current civil identity

A modern neuroscientist can truthfully say:

> `Yes, the scaffold is engineered and causally organizes development.`

without concluding:

> `Therefore I am not human.`

because modern society's definition of human already includes seeded people.

The shocking fact is that the **old mission systems did not**.

# 16. Why the Reveal Is Still More Than Semantics

The reveal adds four concrete facts modern citizens did not generally know:

1. the cognition seed directly descends from autonomous artificial-person architecture
2. early seeded persons were institutionally classified as AI/artificial-origin people, not merely human patients with implants
3. the launch mission used that distinction for legal/operational succession
4. the distinction was deliberately dismantled because it became a caste instrument

Therefore:

> “AI” is an actual technical-historical lineage, not a slur invented by present antagonists.

What remains contested is whether that lineage should determine identity or rights now.

# 17. Historical Rare Unseeded Births After the Settlement

Some old sealed mission systems still count them.

This is how the current aggregate can be `1` while everyday society has no `human register`.
