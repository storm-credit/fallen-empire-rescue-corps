# Destination Decision v2

Status: **PREFERRED PROVISIONAL — NOT FROZEN**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》
Astronomy check: 2026-08-17

## Requirements

Target should:
- be ~10–15 light-years away
- fit ~0.02c / ~600-year mission envelope
- have a credible terrestrial destination
- not be an easy Earth clone
- create settlement engineering choices before arrival
- require no alien-civilization plot
- keep orbital/shipborne life viable

## Candidate A — Ross 128 b

NASA current catalog/coverage:
- ~11 light-years away
- M-type host
- ~1.4 Earth masses
- estimated radius ~1.11 Earth
- orbit ~0.0496 AU
- period ~9.9 days
- host is relatively quiet/inactive compared with Proxima Centauri
- likely close enough for synchronous/tidally locked rotation

Approximate gravity from current mass/radius estimates:
`1.4 / 1.11^2 ≈ 1.14 g`.

Strengths:
- almost ideal distance for current voyage arithmetic
- real known nearby planet
- red star / likely tidal lock gives strong visual identity
- ~1.1 g is meaningful but manageable
- quiet host reduces need for constant flare-disaster plotting

Risks:
- atmosphere currently unknown
- later real observations could date the fiction

Score: **45/50**.

Score: **43/50**.

Score: **44.5/50**.

## Tau Ceti note

Do not currently anchor the story to Tau Ceti e/f as established destinations. NASA's 2026 science coverage notes that recent follow-up work indicates several 2017 planet signals may be stellar/data-analysis artifacts.

A future-fiction planet around Tau Ceti is possible, but offers no major advantage here.

# Fictional Future Environment — Working Candidate

These details are fiction, not present-day astronomical claims.

- gravity: ~1.1–1.15 g
- rotation: synchronous or near-synchronous
- atmosphere: substantial but not directly breathable
- broad chemistry: N2/CO2-dominated working direction, oxygen below safe breathing range
- significant water inventory
- atmospheric/ocean transport creates a broad temperate region rather than a razor-thin twilight strip
- no complex native biosphere as a series engine
- initial settlement requires enclosed/partially enclosed habitats and atmospheric/ecological processing
- orbital/ship habitats remain competitive and desirable

# Seeded vs Unseeded Adaptation

Do not make seeded people superhuman.

Permissible difference:
seeded neural architecture is more compatible with standardized neurovestibular adaptation/repair protocols; BH-1 is less predictable under those tools.

Both developmental types can settle with support.

# Destination Story Rule

The destination is real.

Forbidden late twists:
- planet never existed
- destination is simulation
- hidden aliens built it
- founders knowingly sent everyone to a fake world

Late conflict is about **how to inhabit the real destination and what the current civilization is willing to transform**, not whether the destination was a lie.
