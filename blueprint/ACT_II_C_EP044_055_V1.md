# ACT II-C Episode Blueprint v1 — EP044–055

Status: **BLUEPRINT DRAFT**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》
Act: II — WHAT ARE WE?
Sub-Act: II-C — MY BIRTH WAS NORMAL

No manuscript prose.

Primary purpose:
- show how incomplete truth harms an ordinary seeded citizen,
- give Haren a fair three-family evidence chain about his own developmental route,
- effectively end repeated `maybe Haren is the one` suspense,
- strengthen Haren/Mara/Yuna/Elin relationships without using them as ontological proof,
- turn restoration anxiety into an arrival-resource decision,
- hand Act III a clear historical question.

Important distinction:
By this Sub-Act's end Haren is **operationally and medically established as standard seeded-development and therefore not the current baseline-route person**.

What remains unresolved is the **historical meaning** of standard seeded cognition as an artificial-origin lineage. That is Act III's job.

# EP044 — The Wrong Candidate

**Immediate Goal**
Protect an ordinary citizen's housing/work transition from collapsing after niche media/professional rumor falsely identifies them as a possible `one baseline human` candidate.

**Primary Engine**
E9 Public Narrative / Information Cascade.

**Secondary**
E1 Transition / E2 Status.

**External Event**
Fragments from the Act-I discovery have spread without full verification or technical context.

Amateur candidate criteria circulate:
- rare neural-treatment incompatibility,
- unusual old clinic codes,
- incomplete continuity-capture compatibility,
- family medical rumors.

A seeded citizen with an unusual but unrelated early-generation scaffold profile is falsely labeled as a possible baseline candidate.

Consequences hit now:
- coworkers hesitate over placement,
- housing transition attracts unwanted attention,
- a clinic receives improper record requests,
- the citizen's family becomes public evidence material.

**Decision / Action**
Haren treats this as a present transition harm, not an opportunity to solve the central mystery.

He freezes only decisions contaminated by unverified provenance assumptions and asks Elin/medical privacy staff for a legally minimal compatibility clarification.

**Result**
The person's move/work status is temporarily protected.

**Cost**
The intervention itself increases public interest; `why are they protecting this person?` becomes another rumor vector.

**Character / Relationship**
C1 Haren; C5 Elin.
R4: Elin supports protecting the patient/citizen but limits what Haren may know.

**Mystery / Info**
M-100 pressure begins without new real clue to Nadia.
INF-005 moves toward rumor/public speculation, not verified public truth.
CQ-05 begins.

**Theme**
TH-008 PRIVACY VS PUBLIC CONSEQUENCE; TH-013 secondary.

**Reward / Hook**

# EP054 — Capacity for the Living and the Returned

**Immediate Goal**
Choose a limited continuity-infrastructure package that does not silently sacrifice arrival readiness or current access.

**Primary Engine**
E5.

**Secondary**
E7/E8.

**External Event**
The options show no cost-free path:
- full present expansion weakens arrival reserves,
- preserve all arrival reserves keeps current restoration inequity/capacity bottlenecks,

A staged package is chosen:
- improve distributed capture reliability/access first,

- seeded pregnancy is real,
- cognition seed is developmental scaffold, not stored person,
- personality/life emerge through development and experience,
- death is real despite capture/restoration,
- restoration produces a real person without rewinding the world,
- his own birth used the standard seeded route,
- modern society legally treats seeded citizens as ordinary persons.

Yet the old systems still used a sharply different `human-origin` taxonomy.

**Decision**
Haren/Toma formalize a narrow historical/technical review focused on current operational consequences of legacy classifications rather than a hunt for the one person's identity.

- old medical standards,
- infrastructure compatibility history,

Act III has a legitimate present-tense reason to enter civilizational archaeology.

The answer can no longer be `we simply forgot a medical term`.

# II-C Rollup

## Factual payoffs
- rare medical traits do not identify baseline origin
- Haren's own three-family evidence chain shows standard seeded development
- Haren is no longer a serious baseline-candidate suspense engine
- modern seeded citizenship remains legally ordinary
- current restoration demand cannot be expanded without material/arrival cost

## Haren evidence chain
- CL-100C soft seed EP009, matured EP049
- CL-100A prenatal record EP047
- CL-100B multi-source medical/compatibility evidence EP048
- functional payoff EP050

No single magic test.

## Relationship movement
- Mara: knowledge rises, friction remains
- Yuna: refuses reassurance role; adult reciprocity grows
- Elin: professional/intimate boundary gains honesty
- Toma: scoped historical review, not omniscient mentor

## Anti-chosen rule
After EP050:
Do not use Haren's possible baseline status as a recurring hook.

Act IV may contain formal/historical classification consequences, but not a renewed `maybe he is the one` guessing game.

## Act III handoff
The live question is now:

> How did standard human developmental medicine become the descendant of a category old systems treated as a different cognitive origin—and why did society deliberately stop using that distinction in ordinary civil life?
