# 0. V2 Mandatory Changes

1. Title premise `baseline human count = 1` is confirmed by **EP007–010**, not EP18–20.
2. EP011 onward spends the premise rather than withholding it.
3. Protagonist is treated as a **field-operational transition specialist**, not desk bureaucrat.
4. Every Act requires visible physical SF / wonder, not only policy and dialogue.
5. ACT III historical material must solve/complicate present-tense problems.
6. Arrival/settlement pressure is seeded from ACT I.
7. Law is only one conflict container among medical, family, labor, resource, emergency, political, and physical action.
8. BH-1 receives independent agency requirements.
9. Post-EP90 story explicitly changes genre pressure from ontology mystery to civilization transition.
10. 250 remains the intended complete story; 300 requires a new earned post-arrival engine.

# 2. ACT I — ONE
## EP001–020

### Reader contract
By EP010 the title is fact, not tease.

### Protagonist function
A field transition specialist who:
- enters neighborhoods and infrastructure under change
- maps hidden dependencies
- coordinates engineers/clinics/schools/transit
- makes limited binding decisions
- stays responsible during execution

They do not merely recommend from an office.

### Opening belief
> Accurate records + fair procedure can preserve continuity during change.

### ACT I wonder/action quota
Must include:
- a visible rotating-habitat horizon or engineered landscape
- a gravity transition through the axial spine
- one live relocation/system execution under time pressure
- one glimpse of destination culture as ordinary background (school/calendar/reserve/training)

### EP001–004

Primary: E1 Transition
Secondary: E4 Relationship / E3 Culture

External event:
A routine district transition is about to separate an invisible care/work/school network even though every household has been assigned “equivalent” replacement space.

EP001 requirements:
- immediate field problem
- protagonist makes a consequential choice
- visible ship-world geography

- no lore opening

## I-B — The Number Is One
### EP005–010

Primary: E6 Infrastructure Archaeology
Secondary: E5 System Constraint / E1 Transition

Why protagonist follows it:
The transition cannot close because the legacy dependency controls a real safety/medical routing rule.

Evidence families:
1. infrastructure protocol
2. medical/developmental terminology
3. protected population aggregate

False explanations:
- founder medical cohort
- corrupted legacy register
- obsolete radiation-risk class

Physical pressure:
A scheduled shutdown/transfer window means the field cannot remain an academic puzzle.

Major payoff by EP009–010:
Independent systems agree:

> current baseline-development biological-human population = **1**

The protagonist is not told who.

Immediate consequence:
The classification's detail layer is privacy-restricted while aggregate/system dependencies remain verifiable.

Hook is no longer “is the title true?”

New question:
> Then what category describes everyone else—including me?

## II-A — A Child Is Not Installed
### EP021–032

Primary: E2 Personhood/Family Claim
Secondary: E3 Culture / E4 Relationship

Present event:
A pregnancy/child-development case intersects with legacy pathway compatibility during a transition.

Concrete factual payoff:
- pregnancy and fetal development are real
- child identity is not copied from a template
- cognition seed provides engineered developmental scaffolding/bootstrapping
- personality emerges through development, body, environment, memory, relationships

## II-B — A Death That Cannot Be Undone by Naming It Backup

## II-C — My Birth Was Normal
### EP044–055

Primary: E4 Relationship
Secondary: E6 History / E9 Information

Public context:
Rumors about the “one” spread, but interpretations are inaccurate and fragmented.

Protagonist action:
Uses lawful medical/family channels to understand their own developmental history.

Payoff:
Their birth clearly used standard cognition-seeded architecture.

This does **not** yet settle every historical ontology term, but it substantially reduces chosen-one ambiguity.

The protagonist may still wonder whether unusual exceptions exist, but the narrative does not repeatedly tease “maybe I am secretly the one.”

Relationship cost:
An intimate/family relationship refuses to function as proof that protagonist is “real.”

Arrival seed:
A small dispute over restoration capacity is sharpened because future settlement continuity systems also require storage/energy/material reserves.

ACT-end question:
> Why did a civilization descended from biological humans stop recording the distinction that created us?

## III-A — Mixed Families Were Ordinary
### EP056–068

Primary: E6 History
Secondary: E1 Transition / E5 System

Present problem:
An old utility/medical/environmental layer cannot be safely modified because it was designed for a mixed developmental population with requirements current manuals no longer describe correctly.

Physical stakes:
A live transition or service interruption depends on recovering the old assumption.

Historical payoff:
Baseline and seeded-origin people lived together for generations as parents, children, spouses, coworkers, rivals.

False theory defeated:
No one-event “AI extermination/replacement.”

Cultural recontextualization:
Current rituals/kinship customs turn out to be descendants of mixed-origin coexistence.

Secondary: E9 Information / E6 History

# 5. ACT IV — THE ONE PERSON

### Purpose
Spend the identity mystery early enough to get the richer aftermath.

### BH-1 working rule
Unrelated ordinary adult citizen.
Not a protagonist relative, ruler, hidden scientist, founder heir, or savior.

### BH-1 agency quota across ACT IV–VII
Must have:
- 3 goals unrelated to baseline status
- 3 decisions frustrating would-be allies/protectors/users
- 2 important relationships independent of protagonist
- 1 professional competence payoff
- 1 consequential mistake
- 1 future preference not framed as “saving humanity”

### ACT IV wonder/action quota
- privacy/deanonymization problem tied to real movement/medical infrastructure
- different-body medical handling without superhuman spectacle
- at least one habitat revisit where social meaning has visibly changed after public truth
