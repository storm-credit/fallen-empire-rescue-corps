# EP170 — Voyage Year 230

**Immediate Goal**
Identify the last transmission that satisfies the old mission's full authentication/authority requirements.

**Primary Engine**
E6 Communication History.

**Secondary**
E8.

**External Event**
The archive shows the final unambiguous parent-mission message arriving around voyage year 230.

Its content is deliberately ordinary rather than apocalyptic:
- science updates,
- mission acknowledgments,
- governance contacts,
- changing institutional names/procedures,
- evidence that Sol's political structure was already evolving.

Later transmissions lack one or more of:
- recognized legal continuity,
- old mission signature chain,
- shared protocol authority,
- institutional recognition aboard ship.

**Decision**
The convention treats year-230 material as the last unquestioned **parent mission authority**, not the last message from Sol.

**Result**
One persistent myth—`home sent one final command and then died`—is rejected.

**Cost / New Question**
The ship has centuries of later information but no simple chain telling it whose orders those messages represented.

**Theme**
TH-010 + TH-016.

**Reward / Hook**
RWD-FACT.
HK-REVERSAL.

**4-point** 4/4.

# EP171 — The Voices After the Parent

**Immediate Goal**
Classify later Sol-origin transmissions by information reliability and authority rather than forcing them into one narrative.

**Primary Engine**
E6/E9 Information.

**Secondary**
E8.

**External Event**
Later centuries contain contradictory but credible material:
- scientific/cultural packets,
- successor-polity claims,
- partially compatible archival updates,
- structured narrowband signals,
- astronomical industrial signatures.

Some messages claim to inherit old mission institutions; others reject those institutions entirely.

**Decision**
The review creates separate labels:
- `source/authenticity confidence`,
- `legal authority recognized by ship`,
- `historical/cultural value`.

A packet can be genuine Sol information without being a command.

**Result**
Sol history becomes plural rather than silent.

**Cost**
Factions lose the ability to quote any convenient `message from Earth` as automatic sovereign authority.

**Theme**
TH-016 + TH-010.

**Reward / Hook**
RWD-FACT + RWD-SOCIAL.
HK-PROMISE.

# EP172 — Humanity Changed There Too

**Immediate Goal**
Determine what later Sol evidence can responsibly establish about surviving humanity without pretending the ship has a current census.

**Primary Engine**
E6.

**Secondary**
E3 Civilizational Wonder.

**External Event**
Authenticated/credible later material indicates Sol civilizations include multiple lineages:
- baseline biological humans,
- heavily modified biological populations,
- synthetic/seeded or related constructed cognition descendants,
- digital/post-biological societies or institutions.

The data is fragmented and centuries-delayed.

**Decision**
The public summary states only what evidence supports:
- Sol did not simply go extinct,
- baseline humans exist/continued there credibly,
- baseline biology is not demonstrably the sole definition of Sol civilization.

**Result**
Nadia definitively ceases to carry any plausible `last human in the universe` burden.

**Cost / Emotional Effect**
The ship loses both comforting myths:
- `we are the only civilization left`,
- `there is a pure Earth waiting to tell us who is human`.

**Character**
Nadia may receive the fact as relief without making it her main life goal.

**Theme**
TH-012 BIOLOGICAL HUMANITY VS HUMAN DESCENT.

**Reward / Hook**
RWD-WONDER + RWD-FACT + RWD-EMO.
HK-CLOSURE+NEXT.

**4-point** 4/4.

# EP176 — What Do We Ask Home?

**Immediate Goal**
Decide the content and legal posture of a new high-power transmission toward Sol.

**Primary Engine**
E7 Governance.

**Secondary**
E8/E9.

**External Event**
Proposed messages range from:
- request permission to amend mission,
- announce independent succession,
- ask for current human/personhood definitions,
- send only scientific arrival status,
- request successor contact/authentication.

Each wording asserts a political relationship.

**Decision**
The convention chooses a message that:
- reports current arrival status,
- identifies the living ship polity and its current institutions,
- requests authenticated successor contact/history,
- does **not** suspend current decisions pending permission.

**Result**
The ship speaks to Sol as a living descendant civilization, not a lost child begging for orders or a defiant stranger denying ancestry.

**Cost**
No wording satisfies both literalists and full-secession advocates.

**Character/Faction**
Livia accepts the message because it preserves the possibility of lawful future relationship.
Kian accepts because it does not cede current sovereignty.

**Theme**
TH-010.

**Reward / Hook**
RWD-CHOICE + RWD-SOCIAL.
HK-PROMISE.

**4-point** 4/4.

# V-C Rollup

## Factual payoff
- Sol alive/plural
- baseline humans exist outside ship; Nadia not last hope
- last uncontested parent mission authority ~voyage year 230
- later signals genuine but politically plural
- no current uncontested parent sovereign
- current round-trip response cannot arrive before climax

## Physical/wonder payoff
Long-range array commissioning and current transmission make light-time material rather than an exposition fact.

## Political payoff
Information from ancestry/home remains valuable without automatically carrying authority.

## Anti-deus-ex
The ship sends a message, preserving future IP/sequel contact, but no answer can resolve this book's ending.

## Handoff
The current civilization must now decide **how** to consent to an irreversible plan when one community's refusal consumes another community's options.
