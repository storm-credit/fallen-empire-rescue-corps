# Sol Communication and Current Humanity v1

Status: **FROZEN HARD CANON**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》
Date: 2026-08-17

This file converts the preferred synthesis in `design/SOLAR_SYSTEM_STATUS_OPTIONS.md` into binding canon.

---

# 1. Core Truth

The Solar System did **not** simply go extinct.

Designer truth:

- Sol remains inhabited.
- Its civilizations are politically and biologically/cognitively plural.
- Baseline biological humans still exist there among other modified, synthetic, and digital/post-biological lineages.
- The shipboard statement `there is one baseline-development human aboard` is strictly local to the ship.

No external Sol society becomes the final moral authority for the ship.

---

# 2. Light-Time

Ross 128 system is roughly 11 light-years from Sol.

Reader-facing order:
- one-way light-time: roughly 11 years
- minimum question-and-response round trip: roughly 22 years

Therefore:

> a query sent around EP001 cannot receive a response within the approximately six-year main-series horizon.

No faster-than-light communication exists.

---

# 3. Early Voyage Communication

At launch, the mission possessed:

- a dedicated high-gain origin-facing communication system,
- authenticated mission cryptography/protocols,
- dedicated Sol-side tracking/transmission infrastructure,
- scheduled delayed archive/science/mission exchanges.

Communication was difficult and expensive but regular enough to matter during the first centuries.

The channel was not equivalent to passive reception of an always-on Solar internet.

It depended on:
- precise long-baseline tracking,
- dedicated transmitting infrastructure,
- compatible authentication/protocol stacks,
- institutions willing to operate it across decades/centuries.

---

# 4. Last Unambiguous Parent-Mission Authority

Working frozen chronology:

> The last message the ship can authenticate unambiguously as coming from the recognized launch-mission authority arrives at approximately **voyage year 230**.

The exact local calendar label may be set later.

That message is not a dramatic final warning.

It contains ordinary mission/science/governance material and reflects a Solar political order already undergoing institutional change.

After this point, no later transmission possesses the same uncontested combination of:
- legal continuity,
- mission authentication,
- shared protocol authority,
- institutional recognition aboard ship.

---

# 5. Why the Parent Channel Ends

No single apocalypse or conspiracy shuts it off.

Over generations:

1. the original launch/colonization authority fragments or dissolves,
2. successor Sol polities dispute ownership/obligation for the old mission,
3. dedicated transmission infrastructure loses unified political sponsorship,
4. protocol/authentication systems diverge,
5. ship-side origin-facing hardware experiences degradation/replacement limitations,
6. later Sol societies adopt new communications/civilizational architectures that are not automatically backward-compatible.

The result is institutional/technical fragmentation, not physical impossibility.

---

# 6. Later Sol Signals

After the authenticated parent channel ends, the ship does receive or detect various later-origin phenomena over centuries, such as:

- narrowband or structured transmissions,
- archival fragments using partially compatible protocols,
- astronomical/industrial signatures,
- messages claiming descent from successor institutions,
- scientific/cultural packets with varying authentication quality.

These establish a broad conclusion:

> **Sol did not simply go dark.**

But they do **not** establish one universally recognized parent government with lawful authority over the ship.

Different later messages may contradict each other politically or culturally.

---

# 7. What the Ship Knows About Current Sol Humanity

By EP001, responsible experts can say:

- Sol almost certainly remains inhabited,
- its societies changed radically,
- no evidence supports treating one current Sol polity as the ship's uncontested sovereign,
- surviving/received material indicates multiple biological, augmented, synthetic, and digital civilizational lineages,
- baseline biological humans probably/credibly still exist in Sol,
- no reliable evidence says baseline biology remained the sole or supreme definition of `human civilization` there.

The ship does not possess a current census or authoritative modern Sol constitution.

---

# 8. Why Sol Did Not Correct the Ship's Historical Understanding

Important distinction:

The ship did not become artificial-origin overnight after communication ended.

The seeded-development transition was gradual and already developing while communication still existed.

Reasons no later Sol message simply says `you are all AI now`:

- Sol itself experienced related biological/synthetic diversification,
- terminology diverged,
- the ship's local Continuity Settlement and civic reforms had their own legal vocabulary,
- later messages were not continuous enough to maintain one synchronized historical taxonomy,
- the decisive modern ignorance is the missing **local historical crosswalk** between current seed medicine and old artificial-person lineage, not ignorance that engineering exists.

No external omniscient observer had a simple duty or shared vocabulary to deliver the novel's answer.

---

# 9. Current Transmission During the Series

During Act V, the ship can send a high-power arrival/status transmission toward Sol containing:

- current mission status,
- Ross 128 arrival approach,
- updated civil/governance contact information,
- request for authenticated successor contact/recognition if any exists.

The transmission is not a plea for permission.

It can be politically debated whether to send it, but once sent:

> **no response can arrive before the main series ends.**

This remains true even if a receiving Sol civilization answers immediately.

---

# 10. Narrative Prohibitions

The following are forbidden unless canon is formally redesigned:

- Sol suddenly answers inside the six-year story because of hidden FTL.
- Earth sends the correct constitution before the climax.
- Sol declares Nadia the rightful human sovereign.
- Sol reveals everyone aboard is mistaken about all prior canon.
- Solar civilization is secretly extinct solely to simplify the plot.
- one late message retroactively decides mission legitimacy.

---

# 11. Act-V Function

The communication history must produce this political result:

> **Information may still come from home; authority does not automatically travel with it.**

The current ship civilization must decide its mission before any outside validation can arrive.

That choice is not isolationist pride; it is a physical consequence of distance plus centuries of institutional divergence.

---

# 12. Freeze Summary

FROZEN:
- Sol alive/plural, not simple extinction.
- last uncontested parent-mission message around voyage year 230.
- later signals exist but lack one uncontested sovereign chain.
- baseline humans still exist outside the ship; Nadia is not humanity's last hope.
- one-way ~11 years; round trip ~22 years.
- no current-response deus ex machina inside series.

Exact texts of historical messages and exact modern Sol factions remain flexible until episode blueprint requires them.
