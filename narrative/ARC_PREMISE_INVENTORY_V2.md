BH-1 must exist before the reader has any reason to suspect her.

During ARC 01, the protagonist's relocation dependency map includes a small fermentation/food cooperative among many neighborhood businesses.

BH-1's role:
- complains that technically equivalent replacement space destroys temperature/water/delivery/customer dependencies
- is one difficult stakeholder among several
- demonstrates one small piece of practical process competence
- no unusual medical clue
- no mysterious narration
- no spotlight ending

The protagonist may not even remember her as uniquely important until much later.

Purpose:
When ACT IV recontextualizes her, the reader recognizes a person who already existed rather than meeting “the last human” at reveal time.

# 3. ARC 09 V2 — THE WRONG CANDIDATE
## EP044–049

### Why revised

V1 placed too much inward family/identity processing next to ARC 10.

### Primary
**E9 Information Cascade**

### Secondary
E1 Transition / E4 Personal

### Present event
A leaked/garbled explanation of the `one human` category causes citizens/media/workplaces to build amateur candidate criteria from:
- rare medical incompatibilities
- old developmental codes
- incomplete clinic histories

A seeded citizen with an unusual but unrelated profile is falsely labeled a possible baseline candidate.

Consequences may include:
- coworkers refusing a placement
- media intrusion
- medical reassessment pressure
- housing/transition disruption

The protagonist must solve the **current harm**, not prove the whole ontology.

### Protagonist provenance subplot
While demonstrating why amateur markers are unreliable, the protagonist lawfully checks his own records.

Payoff:
His birth clearly used ordinary seeded-development architecture.

This reduces chosen-one ambiguity.

### Emotional consequence
Parent is angry that he has converted family memories into evidence.

### Arc reward
- false candidate publicly cleared/protected
- protagonist gets a real answer about his own birth pathway
- society learns `rare medical profile ≠ baseline human`

### Ending
Confirmation/consequence, not another inaccessible clue.

# 4. ARC 18 V2 — PRIVACY HAS A PHYSICAL SHAPE
## EP103–108

### Purpose
Give Act IV visual/world wonder and show privacy is infrastructure, not just law.

### Habitat
H7 Low-Link Communities.

### H7 physical design relevant to plot
H7 deliberately uses more:
- local credential caches
- physically separated community service nodes
- short-retention sensor systems
- manually bridged records
- less continuous cross-habitat telemetry
- architectural routes where identity is not automatically broadcast to every civic service

It is not primitive or anti-technology.

It is **privacy-expensive technology**.

### Present conflict
After deanonymization attempts, a stronger shipwide privacy measure is proposed using H7 principles.

Problem:
The same fragmentation makes a real transition/medical coordination task slower and riskier.

### Action
Protagonist/rival/H7 local staff must physically coordinate across low-link service islands during a time-bounded move.

### Payoff
A privacy safeguard is kept, but redesigned with:
- emergency consent channels
- local data trustees
- explicit expiration/access rules

No return to total central telemetry.

### Wonder
The reader sees a future district whose built environment embodies a philosophy without it being a themed city.

# 5. ARC 22 V2 — THE SIGNATURE HE LOSES
## EP133–140

### Why revised

The protagonist's secret must affect action and authority, not produce 8 episodes of family confrontation.

### Primary
**E7 Institution + E1 Transition**

### Secondary
E4 Sister / E2 Status

### Trigger
Sister's destination-track review exposes timestamp / certification inconsistencies from the old H8 emergency relocation.

The protagonist's unauthorized eligibility extension is verified.

### Immediate professional consequence
His unilateral signing / record-amendment authority is suspended during an ongoing transition project.

A project he designed now requires:
- co-signatures
- reassignment of decisions
- another officer taking authority

### Dramatic pressure
He watches someone else make a defensible choice he would have made differently.

He cannot simply override it.

This makes loss of authority physically/emotionally legible.

### Sister
She controls:
- correction of her record
- what personal medical history is disclosed
- whether she accepts any attempt by protagonist to “fix” the consequences

### Displaced household
The investigation identifies the real downstream household cost from 11 years earlier.

No melodramatic death reveal.

### Payoff
The protagonist learns that even when the original classification was inadequate, **his humane certainty did not erase other people's claims or his sister's agency**.

### Ending
Professional consequence persists into Act V.

He later earns delegated coordination through transparency, not restoration of pristine authority.

# 6. ARC 35 V2 — THE MOVE THAT BREAKS THE MODEL
## EP214–219

### Why revised

V1 had two consecutive E5 capacity/optimization Arcs.

### Primary
**E1 Cross-Habitat Transition Execution**

### Secondary
E5 Material / E4 Colleague-Rival

### Present action
A synchronized movement/conversion schedule derived from the new material constraints begins across several habitats.

The numerical model is physically valid.

It fails socially in execution because several care/labor dependencies are not encoded:
- caregiver arrives from another habitat after a shift
- informal child/elder handoffs cross official household boundaries
- one school/work transit chain depends on a small local route
- a religious/community meal network provides invisible food support

### Physical consequence
Not merely inconvenience.

The failed dependency chain creates:
- missed medical support
- stalled transfer
- overloaded transit
- a temporary life-support / staffing vulnerability

### Colleague
Her model was correct about mass and heat but incomplete about people.

### Protagonist
His social map can repair the operation but cannot ignore the physical budget learned in Act V.

### Rival
Local refusal alone also cannot solve cross-habitat interdependence.

### Payoff
The final transition authority must encode both:
- material accounting
- socially discovered dependency networks

This directly prepares ARC 36.
