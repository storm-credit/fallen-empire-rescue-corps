# 0. Freeze Philosophy

Freeze only what later episode design must be able to trust.

Three states:

## `FROZEN HARD CANON`
Changing this later requires a formal change record and downstream impact audit.

## `FROZEN STRUCTURAL`
The function/relationship is binding, but exact cosmetic implementation may still vary.

## `FLEXIBLE EXECUTION`
Episode-level order, minor incidents, exact scene content, small names, and local texture may move if ledgers remain satisfied.

A design should not remain provisional merely because every fictional decimal has not been chosen.

# 1. Premise / Ontology

## Decision 1A — One baseline-development person exists
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

At the beginning of the series, the ship has approximately 300,000 persons and exactly **one current baseline-development person** under the old developmental-origin classification.

This is a current demographic fact, not a sacred perpetual quota.

Why freeze:
- title promise
- mystery architecture
- Nadia arc
- legal/privacy conflict
- ending philosophy

---

## Decision 1B — Other citizens are embodied artificial-origin persons
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

The other citizens are not remote avatars or metallic robots.

They:
- have human-derived organic bodies,
- are conceived/gestated/born,
- undergo infancy and childhood,
- develop unique personalities through lived experience,
- age, become ill, and die.

Their artificial origin is the **causal developmental architecture of cognition**, not fake social behavior.

---

## Decision 1C — Cognition seed is developmental architecture, not a personality file
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

The cognition-seed system:
- helps bootstrap/organize neural development,
- does not contain an adult self,
- does not encode autobiographical memory, loyalty, occupation, ideology, or future identity,
- cannot reconstruct a dead adult.

No central kill switch / hidden mind network / personality reset exists.

Reason:
Without this boundary, birth, agency, death, restoration, and emotional legitimacy collapse.

# 2. Protagonist / Sole Human

## Decision 2A — Protagonist is seeded-origin
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Haren Savin is **not** the sole baseline-development person.

His importance comes from:
- competence,
- professional position,
- relationships,
- choices,
- institutional consequences.

Chosen-one-by-biology structure remains prohibited.

All previously defined freeze conditions have been met:
- fair clue architecture exists,
- protagonist has strong post-reveal agency,
- BH-1 is a real independent adult character,
- web-novel durability audit passes after Act I acceleration,
- ending works better with seeded Haren.

---

## Decision 2B — Nadia Velo is BH-1
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Nadia Velo is the current sole baseline-development person.

Binding properties:
- unrelated to Haren,
- adult ordinary citizen,
- community-embedded work,
- partner/family life,
- seeded-origin child/family relation,
- no command key,
- no founder throne,
- no sacred reproduction duty,
- strong privacy/medical/autonomy rights.

Exact micro-biography may still adjust during episode blueprint, but identity/function may not.

---

## Decision 2C — Nadia is not necessarily last forever
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Baseline development remains a rare medically possible route.

Nadia is not guaranteed to be:
- first post-transition baseline birth,
- final baseline birth in history,
- required ancestor of future baseline humans.

This prevents sacred-lineage logic.

# 3. Reproduction / Kinship

## Decision 3A — Real pregnancy and childhood
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Seeded children are not downloaded.

Pregnancy, fetal development, birth, attachment, learning, play, education, and bodily development are causal to the resulting person.

---

## Decision 3B — Rare baseline route is a medical/reproductive exception
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

The ship retains the technical/medical ability to support non-seeded autonomous neurodevelopment in rare compatible cases.

The route survives because of:
- reversibility,
- medical compatibility,
- safety resilience,
not because a secret authority is preserving a sacred specimen.

Working demographic rate may be tuned modestly in final numerical QA, but it must keep the present expected live count near one without intentional one-person maintenance.

---

## Decision 3C — Family/personhood not gene-exclusive
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Law recognizes caregiving/gestational/adoptive/household relationships as well as genetics.

This is historically rooted and central to the story's emotional/legal architecture.

# 5. Historical Truth

## Decision 5A — AI did not exterminate original humans
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Original launch humans mostly disappear through ordinary generational death while becoming ancestors/caregivers of the civilization that follows.

---

## Decision 5B — Artificial cognition enters families through medicine
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Designed cognitive developmental architecture descends from artificial-person technology and enters human reproduction first as stabilization/medical support, later as a standard safer developmental platform.

The transition is multi-generational and morally mixed.

---

## Decision 5C — Mixed-origin families precede civic reform
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Baseline, transitional, seeded, and later restored people coexist inside real families and institutions.

No clean species-war history exists.

---

## Decision 5D — Continuity Settlement removes provenance from civic rank
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

After provenance-based discrimination/conflict, reform:
- removes origin from ordinary civil rank,
- compartmentalizes medical provenance,
- preserves necessary medical/safety data,
- weakens the public historical crosswalk between modern seeded development and old artificial-person lineage.

This reform is both genuinely liberating and historically costly.

No mass memory wipe.

# 6. Human Count / Information System

## Decision 6A — No secret list of “real humans”
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

The system does not maintain a public or hidden sacred roster of true humans.

Modern rare-route services maintain anonymous aggregate capacity counts.

Legacy safety translation maps the present non-seeded route to an old baseline-human classification.

This is how the story can fairly verify `1` without exposing Nadia immediately.

# 7. Automation / AI Boundary

## Decision 7A — No hidden central super-AI society
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Ship automation uses bounded tool cognition/automation architectures that are legally and technically separated from person-capable developmental architectures.

The novel does not secretly contain:
- millions of enslaved digital persons,
- one omniscient ship mind,
- an AI puppet master.

Any later personhood ambiguity involving machines would require a formal redesign and is outside this story's core.

# 8. Ship Physical Architecture

## Decision 8A — One ship, habitat archipelago
**FREEZE: STRUCTURAL CANON**

- one connected generation ship,
- eight major rotating habitat worlds,
- axial/service/transit infrastructure,
- multiple distinct cultures/economies/lifestyles,
- habitats remain politically active after introduction.

Exact minor geometry may adjust, but not the one-ship/eight-world structure without major redesign.

---

## Decision 8B — Rotation provides ordinary gravity
**FREEZE: STRUCTURAL CANON**

~1.5-km-class radius habitats at sub-1-rpm order are the reference design.

Exact dimensions may tune slightly if final engineering consistency requires it.

---

## Decision 8C — Closed civilization uses nested recycling, not magic perfect efficiency
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Water/air/nutrients/materials circulate through multiple recovery loops and inventories.

Losses remain inside ship mass where possible and are reprocessed over long periods.

---

## Decision 8D — Radiation architecture is layered
**FREEZE: STRUCTURAL CANON**

Uses combination of:
- low-Z passive material,
- atmosphere/water/food/soil placement,
- storm shelters,
- active magnetic protection as future extrapolation.

No universal meter-thick magic shield.

**FREEZE: STRUCTURAL CANON**

## Decision 10B — No paradise / no fake planet / no alien-invasion pivot
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

- limited/misdistributed accessible water,
- strong permanent-day / terminator / permanent-night regional differences,
- settlement initially depends on controlled local ecologies and cautious water transfer.

## Decision 10C — Ship does not land whole
**FREEZE: HARD CANON**

Large rotating habitats do not land on the planet.

Settlement uses dedicated modules, fabrication, orbital logistics, and transformed ship material.
