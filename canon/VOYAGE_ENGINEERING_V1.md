# Voyage Engineering v1

Status: PROVISIONAL / ENGINEERING ENVELOPE — NOT FROZEN
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

## 1. Design Philosophy

The ship is not near-light-speed magic.

A 300,000-person interstellar generation ship is far beyond demonstrated or currently credible engineering scale. The story should acknowledge that it was a **civilization-scale Solar System project**, not an ordinary spacecraft.

The design goal is:

> speculative future engineering with understandable constraints, not a hard-SF propulsion textbook.

---

# 2. Speed Envelope

## Preferred range

Cruise speed order of magnitude:

**~0.015c–0.025c**

This is much slower than many interstellar-probe concepts and creates a genuine multi-century generation voyage.

### Why not 0.1c

- collision shielding becomes even more extreme
- kinetic energy becomes much larger
- giant inhabited payload makes acceleration/deceleration brutally expensive
- travel becomes too short for the desired deep generational social history to feel inevitable at nearby stars

### Why not 0.001c

Voyage to even nearby stars becomes many millennia, making continuity and maintenance much harder to believe and changing the intended social scale.

---

# 3. Target Distance Envelope

Preferred target distance:

**~10–15 light-years**

At ~0.02c, pure cruise time over 11 light-years is roughly 550 years, before acceleration/deceleration complexity.

This supports:

- many generations
- large historical drift
- enough time for the biological/artificial transition
- a still-recognizable launch-era civilization through archives

---

# 4. Candidate Real Target — Ross 128 System

Status: CANDIDATE ONLY.

Why it is attractive:

- ~11 light-years from Earth
- relatively quiet M dwarf compared with highly active red dwarfs
- confirmed super-Earth Ross 128 b
- short orbital period and likely strong tidal effects create a distinctive future settlement environment
- current habitability remains uncertain, leaving room for future mission-era surveys to establish what the founders actually knew

Current NASA Exoplanet Archive values include approximately:

- planet minimum mass ~1.4 Earth masses
- orbital period ~9.87 days
- orbital radius ~0.05 AU
- equilibrium temperature estimate around 301 K under stated assumptions

### Why it is not frozen

- using a real exoplanet ties fiction to scientific updates
- atmosphere/surface remain uncertain today
- M-dwarf/tidal-lock setting is common in SF
- a future-discovered planet around another real nearby star may provide better originality

# 5. Propulsion Architecture

## 5.1 Launch acceleration

Preferred broad concept:

**Solar-System external infrastructure + onboard advanced propulsion**.

The generation ship should not need to carry every joule and every kilogram of reaction mass required for initial acceleration.

Possible launch-era infrastructure:

- beamed particle/pellet momentum systems
- distributed laser/particle accelerators
- long-duration external push over years
- fusion-powered support craft/boost stages

This is a major extrapolation from current NIAC probe-scale concepts.

## 5.2 Onboard propulsion

Onboard fusion-class pulsed/plasma propulsion handles:

- trim
- course correction
- redundancy
- later braking assistance
- target-system maneuvering

Do not specify impossible constant high thrust.

## 5.3 Deceleration

Potential combination:

- magnetic sail interaction with interstellar medium / stellar wind
- onboard fusion propulsion
- destination-system staged braking

Magnetic-sail concepts have been studied as possible interstellar braking methods, but applying them to this mass is fictional future engineering.

---

# 6. Acceleration Experience

Most residential artificial gravity comes from rotation.

Long-duration interstellar acceleration is low compared with 1g habitat rotation.

The ship therefore does not rely on rocket thrust for everyday gravity.

---

# 7. Forward Shielding

At percent-of-light-speed travel, interstellar dust is a critical threat.

Mandatory architecture:

- large forward sacrificial shield
- water/ice/graphite/metal mass layers
- separated Whipple-like bumper structures
- active long-range detection for larger particles
- laser/plasma/particle mitigation where possible
- replaceable forward shielding material
- navigation avoiding known dense dust structures

This should be treated as a huge material system, not a transparent window at the front of the ship.

The main habitats sit well behind forward protection.

---

# 8. Navigation / Observation

The ship must continuously measure:

- interstellar dust
- star position/proper motion
- target system
- radiation environment
- shield erosion
- course error

Astronomical observation is therefore operational infrastructure, not a hobby.

---

# 9. Arrival Timing Model

Recommended story-state concept:

- the voyage has lasted roughly **five to six centuries**
- the ship is in a late approach/deceleration era
- physical arrival is still **roughly one to two decades away**
- a much nearer **irreversible settlement-preparation threshold** is approaching in a few years

Exact numbers remain OPEN.

Why this works:

- destination decisions matter immediately
- the series does not need to be a 30-day countdown
- protagonist’s habitat-transition profession is central
- characters can plausibly live to see arrival
- settlement preparation can consume/repurpose ship infrastructure now

---

# 10. Proposed Working Numerical Envelope

Not canon; use for QA:

- distance: ~11 ly
- total mission duration: ~580–650 years
- peak/cruise velocity: ~0.02c
- current remaining time: ~12–20 years
- major irreversible conversion threshold: ~2–4 years

These numbers must later be reconciled with acceleration/deceleration profiles.

---

# 11. Communication Problem

At 10–15 light-years, communication with the Solar System is delayed but physically possible.

Therefore the story **cannot ignore Earth/Solar communication history**.

Mandatory future answer:

- when regular targeted communication ended
- why
- what the final verified messages said
- whether the ship can still transmit
- what the designer knows about current Solar civilization

This is a blocking canon issue.

---

# 12. Source / Plausibility Notes

Present-day reference concepts informing only the broad physical families:

- NASA NIAC has investigated fusion spacecraft architectures.
- NASA NIAC has investigated beamed interstellar propulsion for probe-scale payloads.
- NASA/NTRS has studied magnetic sails for braking/advanced missions.
- Project Daedalus/Icarus studies illustrate that even far smaller interstellar probes require extreme propulsion power and mass.

Therefore the generation ship’s propulsion must be framed as a massive future civilizational achievement, not technology casually available to current society.
