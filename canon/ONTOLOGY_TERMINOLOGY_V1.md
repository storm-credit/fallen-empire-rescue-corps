# 1. The Terminology Problem

The current world model intentionally gives seeded-origin citizens:

- human-derived genomes
- organic bodies
- real pregnancy/birth
- biological brains
- human-like growth/aging/disease

Therefore it would be scientifically sloppy to claim:

> “Only one body aboard is biologically human.”

Most citizens' bodies are biologically human-derived organisms.

The real difference lies in the **developmental origin of the conscious neural architecture**.

The title/premise must not rely on semantic cheating.

---

# 2. Three Separate Questions

Never collapse these into one label.

## A. What kind of body is this?

For almost everyone:
**human-derived organic body**.

This is ordinary biology.

## B. How did this person's conscious neural architecture originate?

Two main developmental lineages:

- **unseeded / baseline developmental origin**
- **seeded / artificial developmental origin**

## C. Is this person socially/legally/culturally human?

This is the contested historical/political question.

Modern society begins the story answering:

> everyone is human.

The old mission ontology used a narrower category.

---

# 3. Preferred Neutral Modern Terms

## `시드 발달` / Seeded Development

A person whose neural/cognitive development depended on the engineered cognition scaffold.

Reader-friendly shorthand:
**시드 발달자** when a noun is necessary.

Avoid calling them “AI” in ordinary modern medical language before the historical reveal.

## `비시드 발달` / Unseeded Development

A person whose conscious neural architecture emerged without the synthetic cognition scaffold.

Reader-friendly shorthand:
**비시드 발달자**.

Why this is preferred over `기준형/baseline` in ordinary dialogue:
- `baseline` sounds normatively superior
- `natural` implies seeded people are unnatural
- `original` implies authenticity hierarchy

`baseline` remains a designer/research term where useful.

---

# 4. Historical Launch-Era Terms

The launch civilization had stronger ontology labels because artificial cognition was still a recognized distinct technical lineage.

Working historical categories:

### `생물기원 인격` / Biogenic-origin person
Conscious architecture emerged without an engineered cognition-generating scaffold.

### `인공기원 인격` / Artificial-origin person
Conscious architecture depended causally on deliberately designed cognition-generating developmental architecture.

These terms can later map to political/legal categories.

The exact launch-era Korean translation remains adjustable for prose simplicity.

---

# 5. What “AI” Means in This Setting

Historically, `artificial intelligence` did not necessarily mean:

- silicon computer
- robot
- server process

It meant an intelligence whose **cognitive architecture had an artificial causal origin**.

Early seeded persons were therefore technically categorized inside the artificial-intelligence lineage even when:
- conceived biologically
- born from a human parent
- embodied organically
- raised as children

Over centuries, that technical category became socially obsolete and eventually politically dangerous.

---

# 6. Why Modern Citizens Do Not Call Themselves AI

The technology became:
- reproductive medicine
- developmental medicine
- inherited normal infrastructure

A seeded person does not experience:
> “software running inside a body.”

They experience:
> being alive.

Calling them “AI” in the modern era would sound as historically loaded as describing a person entirely by an obsolete medical origin class.

After the historical truth emerges, some groups reclaim the term, some reject it, and some use it as a slur/technical descriptor/political identity.

No single usage becomes automatically correct.

---

# 7. The Title-Premise Fairness Solution

The story's early discovery should **not** initially be phrased as an omniscient scientific claim that only one organic human body exists.

Preferred factual sequence:

### Step 1
A launch-era protected system still contains a category equivalent to:

> `human-origin / unseeded cognitive lineage`

### Step 2
Current aggregate count under that historical category:

> **1**

### Step 3
The old mission's own legal/technical vocabulary treated that category as `human`, while engineered-origin persons belonged to the artificial-intelligence lineage.

Therefore the title's provocative statement is fair:

> **Under the civilization's original definition, the ship currently contains one human.**

The entire novel then challenges whether that original definition deserves moral/legal authority.

---

# 8. Important Reader-Facing Clarification

By ACT II–III the reader must understand clearly:

> The other 299,999 are not robots pretending to have human bodies.

They possess human-derived organic bodies.

What is artificial is the causal developmental architecture from which their minds emerged.

This must be paid early enough that the premise does not feel like a bait-and-switch.

---

# 9. Why the Difference Still Matters Technically

If there were no concrete consequences, the distinction would be semantic.

Seeded developmental origin affects some:

- developmental medical records
- neural repair compatibility
- state-capture/restoration compatibility
- old mission protocols
- historical reproduction policy
- old destination assumptions

Unseeded development may show:
- different variance
- missing scaffold markers
- poorer compatibility with seed-standardized tools

None implies greater intelligence, creativity, emotion, or moral worth.

---

# 10. Legal Terminology Before Reveal

Modern law should avoid origin-based citizenship categories.

Most systems classify people by:
- age
- health need
- residency
- household/care relationship
- professional certification
- current consent/status

Developmental-seed information is medical information, not routine civic identity.

This makes origin privacy plausible.

---

# 11. Legal Terminology After Reveal

Preferred final framework separates:

## `인격/시민 지위`
Universal rights-bearing status.

## `발달 기원`
Medical/historical provenance.

## `복원 연속성 상태`
Relevant where a person emerged through restoration/capture.

## `인간`
Cultural, historical, biological, and personal identity term rather than the sole gate to civic rights.

The state stops asking:

> “How human are you?”

as a rights hierarchy.

# 12. Restoration Terminology

Avoid `백업` as the universal reader term because it falsely implies an easy reversible file copy.

Preferred:

### `상태 기록`
High-resolution adult neural/embodied continuity capture.

### `복원`
Creation/reconstruction of a conscious claimant from a prior state record.

### `복원자` / `복원 인격`
Working reader terms for the resulting person.

Exact final Korean term remains open after audio QA.

# 13. Provenance Terminology

Avoid overusing the loanword `프로비넌스` in Korean prose.

# 15. “Human” Vocabulary by Era

## Launch era
`human` may be a technical/legal lineage category.

## Mixed-origin era
Term becomes politically contested.

## Post-Continuity-Settlement era
`human` broadens socially; origin category leaves routine civil records.

## Series opening
Almost everyone uses `human` normally for everyone.

## Reveal period
Old technical definition returns and destabilizes language.

## Ending
No state-imposed single definition ranks personhood.

This gives the word a **history**, not just a philosophical definition.

---

# 16. Forbidden Terminology Mistakes

Do not say without qualification:
- “everyone except BH-1 is biologically fake”
- “their bodies are artificial robots”
- “their DNA is AI DNA”
- “only BH-1 is Homo sapiens” if seeded bodies remain genetically human-derived
- “the AI process runs on the ship computer”

Do not equate:
- synthetic developmental origin = copied personality
- organic body = baseline-origin mind
- restoration = seed development

---

# 17. Title/Marketing Rule

The title remains strong because it expresses the **launch-era classification shock** that drives the story.

But the work itself must quickly complicate the phrase rather than secretly changing definitions at the end.

Recommended reader payoff rhythm:

- EP~10: old system count = 1
- EP~11–32: clarify the rest have real organic lives/births and known seed medicine
- EP~56–95: explain why old system called their cognition artificial and why that category disappeared

No 100-episode semantic withholding.

---

# 18. Final Thematic Precision

The series is **not** asking:

> “Are organic bodies better than robots?”

It is asking:

> If a conscious person was caused by an engineered developmental architecture but then genuinely grew, loved, remembered, suffered, chose, and died—what, if anything, should origin decide about who they are?

And the political version:

> Can a society preserve meaningful biological/medical differences without converting them into human worth?
