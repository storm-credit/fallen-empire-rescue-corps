# Relationship State Ledger

Status: **ACTIVE PRE-BLUEPRINT LEDGER**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Track how major relationships change across Acts so no relationship survives 250 episodes by repeating one emotional beat.

A relationship state is defined across five dimensions:

- **Trust** — willingness to rely on the other person
- **Power** — who can materially/legally constrain whom
- **Knowledge** — what each person knows about the other
- **Obligation** — what each believes they owe the other
- **Distance** — emotional/social/professional closeness

Every Act return must change at least one dimension materially.

---

# R1 — Haren ↔ Mara

Core pressure: **planned continuity vs lived place**

## ACT I
- Trust: high family trust, low professional trust
- Power: Haren's office can affect H8 transition; Mara has local legitimacy
- Knowledge: Mara knows Haren's habits better than his current doubts
- Obligation: Haren thinks he should save home efficiently; Mara wants presence before solution
- Distance: affectionate but work creates friction
- Change: Haren's professional role becomes personally threatening

## ACT II
- Trust: reduced when Haren treats family memory as evidence
- Power: Haren still has institutional authority
- Knowledge: Mara realizes Haren is questioning his own origin
- Obligation: Mara refuses to become a witness proving his identity
- Distance: emotionally sharper, not estranged

## ACT III
- Trust: partially restored through shared handling of public truth
- Power: public institutions increasingly matter more than Haren alone
- Knowledge: both learn their family history remains real regardless of provenance
- Obligation: Mara becomes more active in H8's own response
- Distance: conflict shifts from ontology to future of place

## ACT IV
- Trust: Mara criticizes Haren's past Yuna manipulation without rejecting him
- Power: Haren loses some authority
- Knowledge: Mara knows the full old falsification consequence
- Obligation: Haren can no longer frame himself as sole protector
- Distance: painful honesty increases adult equality

## ACT V
- Trust: functional
- Power: H8 residents gain more formal voice
- Knowledge: arrival conversion costs become concrete
- Obligation: Mara organizes what H8 itself wants preserved
- Distance: professional/political disagreement remains

## ACT VI
- Trust: high enough for loss without betrayal framing
- Power: neither can preserve everything
- Knowledge: both understand full material cost
- Obligation: Haren helps execute a choice Mara helped shape
- Distance: grief and respect coexist
- Major change: original workshop/neighborhood form ends

## ACT VII
- Trust: mature
- Power: relationship no longer mediated by Haren's office
- Knowledge: both know what was preserved and what was not
- Obligation: no debt of agreement
- Distance: close family with permanent disagreement

Final state:
**Mutual respect without consensus.**

# R2 — Haren ↔ Yuna

Core pressure: **protection vs adult agency**

## ACT I
- Trust: warm but asymmetrical
- Power: Haren still behaves like former protector
- Knowledge: Yuna knows he worries excessively; does not know full falsification cost
- Obligation: Haren feels responsible for her life trajectory
- Distance: affectionate with irritation

## ACT II
- Trust: changes when Yuna's ecology decision fails independently
- Power: Yuna owns her professional consequence; Haren cannot fix it
- Knowledge: Haren sees Yuna capable of failure and recovery without him
- Obligation: Yuna rejects rescue but accepts ordinary sibling support
- Distance: more adult equality

## ACT III
- Trust: complicated by public provenance debate
- Power: neither controls the other's identity status
- Knowledge: both understand their seeded origin increasingly clearly
- Obligation: family solidarity without identity proof
- Distance: strengthened through shared pressure

## ACT IV
- Trust: sharply damaged by exposure of Haren's old record manipulation
- Power: Yuna gains control over correction/disclosure of her own file
- Knowledge: she learns another household paid a cost
- Obligation: Haren owes accountability, not a second unilateral fix
- Distance: temporary rupture

## ACT V
- Trust: rebuilt through boundaries
- Power: Yuna makes her own settlement-track choices
- Knowledge: Haren knows she may choose risk he dislikes
- Obligation: advice replaces guardianship
- Distance: adult siblings rather than protector/dependent

## ACT VI
- Trust: stable
- Power: independent careers
- Knowledge: Yuna's own ecological failure informs policy
- Obligation: reciprocal, not one-way
- Distance: close despite separate work/worlds

## ACT VII
- Trust: high
- Power: Yuna makes a surface go/no-go decision Haren cannot overrule
- Knowledge: Haren understands autonomy includes consequences he cannot prevent
- Obligation: no protective debt remains
- Distance: chosen family closeness

Final state:
**Sibling bond without ownership.**

# R4 — Haren ↔ Elin

Core pressure: **benevolent anticipation vs explicit adult choice**

## ACT I
- Trust: residual from old relationship
- Power: mostly professional separation
- Knowledge: each knows the other's avoidance patterns
- Obligation: deliberately limited
- Distance: unresolved, controlled

## ACT II
- Trust: grows through continuity/birth cases
- Power: Elin has medical authority in her domain
- Knowledge: ontology uncertainty reopens personal vulnerability
- Obligation: Elin refuses to serve as proof of Haren's reality
- Distance: emotionally closer but boundaries strengthen

## ACT III
- Trust: pressured by public reveal and consent debates
- Power: separate professional legitimacy
- Knowledge: each sees the other's public role under stress
- Obligation: mutual professional defense without romantic restoration
- Distance: intimate history acknowledged, not resolved

## ACT IV
- Trust: affected by Haren's falsification exposure
- Power: Elin judges him as an adult partner candidate, not patient
- Knowledge: she sees the pattern of deciding for others
- Obligation: none to forgive quickly
- Distance: may widen temporarily

## ACT V
- Trust: rebuilt through behavior
- Power: Elin owns continuity-medicine transition policy
- Knowledge: Haren learns not to infer her future wishes
- Obligation: explicit discussion replaces anticipation
- Distance: possible renewed intimacy begins slowly

## ACT VI
- Trust: tested by high-cost consent case
- Power: each must accept the other's domain decisions
- Knowledge: Elin confronts her own blind spot about burden discussion
- Obligation: mutual honesty about what choices cost others
- Distance: deeper and more reciprocal

## ACT VII
- Trust: chosen
- Power: separate careers/world responsibilities
- Knowledge: no illusion that old relationship can be restored unchanged
- Obligation: explicitly negotiated
- Distance: new relationship begins/continues under real geographic separation

Final state:
**A new relationship, not restoration of the old one.**

# R5 — Haren ↔ Toma

Core pressure: **adaptive exception vs common institutional floor**

- Power: Toma clearly superior institutionally
- Knowledge: Toma knows Haren's strengths, not full old secret
- Obligation: Haren seeks legitimacy; Toma expects procedural loyalty

## ACT II

- Power: Toma can overrule
- Knowledge: Haren sees institutional caution as more than cowardice

## ACT III

- Power: Toma still controls information briefly
- Knowledge: Haren learns Toma chose delay knowingly
- Obligation: Toma believes he protected patients; Haren sees paternalism
- Distance: major rupture
- Consequence: Toma loses unilateral information-release authority

## ACT IV
- Trust: conditional
- Power: Haren later loses his own unilateral authority, complicating his moral certainty
- Knowledge: each now understands the other's dangerous form of benevolent control
- Obligation: neither can claim clean hands
- Distance: colder but more equal

## ACT V
- Trust: functional
- Power: shared oversight structures grow
- Knowledge: both accept no single institution can own transition legitimacy
- Obligation: protect common floor while broadening consent
- Distance: repaired professionally

## ACT VI

- Power: Toma's central institution gives up real control
- Knowledge: common floor and local authority both prove necessary
- Obligation: cooperate despite different instincts

## ACT VII
- Trust: mature institutional respect
- Power: decentralized
- Knowledge: both strongest arguments survive
- Obligation: maintain compact safeguards
- Distance: no return to simple mentor/protégé hierarchy

Final state:
**Former mentor and protégé become institutional peers with persistent disagreement.**

# 8. Ledger Execution Rules

During full episode blueprint:

1. Every major relationship appearing in an episode references its `R#`.
2. No relationship may go more than ~25–35 episodes without either:
   - a state change,
   - a callback showing the consequence of a prior state,
   - or a documented intentional absence.
3. Repeated arguments without changed power/knowledge/obligation are rejected.
4. Major reveal episodes do not automatically count as relationship movement unless behavior changes.
5. Act VII must not resolve every relationship through agreement.
