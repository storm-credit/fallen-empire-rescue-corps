# RT2-01 — EXACTLY ONE STILL NEEDS STATISTICAL TEETH

## Attack

Current answer:
A rare scaffold-bypass/unseeded developmental contingency exists, and Nadia happens to be the only currently living unseeded person.

This is philosophically strong but statistically underdesigned.

Reader attack:
> “If the route exists and the population has had millions of births, why is there exactly one now?”

## Required fix

Design:
- annual birth rate / population replacement
- how often scaffold-bypass is medically triggered
- how many unseeded births occurred historically
- survival/age distribution
- why nobody electively chooses it before the reveal
- whether new elective unseeded births become possible after the reveal

Preferred direction:
The current `1` should be a **plausible Poisson-like rare outcome**, not a hidden quota.

Historical unseeded people after seed dominance may have existed occasionally.

Nadia need not be the first or last—only the sole living one at EP001.

### Severity
**CRITICAL CANON BLOCKER.**

# RT2-03 — “AI” MUST REMAIN MORE THAN AN OLD LABEL

## Attack

Modern citizens already know cognition-seed medicine exists.

If the reveal is merely:
> “People 400 years ago called this AI,”

the title loses force.

## Existing defense

`COGNITION_SEED_SYSTEM_V1` makes the scaffold a **necessary causal organizer** of seeded conscious neural development, not simple gene therapy.

## Remaining burden

Modern technical education must make sense:
- doctors/engineers know what the scaffold physically does
- they need not accept the old ontological conclusion

Historical reveal must add something concrete:
- seed architecture descends directly from deliberate artificial-consciousness systems
- early seeded persons were legally/socially recognized as artificial-origin minds
- the architecture's role was not merely “prevent birth defect” but **reliably bootstrap cognitive organization that biology alone was no longer providing in that developmental pathway**

Do not make modern science mysteriously ignorant.

The shock is:

> the technology everyone regards as normal human developmental medicine is genealogically and causally the artificial cognition lineage that once lived alongside baseline humans.

This is historical/technical continuity, not wordplay.

### Severity
**HIGH — CAN BE CLOSED WITH HISTORY/TECH CROSSWALK.**

# RT2-04 — WHY SOCIETY DOES NOT COLLAPSE AFTER EP90

## Attack

At EP83–95, 300,000 citizens learn they descend through a technical lineage once categorized as AI.

Yet the ship must keep operating.

Reader attack:
> “Wouldn't everyone stop working / riot / collapse?”

## Answer

Not everyone experiences the reveal as discovering they are machines.

They already know:
- their bodies are organic
- seed developmental medicine exists
- they were born and grew normally

The new facts are:
- old origin classification
- mixed-family history
- deliberate provenance erasure
- current sole unseeded count

Therefore the strongest realistic disruption is:
- politics
- law
- religion
- identity language
- privacy
- family histories
- mission legitimacy

not universal inability to function.

## Required design

Build a **post-reveal social adaptation timeline** across Acts III–V:
- first week
- first months
- first year
- institutional stabilization
- persistent minority radicalization

Show work continuing because people still need food, heat, care, and arrival preparation.

### Severity
**HIGH SOCIAL BLOCKER.**

# RT2-08 — FINAL COMPACT RISKS CONSENSUS UTOPIA

## Attack

The current ending preserves one good principle from every faction.

That is elegant but can feel engineered:
> everybody learns a lesson and the protagonist synthesizes the perfect constitution.

## Required correction

The final compact should contain **durable tradeoffs**.

Examples:
- H7 accepts shared safety telemetry it dislikes
- F2 accepts public funding for restoration access it doubts metaphysically
- F3 accepts restoration does not automatically carry every prior legal obligation unchanged
- H8 loses real material despite autonomy gains
- F4 accepts mission rewrite but preserves binding intergenerational reserve obligations
- surface settlements gain autonomy but accept orbital dependence for years

At least one faction wing refuses ratification yet remains under negotiated safety/mobility treaty rather than being defeated.

### Severity
**HIGH.**

# RT2-10 — ENGINEERING MIRACLE STACKING

## Attack

Current major extrapolations include:
- 0.06–0.08c-class effective direct-fusion exhaust
- petawatt propulsion
- giant superconducting active radiation shielding
- synthetic cognition development
- adult continuity restoration

Too many miracles can erode credibility.

## Required hierarchy

Explicitly classify:

### Miracle Tier 1 — central speculative technologies
1. synthetic developmental cognition
2. high-performance interstellar propulsion

### Tier 2 — advanced descendants of known research families
- HTS magnetic shielding
- bioregenerative closed material loops
- advanced fusion civil power
- neural state capture/restoration

Restoration is philosophically important, but its performance remains deliberately limited so it does not function as magic.

Do not add:
- FTL
- reactionless drive
- gravity control
- mind reading
- arbitrary matter printing

### Severity
**HIGH SF-DISCIPLINE RULE.**
