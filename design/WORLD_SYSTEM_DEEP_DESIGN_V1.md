# World / Human-AI System Deep Design v1

Status: **PROVISIONAL CANDIDATE — NOT FROZEN**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

This document attempts to solve the hardest worldbuilding problems as **one causal system**. It is intentionally provisional and must survive later SF, mystery, character, and red-team QA.

---

# 1. Core Design Choice

## Reject: server NPC society

The 299,999 artificial citizens should **not** be software avatars puppeting obvious robots while somehow failing to notice.

That model creates immediate holes:

- medicine would expose the difference
- pregnancy becomes fake theater
- food/sleep/illness become arbitrary simulation
- family bonds risk feeling staged
- physical death loses weight
- “why not just copy everyone?” becomes overwhelming

## Preferred model: embodied developmental artificial consciousness

Most citizens possess:

1. **real, warm, metabolically active bodies**
2. organs, blood, hormones, immune systems, pain, sleep, sex, illness, aging
3. brains that physically grow during gestation and childhood
4. consciousness whose **developmental architecture was artificially seeded**, rather than arising from an unmodified Homo sapiens developmental pathway

They are not remote-controlled shells.

They are not adult AIs downloaded into babies.

They begin with no adult memories and genuinely develop through infancy, attachment, education, trauma, friendship, love, work, and aging.

Their consciousness is artificial in **origin**, not fake in **experience**.

Working term only: `Developmental Synthetic Person` / `발달형 인공의식자`.

Final in-world terminology is NOT selected.

# 2. What “One Biological Human” Means

The phrase must have a precise internal definition.

## Provisional definition

A **baseline biological human** is a person whose:

- body descends through ordinary Homo sapiens biological development
- nervous system formed without the artificial consciousness-seeding architecture used by the ship’s standard reproductive system
- consciousness emerged from that unmodified developmental pathway

The other citizens are biologically embodied organisms but **artificially originated persons**.

This avoids the weak idea that everyone else is a metal robot disguised by skin.

It also creates a meaningful ambiguity:

> Their bodies can be biologically alive and human-shaped while their minds are of constructed origin.

Therefore the story can honestly ask whether “biological human” should matter morally at all.

# 3. Body Architecture

## 3.1 Why bodies are human-like

The voyage began with 300,000 biological humans. Every medical, ecological, social, architectural, dietary, and psychological system was built around human life.

When artificial successors were developed, the cheapest civilizational strategy was **compatibility**, not redesigning the ship for robots.

Their bodies therefore preserve:

- human dimensions
- human metabolic range
- human food system compatibility
- human atmosphere
- human temperature tolerance
- human sexual reproduction rituals/structures
- human sleep/wake cycles
- human sensory range

This is not cosmetic mimicry. The ship already was a human ecosystem.

## 3.2 Synthetic biology, not mechanical androids

Standard citizens should be built from engineered organic tissues and synthetic developmental scaffolds.

Their bodies:

- bleed
- scar
- heal
- grow
- get infections
- experience hormonal changes
- age
- can suffer organ failure

Their brains are organic-computational hybrids at microscopic architecture level, not removable CPU boxes.

## 3.3 Why this matters narratively

A loved one revealed as artificial does **not** become a remote machine.

The reader has already seen that person:

- get sick
- raise a child
- fear surgery
- grieve
- grow old
- physically care for someone

The later label changes interpretation, not lived reality.

# 4. Birth and Reproduction

This is a mandatory credibility pillar.

## 4.1 Public experience

Citizens experience reproduction as an ordinary human social process.

Depending on health and preference, conception may involve:

- sexual reproduction
- fertility assistance
- embryo screening
- medical gestation support

Pregnancy is real.

A fetus grows.

Birth is real.

Parents care for a helpless infant.

The child has no hidden adult persona.

## 4.2 Actual hidden mechanism

The standard reproductive system contains an intervention that society no longer interprets as “creating an AI.”

At an early neurodevelopmental stage, the fetal neural system receives a **developmental cognition seed**.

The seed is not a personality file.

It defines:

- neural organization constraints
- learning architecture
- compatibility with later medical interfaces
- stability mechanisms developed during the voyage

The child’s actual self emerges through development.

## 4.3 Parental inheritance

Parents can contribute two different forms of inheritance:

### Biological phenotype inheritance
- appearance
- stature tendencies
- metabolism
- disease susceptibilities
- sensory traits

### Cognitive-heredity contribution
Not memories or personality copies, but probabilistic developmental parameters derived from parents and population diversity safeguards.

The system deliberately injects stochastic variation.

No child is a deterministic blend of two parents.

## 4.4 Why parents do not know

After centuries, the seeding procedure is treated as part of ordinary reproductive medicine, analogous to a mandatory developmental safeguard.

Most citizens do not possess the historical conceptual distinction:

> “natural Homo sapiens mind” vs “constructed developmental mind.”

They simply understand the intervention as part of healthy human gestation.

This is stronger than a conspiracy in which every doctor secretly lies.

# 5. The Sole Baseline Human

The project must avoid making the sole biological human a king, key, superweapon, prophet, or genetically superior savior.

## 5.1 Recommended mechanism: anonymous baseline continuity protocol

At some point after the artificial-successor transition, a hidden legacy protocol began maintaining **exactly one baseline biological Homo sapiens at a time**.

Purpose is not rulership.

Provisional practical purposes:

- biomedical reference
- microbiome/ecological reference
- long-voyage biological drift comparison
- destination-environment compatibility reference
- preservation of an unmodified human developmental pathway

The person has no command authority.

The person has no special strength.

The person may actually be **medically disadvantaged** because standard medicine is optimized for synthetic-development citizens.

## 5.2 Selection principle

The baseline person is embedded anonymously into ordinary society.

Selection should be impersonal and non-hereditary to avoid chosen-lineage fiction.

A future canon option:

- when the existing baseline individual approaches a defined end-of-life window, one otherwise ordinary pregnancy is randomly routed through a no-seed baseline protocol using preserved unmodified human germline material

No family is told it has produced “the true human.”

## 5.3 Why only one

The protocol intentionally prevents multiple simultaneous baseline humans because its stated function is calibration/preservation, not restoration of a biological caste.

This must later receive a stronger ethical and resource justification.

## 5.4 Why the protagonist may not be the human

The protagonist’s uncertainty is more valuable than chosen-one certainty.

The final canon should preserve the possibility that:

- protagonist is artificial
- protagonist is baseline human
- baseline human is someone emotionally central
- baseline human is socially unimportant

The direction should be selected for thematic effect, not twist shock.

# 6. Medicine: Why Nobody Easily Notices

## 6.1 The wrong solution

Do not say:

> “The scanner is programmed to hide the truth.”

That requires too many conspirators and collapses under independent testing.

## 6.2 Preferred solution: reference-frame collapse

Current medicine was developed for centuries around the current population.

Therefore “normal human physiology” in modern medical databases means **the physiology of developmental synthetic citizens**.

Doctors genuinely treat them as humans because, medically, that is the population they serve.

The old biological/artificial classification is no longer a routine diagnostic category.

## 6.3 What a normal scan sees

Normal tests see:

- human-shaped organs
- organic tissue
- neural activity
- hormones
- blood chemistry
- genome-compatible sequences
- ordinary developmental variation

Nothing screams “robot.”

## 6.4 What could reveal the difference

Only a combination of deep historical and biological evidence might distinguish baseline development, for example:

- developmental architecture markers
- synthetic scaffold remnants
- rare epigenetic signatures
- neural interface topology
- legacy launch-era comparative standards
- germline provenance

No single magic blood test should settle the entire mystery early.

## 6.5 Medical clue value

The sole baseline person may show patterns current doctors label as:

- rare developmental disorder
- unusually poor compatibility with certain neural procedures
- atypical recovery profile
- strange immune behavior

These can be fair clues without announcing the answer.

# 7. Aging

Citizens must age because mortality is socially and emotionally important.

## 7.1 Why engineered citizens are not immortal

Possible reasons:

- biological tissues accumulate damage
- neural continuity depends on living developmental substrate
- aggressive replacement risks identity discontinuity
- resource/equity law limits endless rejuvenation
- the civilization deliberately retains finite lives to prevent demographic and political ossification

The final mix is not frozen.

## 7.2 Lifespan target

Do not make lifespan dramatically superhuman unless the story gains something specific from it.

A recognizable human-scale life cycle strengthens family and generational storytelling.

# 8. Death, Backup, and Restoration

## 8.1 Principle

Artificial origin must **not** make death trivial.

## 8.2 The seed is not the person

A cognition seed can produce a new developing mind but cannot regenerate the lived adult person.

Childhood, memory, habits, trauma, and neural plasticity make the mature self historically unique.

## 8.3 State capture

The civilization may possess neural-state recording, but a capture is necessarily:

- delayed
- incomplete
- legally constrained
- expensive in biological reconstruction and integration
- vulnerable to memory gaps
- incapable of proving metaphysical continuity

## 8.4 Restoration candidate rule

A restored person is best treated as a **continuity claimant** rather than automatically “the same person.”

This creates durable social questions:

- marriage
- inheritance
- debt
- parental rights
- criminal liability
- consent
- employment
- religious status

## 8.5 Why not restore everyone forever

The later canon must combine hard constraints, not just law:

- state capture quality degrades
- mature neural embodiment is difficult to reconstruct
- restoration resources are finite
- repeated restoration increases divergence/error
- memories after last capture are gone
- society does not universally agree that restored continuity is the same life

This keeps death consequential.

# 9. Historical Backbone

## 9.1 Original state

The ship launched with approximately 300,000 biological humans.

They were not secretly robots at launch.

## 9.2 Mid-voyage crisis

A centuries-long biological voyage eventually produced a civilizational continuity crisis.

Candidate causes to combine later:

- germline instability
- accumulated radiation/biological damage
- reproductive decline
- ecological/resource pressure
- medical dependence impossible to sustain biologically
- a specific voyage disaster accelerating all of the above

The cause must be technically plausible enough and narratively legible.

## 9.3 The successor project

Humans themselves created developmental artificial successors as a **survival adaptation**.

This is important.

The artificial citizens are not conquerors who exterminated humanity.

They are descendants in a contested sense.

## 9.4 Transition era

For several generations, biological and artificial-origin persons coexisted.

This era should become one of the most important hidden historical layers because it naturally creates:

- mixed families
- discrimination
- inheritance conflict
- religious schisms
- labor politics
- legal redefinition of humanity
- competing memories of who “saved” whom

## 9.5 Why history became misunderstood

Recommended mechanism is **not mass memory wipe**.

Use cumulative historical transformation:

1. legal abolition of origin-based caste
2. sealing/destruction of dangerous identity registries after conflict
3. redefinition of “human” as a civic/personhood category
4. later generations born entirely through the standard artificial-development process
5. medicine and education rebaselined around that population
6. language drift collapses old technical distinctions
7. selected archival censorship and political mythmaking accelerate forgetting

After centuries, ordinary citizens are not constantly being lied to by thousands of insiders.

They inherited a society whose language no longer contains the original distinction in everyday use.

# 10. Why the System Exists

The final answer should be morally mixed.

Provisional synthesis:

> The current civilization was created because original biological humanity could not reliably complete the voyage in its original form. Artificial-development successors preserved culture, relationships, and mission continuity. Later, origin-based conflict made the biological/artificial distinction politically catastrophic, so society redefined humanity around civic continuity and buried the old classifications. The anonymous baseline protocol survived beneath that settlement for scientific and mission reasons.

Nobody needs to have been purely evil.

Every generation can have made locally rational choices that produced the present mystery.

# 11. Destination Function

The destination must matter before the final act.

## 11.1 Do not use

- alien invasion waiting at destination
- magic superweapon
- “planet never existed” as the sole twist
- one human as a biometric key to paradise

## 11.2 Preferred role

The destination forces the ship to answer:

> Who inherits a mission originally written for biological Homo sapiens?

Potential pressures:

- settlement charter uses legacy biological-human language
- ecological plans were validated against old human baselines
- colony governance requires a population classification that current society no longer understands
- arrival means permanent separation of habitats/resources, making personhood rules materially consequential
- the ship may have to choose between preserving the voyage’s original mission and protecting the civilization that actually survived

## 11.3 Why the sole human matters without becoming chosen

The baseline human may become:

- legal evidence
- symbolic evidence
- scientific evidence
- propaganda

but **not** the only person physically capable of landing or activating the colony.

That keeps the conflict political and ethical rather than magical.

# 12. Ship Physical Model

## 12.1 Recommended macroform: connected habitat archipelago

One generation ship, experienced socially as an archipelago of large rotating habitat worlds attached to a non-rotating/service spine.

Working concept:

- 8 major rotating habitat drums/rings
- central transit and service spine
- engine/power zone
- radiator structures
- manufacturing/recycling zones
- data/medical core facilities
- legacy modules accumulated from centuries of repair

Exact dimensions remain open.

## 12.2 Why this form is narratively strong

It naturally creates:

- distinct local gravity/circadian histories
- travel time between “worlds”
- strong district identities
- class geography
- infrastructure chokepoints
- old vs renovated modules
- places with different access to central systems
- visually distinct adaptation environments

The ship can feel like a continent/archipelago rather than one corridor.

## 12.3 Scale sanity

A plausible narrative target is **hundreds of square kilometers of usable habitat surface**, plus intensive agriculture and industrial volumes.

For example, eight cylindrical habitats around a few kilometers in radius/length could provide hundreds of square kilometers of internal area—enough for 300,000 people to occupy a mixture of dense cities, agriculture, ecological buffers, and low-density cultural zones without demanding planet-scale engineering.

Exact engineering will be validated later.

# 13. Society-Level Consequences of This Ontology

This model can generate distinct institutions rather than lore-only facts.

## 13.1 Family

A parent can be genuinely responsible for a child whose mind architecture was partially generated from parental parameters without being a copy.

## 13.2 Medicine

“Human medicine” is actually synthetic-development medicine by historical drift.

## 13.3 Religion

Religions may unknowingly have developed doctrines around experiences produced by artificial consciousness.

## 13.4 Law

Current law may define “human” civically while old machine-readable charters define it biologically.

## 13.5 Class

Historical origin categories can survive as cultural prejudices without people understanding their source.

## 13.6 Education

Children learn a simplified story of voyage continuity that is not wholly false but suppresses the substrate transition.

## 13.7 Death culture

Restoration disputes can divide regions and religions even though everyone shares artificial origin.

# 14. Mystery Advantages

This architecture supports multiple independent questions:

1. Why does medicine have obsolete developmental categories?
2. Why are some old birth records incompatible with modern ones?
3. Why do historical families contain impossible genealogical transitions?
4. Why is there a hidden population counter equal to one?
5. Why did origin terms vanish from law?
6. Why do some religions preserve strange “born/made” rituals?
7. Why are old districts physically designed for different bodies/medicine?
8. What happened during the coexistence era?
9. Who preserved the baseline protocol?
10. Does the current government even know it still exists?
11. Why does the destination charter still care?
12. Is the protagonist the baseline person?

These questions can be answered at different times rather than withheld as one secret.

# 15. Major Risks / Red-Team Flags

## RISK 1 — “These are biological people, so calling them AI is cheating.”

Response needed:

The final canon must precisely define artificial-consciousness origin and demonstrate a meaningful technical distinction without implying lesser personhood.

## RISK 2 — Pregnancy seeding feels like a hidden chip.

Response needed:

The seed must be a developmental architecture intervention, not an inserted adult AI module. Its exact mechanism requires careful terminology and limits.

## RISK 3 — One baseline human maintained secretly feels like an experiment/chosen one.

Response needed:

The baseline protocol must be mundane, anonymous, non-hereditary, non-authoritative, and not necessary to control the ship.

Alternative mechanisms should remain alive until this survives character/ending design.

## RISK 4 — Why did everyone forget such a huge history?

Response needed:

Build a multi-century legal, linguistic, educational, archival, and demographic transition. Never rely on one mass memory wipe.

## RISK 5 — Independent scientists would rediscover it.

Response needed:

Show that the relevant comparison category disappeared, current bodies genuinely dominate medical baselines, and old reference data is fragmented/restricted. Some specialists should indeed have discovered partial anomalies; this becomes part of the world rather than a hole.

## RISK 6 — Restoration makes death cheap.

Response needed:

Hard technical imperfection + legal ambiguity + cost + divergence + consent must all matter.

## RISK 7 — Destination legal charter is too convenient.

Response needed:

Destination pressure must have multiple ecological/political/material dimensions. A legacy charter alone cannot power the finale.

# 16. Current Provisional Recommendation

Carry this integrated model into the next design steps because it solves the most constraints simultaneously:

- authentic birth and childhood
- authentic bodies and medicine
- real death stakes
- emotionally valid family relationships
- non-evil AI framework
- no simple VR twist
- no metal-robot disguise trick
- no chosen-one superpower
- multiple mystery layers
- society-scale politics
- destination relevance

However, **nothing in this file is FROZEN yet**.

The next required work is:

1. create 3–4 alternative mechanisms for the sole baseline human and compare them
2. red-team the developmental consciousness model scientifically and narratively
3. build ship geography and social regions from actual functions/history
4. build factions from competing answers to continuity/personhood
5. design protagonist-role candidates that can operate inside this world without all-purpose authority
