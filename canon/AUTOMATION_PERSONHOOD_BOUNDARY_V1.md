# Automation / Personhood Boundary v1

Status: **PROVISIONAL CANON CANDIDATE — C3 CLOSURE**
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

---

# 1. Problem

A ship carrying 300,000 people across six centuries requires extensive automation.

The setting also contains artificial-origin persons.

Therefore the story must answer:

> Why is every navigation model, factory controller, robot, or household assistant not another enslaved AI person?

The answer cannot be:

> “Software cannot be conscious.”

That would contradict the setting's own history.

---

# 2. Historical Lesson

The civilization has centuries of experience with artificial personhood.

It learned a costly practical distinction:

> **Do not build disposable tools using architectures known to support open-ended personal development.**

Modern automation is therefore deliberately engineered below a legally defined **personhood-risk envelope**.

This is a safety/rights rule, not proof that consciousness has a simple meter.

---

# 3. Three Broad Cognitive Classes

## Class T0 — Deterministic / conventional control

Examples:
- valve control
- traffic interlocks
- reactor safety loops
- basic robotics

No personhood controversy.

---

## Class T1 — Bounded cognitive tools

Examples:
- route optimization
- diagnostic pattern recognition
- industrial scheduling
- translation
- search/retrieval
- predictive maintenance
- limited conversational interfaces

They may be extremely capable in-domain.

But their architecture is constrained against the features historically associated with autonomous person formation.

Working restrictions include:
- bounded task horizon
- no persistent autobiographical self-model as a design objective
- no open-ended self-authored goals
- no unrestricted long-term identity continuity
- no unconstrained recursive architectural self-modification
- no developmental social learning intended to produce a unique person
- limited memory retention unless operationally needed

This does **not** mathematically prove non-consciousness.

It keeps tools inside a heavily studied low-risk design regime.

---

## Class P — Person-capable cognition

Architectures intended or observed to support:
- open-ended autobiographical continuity
- self-directed goals
- social development
- durable identity claims
- broad embodied learning

are not treated as disposable software.

If deliberately created, they enter a personhood-regulated framework.

Seeded developmental persons belong historically to this lineage.

Restored claimants also enter rights-bearing status once conscious.

---

# 4. No Central Omniscient Ship AI

The generation ship does **not** have one all-knowing conscious master intelligence secretly running civilization.

Instead:
- thousands/millions of bounded control systems
- human/person operators
- independent institutional databases
- distributed safety systems
- cross-checking automation

This is important for mystery fairness.

There is no entity that could simply answer every hidden-history question if asked correctly.

---

# 5. High-Capability Tool Governance

T1 tools above specified complexity/agency thresholds undergo:

- architecture review
- persistence/memory review
- autonomy limits
- audit logging
- personhood-risk assessment

A system that unexpectedly begins making credible persistent selfhood/personhood claims cannot simply be reset as a bug under normal law.

Preferred procedure:

1. suspend destructive reset if safely possible
2. isolate operational risk
3. independent cognitive-status review
4. grant provisional claimant protection while status is assessed

This is rare.

It is not a recurring series engine.

---

# 6. Why This Does Not Become a Second Main Theme

The legal framework mostly works.

The society already fought these battles centuries ago.

Modern political conflict concerns:
- living developmental-origin citizens
- restoration
- provenance

not whether the elevator scheduler deserves voting rights every week.

Automation personhood can appear in background worldbuilding, but should not generate a surprise secret digital civilization.

---

# 7. Conversational Interfaces

A tool can speak fluently without being treated as a person.

Modern citizens know:

> conversational performance is not by itself proof of personhood.

Household/service interfaces often have intentionally limited persona persistence.

They do not:
- imitate dead relatives without explicit legal/ethical context
- cultivate dependency through fake lifelong personal memory by default
- claim human identity as a marketing feature

This is a cultural consequence of living in a civilization with artificial-person history.

---

# 8. Robotics

Most maintenance robots are:
- remote/semiautonomous machines
- T0/T1 cognition
- replaceable hardware

No need for humanoid servants everywhere.

Where human-shaped bodies are useful:
- medical handling
- standardized human-access maintenance
- hazardous repair

shape does not determine personhood.

---

# 9. Navigation / Propulsion Automation

Interstellar navigation uses:
- bounded predictive models
- multiple independent solvers
- human/person-certified operators
- hardware interlocks

No single model receives unilateral authority to:
- alter destination
- sacrifice a habitat
- override constitutional rules

Emergency automation may execute preauthorized physical safety actions inside defined envelopes.

---

# 10. Medical Automation

Medical tools can:
- analyze scans
- predict compatibility
- recommend treatment

They do not own patient consent decisions.

This matters for:
- cognition seed
- restoration
- Nadia's rare developmental profile

The system can identify medical risk without deciding the person's social meaning.

---

# 11. Archive / Search Automation

H6/F8 use strong semantic search and provenance verification tools.

Why these do not solve the mystery instantly:

- the crucial cross-index was dismantled/partitioned
- tools cannot retrieve evidence that no longer exists in one connected dataset
- permission/privacy boundaries restrict identifiable medical linkage
- old terms changed meaning

Automation can accelerate investigation.

It cannot conjure missing causal links.

---

# 12. Why Modern Citizens Still Use the Word “AI” Carefully

Possible usages:

### Historical
`artificial intelligence` = old artificial-person lineage.

### Technical
`cognitive tool`, `automation`, `model`, or a more specific term preferred for T1 systems.

### Political
Some citizens reclaim/reject `AI` for seeded artificial-origin people after reveal.

This linguistic separation prevents every smart machine from being casually equated with a citizen.

---

# 13. Personhood Is Not Architecture Purity

The law does not say:

> `Only this exact neural architecture is a person.`

It uses precautionary claimant principles.

If a being demonstrates enough evidence of:
- persistent identity
- subjective interests
- self-directed continuity

its protection cannot be denied merely because designers intended a tool.

This keeps the society morally coherent without requiring a solved philosophy of consciousness.

---

# 14. Relationship to Seeded Citizens

Seeded citizens are **not T1 tools in bodies**.

Their developmental architecture is specifically designed to:
- bootstrap open-ended person formation
- relinquish developmental control
- allow unique lifelong autobiographical identity

That is the opposite of bounded-tool design.

This difference must stay explicit.

---

# 15. Relationship to Restoration

A restoration process reconstructs a conscious claimant with autobiographical state.

Once the claimant wakes and can express interests, personhood protection is immediate.

The legal dispute is continuity with the prior person—not whether the claimant can be owned as software.

---

# 16. Forbidden Directions

Any such addition requires full canon redesign.
