# 1. Problem

The Continuity Settlement removed developmental-origin caste fields from routine civic identity roughly 350 years before EP001.

Therefore EP005–010 cannot rely on:

> an ancient frozen citizen list that magically still knows Nadia is alive.

The count must be:
- current
- operationally justified
- privacy-preserving
- capable of surviving social reform without becoming a secret caste registry

---

# 2. The Modern System That Still Needs Rare Developmental Information

Modern medicine maintains a **Developmental Compatibility Profile** for each person where clinically relevant.

It contains medical properties such as:
- cognition-scaffold family/version
- whether a scaffold was used
- neural repair compatibility classes
- continuity-capture compatibility
- unusual developmental support requirements

This is protected medical information.

It is not printed on ordinary civic identity records.

For most citizens, the relevant value is a common seeded-development family.

For Nadia, the medically relevant fact is:

> **no cognition scaffold participated in conscious neural development.**

Modern clinicians understand this as a rare scaffold-bypass/unseeded developmental history.

They do not ordinarily call it `human-origin`.

---

# 3. Why Infrastructure Needs Aggregate Counts

The ship must not decommission the last medical capability required by a rare patient cohort merely because the cohort is tiny.

Examples of cohort-dependent infrastructure:
- rare neural-repair compatibility tools
- diagnostic calibration
- continuity-capture fallback methods
- specialized drugs / implant interfaces
- clinical expertise / training

Therefore transition planning uses an anonymous service-dependency layer answering questions like:

> How many currently living people still require capability class X?

It does **not** normally answer:

> Who are they?

This is directly relevant to Haren's profession.

---

# 4. Privacy Architecture

Modern clinical systems retain identifiable medical provenance locally / under medical access control.

A privacy-preserving aggregation layer produces only the minimum infrastructure-planning outputs:

- cohort count
- broad habitat/service demand
- required compatibility capability
- confidence / update age

Individual names, household identities, and full medical histories are excluded.

Future cryptographic/secure-computation methods can enforce much of this separation, but the story need not lecture on algorithms.

Reader-facing concept:

> the transition system knows **that someone needs a service**, not automatically **who**.

---

# 5. The Launch-Era Resilience Adapter

One old mission-critical service survived the Continuity Settlement because it did **not** maintain a public person-by-person origin list.

Working design name:

**Developmental Resilience Monitor (DRM)** — design-only English; final Korean reader term open.

Original purpose:
- track whether the expedition retained more than one viable cognitive-development pathway
- maintain rare medical compatibility capability
- preserve the unseeded developmental option even if no current citizen used it
- prevent planners from deleting the final supporting infrastructure by accident

The Monitor receives **aggregate compatibility states**, not identities.

---

# 6. The Critical Old Crosswalk

Modern medical input might say, conceptually:

`scaffold participation: NONE`

The old adapter maps this to its launch-era ontology:

`cognitive lineage: HUMAN_ORIGIN`

Seeded developmental profiles map to:

`cognitive lineage: ARTIFICIAL_ORIGIN`

This mapping is the buried crosswalk.

It is technically real and historically obsolete.

---

# 7. Why the Continuity Settlement Did Not Delete It

The Settlement had two conflicting goals:

### Eliminate caste power
Remove origin from ordinary civil identity, benefits, employment, custody, and public genealogy.

### Preserve safety / medical diversity
Do not destroy the ability to:
- treat rare developmental architectures
- preserve alternative reproductive capability
- detect whether a compatibility class has disappeared

The compromise:

- identifiable origin registry: dismantled / sealed
- anonymous medical compatibility: retained
- old resilience monitor: retained but stripped of direct identity access

At the time this was considered a privacy-preserving safety exception.

---

# 8. Why Exact `1` Is Allowed Instead of Privacy Suppression

Modern statistics normally suppress tiny cohort counts because `n=1` can enable re-identification.

The resilience system has a narrow safety exception:

> **zero versus nonzero exact count matters when deciding whether the final supported pathway may be retired.**

Therefore authorized infrastructure-transition software may receive exact small counts for capability-preservation purposes.

It still does not receive identity.

This is a deliberate privacy tradeoff, not an ancient bug.

---

# 9. Why Haren Sees It

ARC 02 concerns a legacy service handoff/decommissioning during a real H8/ship transition project.

Haren's transition risk graph encounters an apparently impossible dependency:

> A deprecated developmental/continuity support path cannot be retired because **1 current person** still maps to a protected mission compatibility class.

The displayed modern label is obscure/technical.

Haren does not immediately see the sentence:

> “There is one human.”

He must follow the old semantic mapping.

---

# 10. Three Independent Evidence Families for EP005–010

The title reveal cannot come from one magic database.

## Evidence A — Live aggregate dependency
Current system says exactly one living person occupies the rare compatibility class.

This proves:
- current, not historical
- count = 1

It does not prove old meaning.

## Evidence B — Physical/technical legacy manual
An old service / medical engineering document maps the compatibility class to a launch-era developmental ontology.

This proves:
- the class was once linked to `human-origin` / unseeded lineage

It does not prove modern person identity.

## Evidence C — Independent historical mission schema
A separate old charter / resilience schema distinguishes:
- human-origin cognitive lineage
- artificial-origin developmental lineage

This proves the word is not a mistranslated one-off label.

Together:

> The current aggregate count for the old mission's human-origin developmental lineage is one.

This is fair enough for the title payoff.

---

# 11. Why Nadia Is Not Immediately Identified

The Monitor cannot return a name.

To identify the person, one would need to combine multiple otherwise legitimate datasets, such as:
- rare clinic capability demand
- movement/transition service patterns
- age/medical incompatibility clues
- habitat-specific care pathways

This is exactly what later privacy/deanonymization arcs explore.

The mystery therefore moves from:

`Does one exist?`

to

`Who has the right to infer who it is?`

rather than Haren simply clicking “show patient.”

---

# 12. Why Clinicians Do Not Already Know “The Last Human”

Nadia's clinicians may know:
- she is unseeded / scaffold-bypass
- her compatibility profile is rare

They do not need to know:
- current shipwide count
- old DRM crosswalk
- old mission legal category

Medical specialists are not given mission-ontology dashboards.

After the historical crosswalk becomes public, some clinicians may realize the implication quickly.

Their confidentiality duty prevents them from simply announcing her identity.

---

# 13. What Happens If Count Reaches Zero

This is crucial for the anti-chosen-one rule.

The system does **not** trigger forced reproduction.

If living unseeded count reaches zero, it triggers only capability-preservation actions:

- do not discard unseeded diagnostic knowledge
- maintain founder/legacy germline resources under existing consent law
- retain unseeded pregnancy/developmental support capability
- flag the loss of a currently living biomedical reference cohort

No child must be created.

---

# 14. What Happens If Count Becomes Two

Nothing mystical.

The aggregate becomes two.

The title condition would no longer be true at that later date.

This is important:

> the universe is not maintaining a literary premise.

`1` is a contingent present fact.

---

# 15. Why the System Survived 350 Years Technically

It is not one untouched launch computer.

Its **function and protocol** survive through repeated hardware migrations because it remains part of certified mission-safety logic.

Over centuries:
- computers replaced
- code ported
- interface wrapped
- labels deprecated

The old semantic crosswalk became a buried dependency because almost nobody needed to inspect its original ontology.

This matches the ship's broader rule:

> systems survive by rebuilding, not by immortal hardware.

---

# 16. How This Creates Later Privacy Politics

The old safety exception is defensible and dangerous.

It creates the exact social question:

> If an aggregate of one can be combined with other lawful data to identify a person, is it still anonymous?

Therefore later privacy reform may:
- keep capability-preservation logic
- alter how exact small counts are exposed
- introduce trusted local intermediaries
- separate zero/nonzero operational checks from general numeric display

The story can improve the system rather than discovering a cartoonishly evil registry.

---

# 17. Final Terminology Fairness

Author-facing exact statement:

> At EP001, exactly one living citizen has an unseeded developmental compatibility profile. A surviving launch-era resilience crosswalk maps that medical state to the mission's old `human-origin` cognitive lineage. The other citizens map to the artificial-origin developmental lineage.

> `이 우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다.`

The novel then spends the rest of the series interrogating whether that old category should control present identity or rights.
