# Information Economy Ledger v1

Status: PROVISIONAL — PHASE MAPPING BEFORE MACRO
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

## 1. Principle

Mystery fairness depends on separating:

- what objectively exists
- what is publicly known
- what specialists know
- what the protagonist knows
- what factions believe
- what the reader can infer

The world must not rely on everyone becoming stupid around one secret.

---

# 2. Knowledge Classes

## PUBLIC
Ordinary educated citizens can know this without special access.

## PROFESSIONAL
Known within relevant professions but not culturally important to most people.

## RESTRICTED
Legally protected information such as medical/provenance/continuity data.

## ARCHAIC
Recorded somewhere but terminology/meaning has drifted.

## FRAGMENTED
Different institutions hold pieces that have not been combined.

## LOST-MAPPING
Facts are known individually, but the old relationship between them has been forgotten.

## DESIGNER-ONLY
True for series design but not necessarily discoverable within main narrative.

# 3. Core Facts

## INF-001 — Standard cognition-seed prenatal intervention exists

Truth: TRUE.

Modern status: PUBLIC / PROFESSIONAL.

Ordinary interpretation:

> Ancient human medicine made healthy neurodevelopment reliable in the closed voyage environment.

Mystery value:

Low alone; very high after historical recontextualization.

---

## INF-002 — Cognition seed is a designed generative cognitive architecture

Truth: TRUE under current v2 model.

Modern status: PROFESSIONAL, but interpreted as biomedical architecture.

Lost mapping:

Transition-era researchers once classified its mature design lineage as artificial general cognition / synthetic mind architecture.

Current citizens do not consider the existence of the seed proof that they are nonhuman.

---

## INF-003 — Transition-era threshold from assistance to generative cognition

Truth: TRUE.

Modern status: ARCHAIC + FRAGMENTED + LOST-MAPPING.

Pieces held by:

- old technical archives
- historical legal cases
- obsolete medical standards
- provenance scholarship

No single modern public source clearly explains the threshold in current terminology.

# 4. Information Access Rules

## Medical privacy

The protagonist cannot lawfully search the identity of INF-006 without extraordinary due process.

## Archive fragmentation

Old technical, legal, medical, and family records use different identifiers and terminology.

## No omniscient master database

There is no single query:

`SELECT * FROM truth`.

Ship systems evolved across centuries and jurisdictions.

## No arbitrary locked files

When access is denied, denial must follow a real rule:

- privacy
- jurisdiction
- damaged archive
- obsolete format
- missing provenance chain

and the denial must create a consequence or alternative route, not merely delay plot.

# 6. Mystery Construction Rule

Major truth emerges only when **at least two knowledge domains intersect**.

Examples:

- medical anomaly + old legal definition
- infrastructure class + archival terminology
- family genealogy + transition policy
- settlement charter + biological development history

This gives the protagonist’s cross-system transition work a legitimate role in discovery without making them the smartest scientist in every field.

---

# 7. Information-State Tracking Later

When Acts are built, every major INF item receives phase states:

- `P0`: unknown/unimportant
- `P1`: anomaly noticed
- `P2`: competing interpretations
- `P3`: privately established
- `P4`: limited institutional knowledge
- `P5`: public rumor
- `P6`: publicly verified
- `P7`: institutionalized consequence

A fact is not narratively complete at `P6`.

It must reach `P7` if it materially changes society.
