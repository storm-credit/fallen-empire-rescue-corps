# Executive Verdict

**250-episode durability: PASS WITH REQUIRED REVISIONS**

The current macro has enough post-reveal engines to avoid one-twist collapse.

However, three risks are serious enough to block macro freeze:

1. **Title-premise payoff may be too slow at EP18–20.**
2. **ACT III can become historical-exposition sludge.**
3. **ACT V–VII can become policy/committee SF unless external action and wonder remain strong.**

Additional risks:
- protagonist profession may read as bureaucratically dry
- BH-1 may become symbolic object rather than recurring person
- artificial-origin distinction may become semantic rather than dramatic
- too many law/policy payoffs may feel samey
- arrival could feel like a second novel stapled onto the first unless seeded continuously

---

# RT-01 — TITLE PROMISE DELAY

## Attack

The reader selected a novel titled:

> 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

If EP001–017 mostly looks like an excellent relocation procedural with vague anomalies, some readers will ask why the advertised premise has not arrived.

A title is not a twist to protect. It is a contract.

## Severity
**HIGH**

## Required revision

Move verified `baseline count = 1` earlier.

Recommended:
- EP001–003: strong anomalous field/protocol appears as part of real work
- EP004–006: first independent evidence that the field is biological/developmental, not random legacy junk
- EP007–010: **count = 1 confirmed**
- EP011–020: immediate practical consequence + first attempt to understand what the other citizens are

The series can still delay:
- who the one is
- exact artificial-origin mechanism
- history
- protagonist provenance

This creates more room for consequence and better honors the title.

## Verdict
**REVISE MACRO V1 before promotion.**

# RT-02 — PROTAGONIST PROFESSION SOUNDS DRY

## Attack

“Habitat Transition Officer” is structurally excellent but commercially weak if scenes become:
- meetings
- forms
- interviews
- planning decks
- hearings

The competence engine needs visible action.

## Required design rule

Transition work must regularly include field operations:
- walking/entering threatened neighborhoods
- live evacuation sequencing
- transit rerouting
- on-site dependency mapping
- emergency resettlement
- physical inspection with engineers
- negotiating while systems are failing
- choosing what infrastructure stays online
- executing moves under deadline

The protagonist is not merely a planner.

Working functional identity:

> **A field transition specialist with limited binding authority who keeps communities functioning while physical parts of the ship are changed.**

Possible later naming should sound concrete and active.

## Severity
**HIGH for web-novel retention, low for story logic.**

---

# RT-03 — ACT III HISTORY LECTURE RISK

## Attack

EP056–095 contains:
- mixed-family history
- registry politics
- Continuity Settlement
- archival release
- public explanation

Forty episodes of this can become characters reading documents about more interesting dead people.

## Required countermeasure

Every historical answer must be attached to a present-tense consequence.

Minimum present-event structure:

### III-A
History emerges because old family infrastructure blocks a live transition.

### III-B
Archive release causes a current person/family to lose safety, status, work, or privacy.

### III-C
Public artificial-origin confirmation changes a live migration/resource/service problem.

Add at least one E5 physical-system pressure within ACT III whose solution depends on understanding an old system layer.

History must **solve or complicate current action**, not pause it.

## Severity
**HIGH**

---

# RT-04 — POST-MYSTERY ENERGY DROP

## Attack

After public truth (~EP90) and BH-1 identity (~EP115), the classic mystery engine is largely spent.

Will readers stay for 130+ episodes of politics?

## Defense

Yes, only if the genre promise visibly mutates from:

`ontological mystery`

to:

`civilization transition under irreversible physical constraints`.

The protagonist's job is the bridge.

## Required escalation

From ACT IV onward, increase:
- visible ship reconfiguration
- movement across habitats
- personal legal stakes
- resource deadlines
- arrival observations
- physical conversion projects
- irreversible choices

Decrease:
- closed-room explanation scenes
- archive-search scenes
- repeated identity debates

## Severity
**CRITICAL if ignored / manageable if enacted.**

# RT-05 — “THEY ARE AI” MAY FEEL SEMANTIC

## Attack

If artificial-origin citizens:
- have organic bodies
- are born
- grow
- age
- love
- die

then a reader may say:

> “So they're basically genetically modified humans. Why call them AI?”

If the distinction cannot generate concrete consequences, the premise feels like wordplay.

## Required canon burden

The cognition-seeded pathway must differ in at least several **observable but non-superpower** ways:

- developmental architecture / neural bootstrapping
- compatibility with state-capture/restoration technology
- specific medical diagnostics
- reproducible engineering lineage of the seed
- possibly different failure modes or therapeutic interventions
- historical evidence showing deliberate artificial design, not merely gene editing

But avoid:
- hive mind
- remote control
- magical software upload
- obvious robot hardware

The distinction must be scientifically/story-relevant without making seeded people less alive.

## Severity
**CRITICAL CANON ISSUE — still OPEN.**

# RT-06 — WHY DOES BASELINE COUNT MATTER IF RIGHTS DON'T?

## Attack

If final thesis says biology does not rank dignity, why should readers care who BH-1 is?

## Required answer

Because factual difference can matter without moral supremacy.

BH-1 matters for:
- medical reference
- reproductive/developmental pathway
- historical truth
- charter interpretation
- privacy
- scientific uncertainty
- destination adaptation hypotheses

The series tension is precisely whether society can handle **important difference without turning it into rank**.

## Verdict
**STRONG — preserve this formulation.**

# RT-07 — BH-1 AS PLOT OBJECT

## Attack

The ordinary sole human can easily become:
- kidnapped asset
- protected witness
- political football
- womb/sperm symbol

and stop being a character.

## Required quota

Before final blueprint:
BH-1 must have at least:
- 3 independent goals unrelated to being baseline
- 3 decisions that frustrate groups trying to protect/use them
- 2 relationships not routed through protagonist
- 1 professional competence payoff
- 1 mistake with real cost
- 1 future preference that is not “what is best for humanity”

No long stretch where BH-1 is physically hidden for protection while others debate them.

## Severity
**HIGH**

---

# RT-08 — PROTAGONIST BECOMES UNELECTED SUPREME PLANNER

## Attack

Professional scaling from neighborhood to civilization can become technocratic chosen-one narrative by another name.

## Required safeguards

Protagonist may:
- synthesize
- negotiate
- propose
- execute assigned transition authority
- earn professional trust

Protagonist may NOT simply:
- rewrite constitution alone
- decide habitat futures unilaterally
- command all resources
- become president because smartest

The finale must require:
- representative institutions
- community choices
- faction bargains
- technical teams
- operational execution by many people

## Severity
**HIGH**

---

# RT-09 — TOO MANY COURT/LAW STORIES

## Attack

Personhood, restoration, privacy, charter, family, provenance, settlement all naturally create legal questions.

If dramatized as hearings, the series becomes repetitive.

## Required resolution rotation

Different dilemmas must resolve through different action containers:

- field transition
- medical decision
- family negotiation
- labor action
- resource budget
- emergency operations
- archive publication
- professional certification
- habitat referendum
- arbitration/court (only some)
- physical refusal/noncompliance

No more than one courtroom/hearing-dominant Arc in any 3 major Arcs.

## Severity
**HIGH**

---

# RT-10 — ARRIVAL FEELS STAPLED ON

## Attack

If destination matters only around EP141, it can feel like the novel changed premises halfway.

## Required seeds from ACT I

Arrival must appear as normal background life from the beginning:
- school curriculum
- jobs/training
- civic calendar
- reserve politics
- forward observations
- childhood assumptions about “when we arrive”
- arguments about whether current sacrifices are for descendants

No mysterious planet twist required.

By ACT II–III, at least one early decision must spend or protect a resource explicitly because of future settlement.

## Severity
**MEDIUM-HIGH**

---

# RT-11 — WONDER DEFICIT

## Attack

The concept can become relentlessly administrative and philosophical.

A giant generation ship should generate awe.

## Required wonder cadence

Across every ~15–20 episodes include at least one memorable SF experience such as:
- gravity transition through the axis
- habitat horizon/rotation visual
- engineered weather/season
- biosphere-scale maintenance
- exterior maintenance view
- ship heat-radiator spectacle
- massive fabrication/recycling operation
- forward observation of destination
- old launch-era structure embedded under a living city
- habitat conversion on civilization scale

Wonder scenes must affect plot; no tourism-only chapters.

## Severity
**HIGH for IP/visual identity.**

---

# RT-12 — PHILOSOPHY MONOTONY

## Attack

Every story can accidentally reduce to:

> “Is this AI a real person?”

## Required differentiation

Sub-Acts must ask different actionable questions:

- does care outweigh household record?
- can restoration be refused?
- can truth be published against privacy?
- can rare biology justify unequal medical allocation?
- can founders bind descendants?
- can local autonomy transfer fatal cost to others?
- what must a community preserve when its place ends?

Characters need not use “humanity” vocabulary in most of these conflicts.

## Severity
**HIGH**

---

# RT-13 — 250 VS 300

## Attack

Can the same engine sustain 300?

## Verdict

**250: YES, provisionally.**

**300: NOT YET JUSTIFIED.**

The current ending naturally closes around a completed arrival/settlement constitutional transition.

Adding 50 episodes before this risks padding.
Adding 50 after it requires a genuinely new phase.

Recommendation:
Freeze architecture at 250 first.
