# 1. Design Goals

The reproductive system must support a civilization where:

- children are genuinely conceived, gestated, born, and raised
- seeded-development children are not installed personalities
- family legitimacy is not reduced to genetics
- same-sex / infertile / donor-assisted families are ordinary possibilities
- population stays near ~300,000 without eugenic merit licensing
- pregnancy retains bodily autonomy
- origin pathway is medically meaningful but not a civic caste
- the current sole unseeded person can exist without a secret quota

---

# 2. Population Envelope

Working population:

**~300,000**, maintained inside a safe operational band rather than an exact fixed number.

Preferred ordinary target band:

**~285,000–315,000**

The ship can temporarily operate outside it, but infrastructure planning tries to return toward the band over decades.

---

# 3. Birth / Death Order of Magnitude

Advanced medicine produces a working ordinary life expectancy around:

**~90–100 years**

with substantial individual variance.

For a roughly stable 300,000 population, long-run births/deaths are therefore on the order of:

**~2,800–3,400 births per year**

Working design center:

**~3,100 births/year**.

This is only ~8–9 births/day across the whole ship.

Reproductive medicine is specialized but not an industrial baby factory.

---

# 4. Population Planning — No Merit-Based Birth License

The ship cannot ignore carrying capacity, but it also does not allocate children according to profession, wealth, IQ, faction, or genetic desirability.

Preferred system:

## Reproductive Capacity Windows

Families/individuals register an intended conception period.

A rolling population model publishes:
- expected births
- expected deaths
- habitat child-service capacity
- school/medical capacity
- ecological/resource margin

### When projected births are inside band
No scheduling restriction beyond ordinary medicine.

### When demand exceeds safe band
The system primarily uses:
- voluntary delay incentives
- flexible conception windows
- queue/lottery allocation among equivalent cases
- priority for age/medical fertility constraints
- hardship/appeal review

### What it does NOT use
- profession score
- genetic quality score
- political loyalty
- developmental origin
- income bidding

### Bodily autonomy rule
Once pregnancy exists, population planning does **not** compel abortion.

The system manages conception access/incentives, not forced pregnancy termination.

# 6. Ordinary Conception

When two compatible gametes are available, conception can be:

- unassisted
- assisted fertilization
- embryo culture followed by implantation

Genetic screening is common for serious known disease risks, radiation-related damage, and fertility concerns.

Most pregnancies are carried in a biological uterus.

Pregnancy is visible and socially ordinary.

# 7. Donor Gametes

Large cryogenic / living-cell germline banks exist for:

- fertility treatment
- genetic diversity
- historic founder diversity
- rare disease avoidance
- families lacking compatible gametes

Gamete contribution does **not** automatically create legal parenthood.

Donor identity/access rules vary by consent regime and habitat law within a common child-rights floor.

No secret anonymous “superior donor” program.

# 8. Same-Sex / Diverse-Parent Reproduction

The civilization separates three concepts:

1. genetic contribution
2. gestation
3. legal/care parenthood

They need not be performed by the same people.

## Current preferred future-tech ladder

### Standard and highly reliable
- donor gametes
- assisted conception
- gestational carrier by consent
- stored gametes

### Advanced but established
- generation of gamete-line cells from a person's somatic stem cells in some sex/chromosome configurations
- extensive genomic/epigenetic QA before use

This is a major extrapolation from current in-vitro germ-cell research, but centuries-ahead reproductive medicine makes it plausible as a regulated option.

### Not assumed universal
A couple does not automatically receive a genetically bi-parental child regardless of biology.

Some families use:
- one genetic parent + donor
- two genetic parents through advanced gametogenesis where validated
- no genetic parent from the legal-parent group

The story does not rank these families.

# 9. Gestation

## Default
Biological gestation in a consenting gestational parent.

## Medical support
Advanced:
- placental monitoring
- fetal intervention
- metabolic control
- scaffold-development monitoring

## Ectogestational rescue
Artificial/external gestation exists primarily as **medical rescue / partial-gestation support**, not the default way citizens reproduce.

Use cases:
- severe pregnancy complication
- extreme prematurity
- gestational contraindication

This preserves pregnancy as a real social/embodied experience while avoiding needless death when medicine can intervene.

Do not make full exowomb gestation the invisible default unless later redesign requires it.

# 10. Legal Parenthood

Legal parenthood is based on an **intent-and-responsibility covenant**, not genetics alone.

Before planned conception/implantation, intended parents normally register:
- parental responsibility
- custody expectations
- financial/care obligations
- emergency guardianship

Genetic contributor and gestational parent are separately recorded medically, with privacy controls.

A genetic donor is not automatically a parent.

A gestational carrier's bodily rights during pregnancy cannot be contracted away.

# 14. Can Parents Refuse the Cognition Seed?

Legally:

**Yes in principle, but refusal does not guarantee a viable unseeded child.**

Modern genomes and reproductive medicine have coexisted with seeded development for many generations.

Most embryos are not clinically certified for unseeded development because standard medicine cannot verify robust endogenous neural bootstrapping without the scaffold.

Refusal therefore usually leads to choices such as:
- use another embryo/gamete combination
- postpone conception
- pursue rare unseeded-viability assessment
- accept elevated medical risk only within a narrow ethical/clinical threshold

The state does not force a seed into an already gestating person simply to preserve population targets.

# 15. Why Unseeded Development Became So Rare

Do **not** claim all modern citizens lost a single “human gene.”

Preferred mechanism is multicausal:

Over centuries of safe seeded medicine:

1. selection pressure on fully endogenous cognitive-development robustness relaxed
2. therapeutic germline corrections prioritized seed-compatible developmental stability
3. seed architecture became integrated with prenatal support protocols
4. many harmless genetic/regulatory variants accumulated that are safe **with** scaffold support but make scaffold-free development less predictable

Result:

Most modern embryos are biologically human-derived but not medically considered robust candidates for fully unseeded cognitive development.

This is analogous to technological dependency emerging gradually, not a species switch overnight.

# 19. Rarity Model — Why Exactly One Living Person Is Plausible

Working stable births:

**~3,100/year**.

Preferred natural contingency incidence:

**~1 qualifying unseeded contingency birth per 250,000–350,000 births**.

Working center:

**~1 / 300,000 births**.

Expected new unseeded contingency births:

~0.010/year

= roughly one per ~97 years on average.

With ~90–100-year life expectancy, the expected number alive at any time is roughly:

**~0.9–1.0**.

Under a simple rare-event/Poisson intuition, having exactly one living person is therefore not extraordinary.

It is a normal possible outcome of a rare process.

- radiation-damage correction
- fertility

# 26. Inheritance and Parenthood

A child's legal parent obligations survive ordinary relocation/status changes.

If a parent dies and a restored claimant appears:
- the claimant is immediately a legal person
- prior parental rights/duties may resume partly or fully according to continuity law, child welfare, and prior directives
- the child is not treated as property automatically transferred to a restored claimant

This can create real personhood cases without making restoration universal.
