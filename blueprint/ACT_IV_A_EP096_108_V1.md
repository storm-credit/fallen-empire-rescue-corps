# EP106 — Who Holds the Bridge Key

**Immediate Goal**
Design an emergency/purpose-limited linkage mechanism that cannot become a disguised central provenance database.

**Primary Engine**
E7 Governance.

**Secondary**
E2/E5.

**External Event**
Competing designs would place emergency linkage power with:
- central planners,
- local officials,
- medical staff,
- automated rules.

Each creates a different abuse/failure risk.

**Decision**
The trial adopts a distributed trustee model:
- narrowly defined purpose,
- multi-party authorization except true emergencies,
- access expiration,
- audit trail,
- user notice where safe.

**Result**
The bridge becomes operational without recreating universal central raw-data access.

**Cost**
It is slower and administratively expensive.

**Character / Faction**
Kian accepts some cross-habitat common rules; Selma accepts more local control than preferred.

# EP108 — Protection Is a Service Too

**Immediate Goal**
Decide which H7-derived privacy mechanisms should become broader ship standards before candidate inference gets more accurate.

**Primary Engine**
E7/E2.

**Secondary**
E5.

**External State**
The trial proved:
- strong privacy lowers deanonymization risk,
- fragmented systems increase cost/latency,
- purpose-limited bridges can work,
- no design eliminates inference completely.

**Decision**
Shipwide policy adopts selected mechanisms:
- purpose-limited verification,
- shorter retention for unnecessary movement telemetry,
- local trustees for sensitive cross-links,
- emergency bridges with expiry/audit.

It does **not** force every habitat to become H7.

**Result**
Candidate-hunting becomes harder.

**Cost**
Legitimate transition/medical planning now has more friction and resource cost.

**Mystery / Handoff**
The protection arrives just as Haren is assigned back to an ordinary H5/H8-boundary cooperative transition whose manager has been difficult since Act I.

That manager is Nadia Velo.

No identity clue is stated in the hook; the reader recognizes the ordinary person returning.

TH-008.
