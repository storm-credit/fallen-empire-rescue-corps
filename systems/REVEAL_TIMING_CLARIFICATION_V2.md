# 2. Frozen Timing

### Question

### Answer
**No.**

By EP050, three evidence families establish Haren underwent standard cognition-seeded development:

1. prenatal developmental record
2. current/molecular/compatibility evidence
3. independent family memory

Therefore Haren is not the current baseline-route citizen.

### Narrative consequence

- no hybrid-route surprise invented later.

What Haren still does **not** know is the full historical meaning of the seeded lineage.

## Phase B — Act III

### Question
What did standard seeded development historically descend from, and why did old systems classify it as a different cognitive origin?

### Answer
The historical crosswalk establishes that modern cognition-seed architecture descends from artificial-person / synthetic-mind developmental technology that entered human family medicine over generations.

### Narrative consequence
Haren's already-known standard seeded development is **recontextualized** as artificial-origin in the old technical sense.

This is not a new test of Haren's body.
It is a new historical meaning attached to an already-established fact.
